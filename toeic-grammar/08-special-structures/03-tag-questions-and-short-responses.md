# 03 — Tag Questions, Negative Questions & Indirect Responses (Listening Part 2)

> **TOEIC parts:** Listening Part 2 · **Priority:** ★★★★ (Part 2 has 25 questions)

## 1. Tag & negative questions: answer the facts

| Question | If it's true | If it's false |
|---|---|---|
| *You've sent the invoice, **haven't you**?* | **Yes**, this morning. | **No**, not yet. |
| ***Didn't** you finish the report?* | **Yes**, I did. | **No**, I still need a day. |

Answer the **fact**, not the grammar: "Yes" = it happened.

## 2. Indirect answers are often correct

Part 2 increasingly uses answers that don't say *yes / no / a time / a place* directly:

- *Q: When is the budget meeting?* → *A: **Ms. Diaz is still checking the room schedule.***
- *Q: Who's going to lead the training?* → *A: **It hasn't been decided yet.***
- *Q: Should we order lunch now?* → *A: **Most people are out of the office today.***
- *Q: Where's the stapler?* → *A: **I think Tom borrowed it.***

## 3. Traps to eliminate

- **Same / similar sound**: *Q: Did you **copy** the file? A: I'd like a **coffee**.* ✗
- **Related word, wrong question**: *Q: **Where** is the conference? A: **Next Tuesday.*** ✗ (that answers *when*)
- **Yes/No to a wh-question**: *Q: **Why** was the flight delayed? A: Yes, it was.* ✗

## 4. Suggestion / offer / request patterns

| Question | Typical correct reply |
|---|---|
| *Why don't we take a taxi?* (suggestion) | *Good idea. / Sure, I'll call one.* |
| *Would you like me to help?* (offer) | *That would be great, thanks.* |
| *Could you review this draft?* (request) | *Sure, leave it on my desk.* / *Sorry, I'm busy until 3.* |
| *Would you mind closing the window?* | ***Not at all.*** (= OK) |
| *Do you want the blue one or the red one?* (choice) | *Either is fine. / The blue one, please.* / *Neither, actually.* |

## 5. TOEIC-style item (spoken, Part 2)

> **Q:** The client meeting has been moved to Thursday, hasn't it?
> (A) Yes, at the same time.  (B) She moved to Chicago.  (C) In the meeting room.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(A).** It confirms the change and adds information. (B) is a repeated-word trap (*moved*). (C) answers *where*.
</details>

## 6. Review Quiz: 20 Part 2-Style Items

> Choose the **best response**. Read the question aloud, or better, ask someone to read it to you. **Target: 16/20.**

1. When does the store open tomorrow?
   (A) At 9 A.M., I think. (B) At the shopping mall. (C) Yes, it's open.
2. Where should I put these boxes?
   (A) Next week. (B) In the storage room. (C) They're heavy boxes.
3. Who's in charge of the budget report?
   (A) The charge is $20. (B) Mr. Ito is handling it. (C) It's due Friday.
4. Why was the meeting cancelled?
   (A) Yes, it was. (B) In Room 5. (C) The manager is sick.
5. You've finished the report, haven't you?
   (A) Almost; I need one more hour. (B) It's on page six. (C) The reporter called.
6. Would you like some coffee?
   (A) I'd like a copy. (B) No, thanks. I just had some. (C) The coffee shop is closed.
7. How long will the repairs take?
   (A) Ten meters long. (B) About two days. (C) Repair the printer.
8. Should we take a taxi or the subway?
   (A) The subway is faster at this hour. (B) Yes, we should. (C) Take it to the station.
9. Why don't we order lunch now?
   (A) Because I ordered it. (B) At noon yesterday. (C) Good idea. I'm hungry.
10. Isn't Ms. Garcia coming to the party?
   (A) She has to work late. (B) Yes, it's a party. (C) The party was fun.
11. Could you send me the file?
   (A) Sure, I'll email it now. (B) I sent it to the wrong file. (C) A sales file.
12. Which hotel did you book?
   (A) Yes, I booked it. (B) A book about hotels. (C) The one near the station.
13. When will the new manager start?
   (A) It hasn't been announced yet. (B) The manager started the car. (C) In the main office.
14. Would you mind closing the window?
   (A) Yes, it's a window. (B) I don't mind the view. (C) Not at all.
15. How often do you visit the factory?
   (A) For three hours. (B) Once a month. (C) By car.
16. Didn't you get my message?
   (A) Yes, it's a message. (B) Sorry, I've been in meetings all day. (C) The messenger left.
17. Who's going to drive the clients to the airport?
   (A) I can do that. (B) At 6 P.M. (C) They're arriving.
18. Where's the nearest bank?
   (A) I need to bank on it. (B) There's one across the street. (C) Bank holidays are nice.
19. Do you want the report in print or by email?
   (A) Email is fine. (B) It's printing. (C) Yes, I want it.
20. The client approved the design, right?
   (A) Yes, she liked it a lot. (B) Right turn ahead. (C) The design team.
