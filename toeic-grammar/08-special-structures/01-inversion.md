# 01 — Inversion (Should you…, Only after…, Not only…)

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★ (helps you move from 650 to 700+)

## 1. When the sentence starts with a negative / limiting word → auxiliary + subject

| Opening | Inverted example |
|---|---|
| **Not only** | ***Not only did** the company meet its target, **but** it also exceeded it.* |
| **Never / Rarely / Seldom** | ***Rarely has** the team received such praise.* |
| **Only after / Only when / Only if** | ***Only after** the contract is signed **will** work begin.* |
| **No sooner … than** | ***No sooner had** we arrived **than** the meeting began.* |
| **Hardly / Scarcely … when** | ***Hardly had** she sat down **when** the phone rang.* |
| **Under no circumstances** | ***Under no circumstances should** passwords be shared.* |

## 2. Conditional inversion (most common on TOEIC)

***Should** you require assistance, …* (= If you require) · ***Had** we known, …* · ***Were** it not for …*

## 3. Inversion after "so / neither / nor" & "as"

*The sales team met its goal, and **so did** marketing.* · *I can't attend, and **neither can** Mr. Lim.*

## 4. Attached / enclosed is / are … (inverted for emphasis, common in letters)

***Enclosed is** a copy of the invoice.* · ***Attached are** the meeting minutes.* (the verb agrees with what follows)

## 5. TOEIC-style question

> ------- you require further assistance, please contact the front desk at extension 200.
> (A) Should  (B) Unless  (C) Had  (D) Whether

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(A) Should.** This is a conditional inversion meaning "If you require". *Unless* gives the wrong meaning, and *Had* would need a past participle (*Had you required*).
</details>

## 6. Practice

1. Only after the inspection is complete ------- the building open to the public.
   (A) it will (B) will it (C) it is (D) does it
2. Enclosed ------- the documents you requested.
   (A) is (B) are (C) be (D) being

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: *Only after* triggers inversion.
2. **(B)**: the subject is *the documents* (plural).
</details>
