## 2. Verb + preposition

apply **for** (job) / apply **to** (place) · comply **with** · respond **to** · participate **in** · specialize **in** · consist **of** · depend / rely **on** · account **for** · deal **with** · refer **to** · contribute **to** · subscribe **to** · register / sign up **for** · inquire **about** · compete **with** · cooperate **with** · invest **in** · result **in** (cause) · result **from** (be caused by)

## 3. Adjective + preposition

responsible **for** · eligible **for** · familiar **with** · compatible **with** · satisfied **with** · aware **of** · capable **of** · similar **to** · subject **to** (change) · equivalent **to** · accessible **to** · proud **of** · interested **in** · comparable **to** · consistent **with** · exempt **from** · free **from**

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> Prices are subject ------- change without prior notice.
> (A) for  (B) with  (C) to  (D) of

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) to.** *be subject to change* is a fixed phrase that appears on almost every real TOEIC test (in notices, menus and price lists).
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. All employees must comply ------- the new safety regulations.
   (A) to (B) with (C) for (D) on
2. ------- behalf of the board, I would like to welcome our new CEO.
   (A) In (B) On (C) At (D) By
3. Full-time staff are eligible ------- health insurance after three months.
   (A) to (B) of (C) for (D) with

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: *comply with*.
2. **(B)**: *on behalf of*.
3. **(C)**: *eligible for* + noun (*eligible to* + verb).
</details>
