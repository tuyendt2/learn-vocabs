# 01 — Prepositions of Time

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★★

## 1. in / on / at

| Preposition | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | exact time, holidays as a period | *at 9 A.M., at noon, at the end of the month, at the weekend (UK)* |
| **on** | days & dates | *on Monday, on May 5, on the first day, on weekdays* |
| **in** | months, years, seasons, parts of the day, **after a period** | *in June, in 2025, in the morning, **in two weeks** (= after two weeks)* |

## 2. ⭐ The high-frequency TOEIC pairs

| Pair | Difference | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **by** vs **until** | **by** = no later than (one action); **until** = continuing up to (ongoing state) | *Submit it **by** Friday.* / *The office is closed **until** Monday.* |
| **for** vs **during** | **for** + length of time; **during** + an event/period name | ***for** two hours* / ***during** the meeting* |
| **for** vs **since** | period vs starting point (perfect tenses) | ***for** 5 years* / ***since** 2020* |
| **within** | inside a time limit | *Refunds are issued **within** 30 days.* |
| **throughout** | during the whole period | ***throughout** the year* |
| **prior to / before** | before | ***prior to** departure* |
| **following / after** | after | ***following** the presentation* |
| **as of / effective** | starting from | ***As of** July 1, the fee will rise.* |
| **from … to / through** | range | *Monday **through** Friday* |
| **over** | across a period | ***over** the past decade* |

## 3. TOEIC-style question

> All registration forms must be returned ------- Friday, March 15.
> (A) until  (B) by  (C) since  (D) during

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) by.** *Returned* is a **one-time action with a deadline**, so **by**. *Until* is used for a continuing state (*wait until*, *open until*).
</details>

## 4. Practice

1. The library will remain closed ------- the renovation is complete.
   (A) by (B) until (C) within (D) for
2. Several ideas were discussed ------- the meeting.
   (A) for (B) while (C) during (D) since
3. Your order will be delivered ------- three business days.
   (A) within (B) since (C) on (D) at

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: a continuing state up to a point; *until* can also be followed by a clause.
2. **(C)**: an event noun.
3. **(A)**: a time limit.
</details>
