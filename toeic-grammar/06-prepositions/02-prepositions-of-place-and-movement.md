# 02 — Prepositions of Place & Movement

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, **Listening Part 1 (photos)** · **Priority:** ★★★★

## 1. Place

| Preposition | Example |
|---|---|
| **at** (point / address) | *at the reception desk, at 25 Oak Street* |
| **on** (surface / street / floor) | *on the shelf, on Main Street, on the third floor* |
| **in** (inside / city / country) | *in the drawer, in London, in the lobby* |
| **next to / beside / by** | *The printer is **next to** the window.* |
| **between** (two) / **among** (3+) | ***among** the applicants* |
| **across from / opposite** | *The bank is **across from** the station.* |
| **in front of / behind** | |
| **above / over** · **below / under / beneath** | *a lamp **over** the table* |
| **near / close to** | |
| **along** | *Trees are planted **along** the road.* |

## 2. Movement

**into / out of, onto, through, across, past, toward(s), up / down, around**
*The workers are walking **through** the gate.* · *A man is getting **into** a car.*

## 3. Listening Part 1 key verbs + prepositions

- *Some boxes **are stacked on** a cart.* · *Chairs **have been arranged in** a row.*
- *A woman **is leaning against** a railing.* · *Documents **are spread out on** the desk.*
- *A ladder **is propped against** the wall.* · *People **are seated around** a table.*
  Note: **have been / are + V3** describes a *state* (no person needed); **is being + V3** needs someone doing it now.

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> The new branch office is located ------- the third floor of the Kensington Building.
> (A) in  (B) at  (C) on  (D) over

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) on.** Floors use **on**: *on the third floor*.
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. Please leave the documents ------- my desk.
   (A) in (B) on (C) at (D) into
2. The award was shared ------- the three team members.
   (A) between (B) among (C) through (D) along
3. The café is directly ------- the post office.
   (A) across from (B) among (C) into (D) through

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: a surface.
2. **(B)**: three or more people.
3. **(A)**: *across from* = opposite.
</details>
