# 02 — High-Frequency Business Collocations

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, 6, 7 · **Priority:** ★★★★★

Collocations are words that "go together". On real tests, three options are often grammatically possible and **only the collocation is correct**.

## 1. Verb + noun

| Verb | Noun partners |
|---|---|
| **make** | a decision, a reservation, an appointment, a payment, a profit, an effort, a complaint, a change, progress |
| **do** | business, research, a job, damage, one's best |
| **take** | place (happen), part in, advantage of, a break, effect (start to apply), measures, notes, responsibility |
| **meet** | a deadline, a demand, requirements, expectations, a target, a goal |
| **reach** | an agreement, a decision, a conclusion, a target |
| **place / put in** | an order |
| **submit / file** | a report, an application, a proposal, a claim |
| **conduct / carry out** | a survey, research, an inspection, an interview |
| **launch** | a product, a campaign |
| **raise** | funds, prices, awareness, a concern |
| **hold** | a meeting, a conference, a position |
| **address** | an issue, a problem, a concern, an audience |
| **implement** | a policy, a plan, changes |
| **issue** | a refund, a statement, a ticket |
| **waive** | a fee |
| **extend** | a deadline, an invitation, thanks, a contract |

## 2. Adjective + noun

**prompt** reply · **detailed** report · **competitive** price/salary · **extensive** experience · **comprehensive** coverage · **tentative** schedule · **valid** ID/passport · **considerable** amount · **sufficient** funds · **strict** regulations · **outstanding** balance/performance · **affordable** price · **preliminary** results · **routine** maintenance · **mandatory** training · **complimentary** breakfast (= free)

## 3. Adverb + adjective / verb

**highly** recommended / qualified / competitive · **fully** booked / refundable · **widely** known / used · **strongly** encouraged / recommended · **readily** available · **conveniently** located · **closely** monitor · **carefully** review · **tentatively** scheduled · **temporarily** closed · **sincerely** apologize

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> Unfortunately, we were unable to ------- the deadline due to a shortage of materials.
> (A) reach  (B) meet  (C) make  (D) do

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) meet.** The fixed collocation is **meet a deadline**. You *reach* an agreement and *make* a decision.
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. The hotel is ------- located near the train station.
   (A) convenient (B) conveniently (C) widely (D) highly
2. The company will ------- a survey to measure customer satisfaction.
   (A) conduct (B) make (C) do (D) take
3. Guests can enjoy a ------- breakfast every morning.
   (A) complementary (B) complimentary (C) compliment (D) completing

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: *conveniently located*.
2. **(A)**: *conduct a survey*.
3. **(B)**: *complimentary* = free.
</details>
