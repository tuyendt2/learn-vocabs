## TOEIC-style question (Part 7 synonym type)

> "…so we have decided to **put off** the product launch until the spring."
> The phrase "put off" is closest in meaning to
> (A) cancel  (B) postpone  (C) announce  (D) reject

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) postpone.** *until the spring* tells you the launch is moved to later, not cancelled.
</details>
