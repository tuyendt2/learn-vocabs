# 04 — Fixed Expressions in Emails, Letters & Notices (Part 6 & Part 7)

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 6, Part 7 · **Priority:** ★★★★

## 1. Openings

- *I am writing **to inquire about** / **in response to** / **with regard to** …*
- ***Thank you for** your inquiry / **choosing** us / **your prompt reply**.*
- *As requested, … / **As discussed** at yesterday's meeting, …*
- ***We are pleased to inform you** that … / **We regret to inform you** that …*

## 2. Body

- ***Please find attached** the invoice.* / ***Attached is** …* / ***Enclosed please find** …*
- ***We apologize for** any inconvenience **this may cause**.*
- ***Effective** immediately / **As of** May 1, …*
- ***Due to** unforeseen circumstances, …*
- *Your order **has been shipped** and **should arrive** within 3–5 business days.*
- *Seating is **limited**; **reservations are recommended**.*
- *Prices are **subject to change** without notice.*

## 3. Closings

- ***Should you have** any questions, **please do not hesitate to contact** me.*
- ***Please let me know** if you need further information.*
- ***I look forward to hearing** from you / **working** with you.*
- ***Thank you in advance** for your cooperation.*

## 4. Part 6 "insert the sentence" questions

Each Part 6 text has **one blank where a whole sentence goes**. Choose the sentence that
(1) matches the **topic** of the paragraph, (2) links to the sentence **before** with a pronoun or connector (*this, these, such, also, however*),
and (3) leads into the sentence **after**.

## 5. TOEIC-style question (Part 6)

> Dear Mr. Alvarez,
> Thank you for your order #4521. Unfortunately, the desk lamp you requested is temporarily out of stock. -------. We apologize for any inconvenience.
> (A) We expect to receive new stock by June 12.
> (B) Our store is located on Pine Street.
> (C) The lamp was delivered yesterday.
> (D) Please submit your résumé by Friday.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(A).** It follows on from "out of stock" by saying when stock will come back. (C) contradicts the text. (B) and (D) are off-topic.
</details>
