# 01 — Comparatives, Superlatives & Equality

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5 · **Priority:** ★★★★

## 1. Forms

| | Short adj (1 syllable / -y) | Long adj (2+ syllables) | Irregular |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comparative** | cheap**er**, bus**ier** | **more** efficient | better, worse, farther/further, less, more |
| **Superlative** | **the** cheap**est**, **the** bus**iest** | **the most** efficient | the best, the worst, the least, the most |

## 2. Clues in the sentence

| Clue | Answer form |
|---|---|
| **than** | comparative: *faster **than*** |
| **the … of / in / among / ever / yet / that I have seen** | superlative: ***the** largest **in** the region* |
| **one of the** + superlative + plural noun | *one of **the most popular** products* |
| **as** + adj/adv (base) + **as** | equality: *as **quickly** as possible* |
| **the same** (noun) **as** · **similar to** · **different from** | |

## 3. Modifiers

- Before comparatives: **much, far, even, still, a lot, considerably, significantly, slightly, a little** (*~~very~~ faster* ✗)
  → *This model is **much** lighter than the previous one.*
- Before superlatives: **by far, the very, easily**: ***by far the best** option*

## 4. Special patterns

- **The + comparative …, the + comparative …**: ***The earlier** you book, **the cheaper** the tickets.*
- **twice / three times as … as**: *Sales were **twice as** high **as** last year.*
- **no later than / no more than / not as … as**
- **prefer A to B / would rather A than B**

## 5. TOEIC-style question

> The new software is significantly ------- than the version we used last year.
> (A) fast  (B) faster  (C) fastest  (D) more fast

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) faster.** **than** shows you need a comparative. *Significantly* modifies the comparative. *Fast* is short, so **-er**.
</details>

## 6. Practice

1. This is the ------- hotel in the downtown area.
   (A) more luxurious (B) most luxurious (C) luxuriously (D) luxury
2. Please reply as ------- as possible.
   (A) soon (B) sooner (C) soonest (D) more soon
3. The more customers we attract, ------- our revenue will be.
   (A) high (B) higher (C) the higher (D) the highest

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: *the … in* means a superlative.
2. **(A)**: *as + base + as*.
3. **(C)**: *the + comparative, the + comparative*.
</details>
