# 04 — Nouns: Countable / Uncountable, Person vs. Thing, Compound Nouns

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★

## 1. Countable vs. uncountable

A **singular countable** noun needs a determiner (*a, the, this, my, each*…). Uncountable nouns are **never plural** and never take *a/an*.

Uncountable nouns that TOEIC loves:
**information, equipment, furniture, luggage/baggage, advice, feedback, research, machinery, merchandise, staff (collective), mail, software, access, permission, clothing, traffic, news**

| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right |
|---|---|
| ~~an equipment~~, ~~equipments~~ | **a piece of equipment**, **equipment** |
| ~~many informations~~ | **much information**, **a lot of information** |
| ~~a permission~~ | **permission** |

## 2. Person noun vs. activity / thing noun

| Person | Thing / activity |
|---|---|
| applicant | application |
| attendee / participant | attendance / participation |
| supervisor | supervision |
| competitor | competition |
| employee / employer | employment |
| consultant | consultation |
| analyst | analysis |
| accountant | accounting / account |

Check the verb or meaning: *The **participants** received certificates* (people receive things).

## 3. Compound nouns (noun + noun)

The first noun works like an adjective and stays **singular**:
**sales** figures, **customer** satisfaction, **safety** regulations, **job** opening, **expense** report, **application** form, **registration** fee, **product** launch, **staff** meeting, **budget** proposal, **retirement** party, **delivery** date.

TOEIC often gives a blank before a noun where the answer is **another noun**, not an adjective:
*Please fill out the ------- form* → **registration** (not *registered*).

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> All ------- for the conference must register online by June 10.
> (A) attend  (B) attendance  (C) attendees  (D) attending

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) attendees.** *All* + plural noun, and the noun must be a **person** (people *register*). *Attendance* is the activity.
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. Our office recently purchased new -------.
   (A) furnitures (B) furniture (C) furnish (D) a furniture
2. Please submit your expense ------- to the accounting department.
   (A) reported (B) reporting (C) reports (D) reporter
3. The company received over 300 ------- for the position.
   (A) apply (B) applicant (C) applications (D) applied

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: *furniture* is uncountable.
2. **(C)**: compound noun *expense report(s)*.
3. **(C)**: a number + plural noun. *Applicants* would also need to be plural; only *applications* is plural here.
</details>
