# 03 — Participle Adjectives: -ing vs. -ed

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5 · **Priority:** ★★★★

## 1. The rule

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **V-ing** (active) | the noun **causes** the feeling / **does** the action | *an **interesting** report* (the report interests people) |
| **V-ed** (passive) | the noun **receives** the feeling / action | *an **interested** customer* (the customer feels interest) |

People usually *feel* → **-ed**: *satisfied customers, qualified applicants, experienced staff*.
Things usually *cause* → **-ing**: *a challenging task, an encouraging result*.

## 2. Fixed pairs that appear on TOEIC

| -ing (active) | -ed (passive) |
|---|---|
| **existing** customers (= current) | **attached** file, **enclosed** document |
| **remaining** items | **detailed** report, **limited** time |
| **leading** company | **experienced/qualified** staff |
| **rising** costs | **revised** schedule, **updated** version |
| **missing** luggage | **dedicated** team, **designated** area |
| **upcoming** event (adj, not a participle) | **damaged** goods, **discounted** price |

## 3. Reduced relative clauses use the same logic

- *Employees **wishing** to attend should register.* (= employees who **wish**: active)
- *All forms **submitted** after May 1 will be rejected.* (= forms that **are submitted**: passive)

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> Please review the ------- document and sign it by Friday.
> (A) attach  (B) attaching  (C) attached  (D) attachment

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) attached.** The document *is attached* (passive) → **-ed** adjective. "the attached document / file / form" is a fixed TOEIC phrase.
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. Only ------- candidates will be contacted for an interview.
   (A) qualify (B) qualifying (C) qualified (D) qualification
2. The sales figures for March were very -------.
   (A) encouraged (B) encouraging (C) encourage (D) encouragement
3. Passengers ------- to Boston should proceed to Gate 12.
   (A) travel (B) traveled (C) traveling (D) travels

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(C)**: candidates *have the qualifications* (passive sense), so **qualified**.
2. **(B)**: the figures *encourage* people.
3. **(C)**: passengers who *are traveling* (active, reduced relative clause).
</details>
