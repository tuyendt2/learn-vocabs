# 02 — Listening Part 1 (Photos) & Part 2 (Question–Response)

## Part 1: Photographs (6 questions)

**Strategy**
1. In the 1–2 seconds before the audio, look at the photo: **who / what action / what objects / where**.
2. Eliminate answers with a **wrong verb** (*He is **typing*** while he's actually writing), a **wrong object**, or **things not in the photo**.
3. **Is being + V3** (*is being painted*) needs a person doing it **now**. **Has been / is + V3** (*has been painted*, *is stacked*) describes a **state**.

**Vocabulary to know**
*be stacked, be piled, be arranged, be lined up, be displayed, be hanging, be leaning against, be facing, be mounted on the wall, overlook (a view), pour, examine, adjust, reach for, sweep, wipe, load / unload, board, be docked, be parked, be shelved*

**TOEIC-style item.** Photo: a woman putting a box on a shelf.
(A) She's opening a box. (B) She's **placing an item on a shelf**. (C) She's sweeping the floor. (D) Shelves are being built.
→ **(B)**

## Part 2: Question–Response (25 questions, the biggest source of listening points)

**Strategy**
1. **Catch the first word.** *Who / When / Where / Why / How / Which / Do / Should / Would you…?* This is 80% of the work.
2. **Eliminate traps:**
   - **Repeated or similar-sounding words** (*copy / coffee, contract / contact*)
   - **Wrong question type** (*Where?* → "At 3 o'clock")
   - **Yes/No answering a Wh-question**
3. **Indirect answers are often right**: *"I'll have to check." / "It hasn't been decided." / "Ask Mr. Brown."*

See [Tag questions & short responses](../08-special-structures/03-tag-questions-and-short-responses.md) for patterns.

| Question word | Typical correct answer |
|---|---|
| **When** | *By Friday. / Not until next week. / Right after lunch.* |
| **Where** | *In the supply closet. / On Ms. Cho's desk. / I think it's been moved.* |
| **Who** | *The sales manager. / Mr. Gupta. / Someone from accounting.* |
| **Why** | *Because… / To… / Due to… / There was a problem with…* |
| **How long / How often / How much** | *For two hours. / Every Monday. / About $50.* |

**TOEIC-style item.**
Q: *Why was the shipment late?*
(A) A truck **broke down** on the highway. (B) It **shipped** to Denver. (C) Yes, it was late.
→ **(A)**. (B) is a similar-word trap. (C) is a yes/no answer to a *why* question.

## Review Quiz: 20 Items

> **Part 1 (1–6):** read the photo description, then choose the statement that matches it.
> **Part 2 (7–20):** choose the best response. If possible, have someone read the items aloud. **Target: 16/20.**

**Part 1**

1. *Photo: A man is standing on a ladder, changing a light bulb.*
   (A) He's climbing a mountain. (B) He's painting the ceiling. (C) The ladder is being folded. (D) He's replacing a bulb.
2. *Photo: Chairs are arranged in rows in an empty hall.*
   (A) Chairs have been set up in rows. (B) A speaker is on stage. (C) Chairs are being carried. (D) People are sitting in the hall.
3. *Photo: A woman is pouring coffee into a cup.*
   (A) She's washing dishes. (B) She's drinking from a bottle. (C) She's ordering a meal. (D) She's filling a cup.
4. *Photo: Several boats are tied up at a pier. No people.*
   (A) Boats are docked at a pier. (B) People are fishing. (C) Passengers are boarding. (D) A boat is being repaired.
5. *Photo: Two workers are loading boxes onto a truck.*
   (A) They're driving a truck. (B) Boxes are stacked in a warehouse. (C) They're unloading a ship. (D) They're putting boxes onto a vehicle.
6. *Photo: Books are displayed on shelves in a shop. No people.*
   (A) Books are on display. (B) The shelves are empty. (C) A customer is buying a book. (D) Books are being shelved.

**Part 2**

7. When is the report due?
   (A) It's in the report. (B) By Thursday afternoon. (C) Yes, it's due.
8. Where can I find the marketing department?
   (A) Since last year. (B) Market prices are rising. (C) On the fourth floor.
9. Who approved the budget?
   (A) The finance director. (B) About $5,000. (C) It's approved.
10. Why is the office so cold today?
   (A) The heater is broken. (B) It's in the office. (C) Yes, it's today.
11. How about meeting at 3 o'clock?
   (A) I met him at 3. (B) About three meetings. (C) That works for me.
12. Have you met the new intern?
   (A) Not yet. Is she here today? (B) The meeting is at noon. (C) An internal email.
13. Can you show me how to use this copier?
   (A) Two copies, please. (B) Sure, it's quite simple. (C) We need more coffee.
14. Which flight are you taking?
   (A) The 7 A.M. one. (B) Taking the bus. (C) It was a long flight.
15. Isn't the store closed on Mondays?
   (A) Not anymore. It's open every day now. (B) The store sells clothes. (C) Close the door, please.
16. Would you like me to call a taxi?
   (A) I'd like some tea. (B) That would be great, thanks. (C) The taxi was late yesterday.
17. How much does the conference cost?
   (A) It's free for members. (B) In the convention center. (C) Three days long.
18. Should I print the agenda or email it?
   (A) Yes, you should. (B) Email is fine. (C) The printer is new.
19. The shipment arrived this morning, didn't it?
   (A) Yes, it's in the warehouse. (B) Ship it by air. (C) Tomorrow morning.
20. When will the renovations be finished?
   (A) The contractor hasn't told us. (B) They renovated it. (C) In the lobby.
