## 2. Part 5: identify the question type in 3 seconds by **looking at the options**

| Options look like… | Type | What to do | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| *analyze / analysis / analytical / analytically* | **Word form** | Look only at the words **next to** the blank | [link](../01-parts-of-speech/01-word-form-and-sentence-position.md) |
| *approve / approved / is approved / will be approving* | **Verb form** | Check **subject (S–V agreement)**, **object (voice)**, **time words (tense)** | [voice](../02-verbs-and-tenses/06-active-vs-passive-voice.md) |
| *he / him / his / himself* | **Pronoun** | What is the slot: subject, object, before a noun, or "alone"? | [link](../04-pronouns-and-determiners/01-personal-possessive-reflexive-pronouns.md) |
| *although / despite / because / however* | **Connector** | **Clause or noun** after the blank? Then the meaning | [link](../05-clauses-and-connectors/02-conjunctions-vs-prepositions.md) |
| *in / on / by / until* | **Preposition** | Time, place, or fixed phrase? | [link](../06-prepositions/01-prepositions-of-time.md) |
| *who / whose / which / what* | **Relative / noun clause** | What follows: verb, noun, or full clause? | [link](../05-clauses-and-connectors/01-relative-clauses.md) |
| Four **different words** with the same part of speech | **Vocabulary** | Read the whole sentence; look for **collocations** | [link](../09-vocabulary-knowledge/02-business-collocations.md) |

**Rule:** do grammar questions fast. **Don't spend more than 30 seconds**: guess, mark it, and move on.

## 3. Part 6: grammar plus context

The difference from Part 5: you often **need the surrounding sentences**.
- **Verb tense**: was the event *already* held (past) or is it *upcoming* (future)? Check the date and context.
- **Pronoun / determiner**: what does *it / they / this* refer to?
- **Transition words**: *However / Therefore / In addition / For example*. Look at the sentence before.
- **Sentence insertion** (1 per text): see [Email & notice expressions](../09-vocabulary-knowledge/04-email-and-notice-expressions.md).

## 4. TOEIC-style item (Part 6 tense from context)

> …We are pleased to announce that our new branch on Elm Street ------- its doors next Monday. To celebrate, all customers will receive a 10% discount during the first week.
> (A) opened (B) has opened (C) will open (D) was opening

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) will open.** *next Monday* and *will receive* show this is future.
</details>
