# 01 — TOEIC L&R Format & What 650–700 Really Means

> Read this first. It tells you **how many questions you can afford to miss**.

## 1. Test format (200 questions, about 2 hours)

| Section | Part | Task | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Listening** | 1 | Photographs | 6 | ~45 min total |
| | 2 | Question–Response | 25 | |
| | 3 | Conversations | 39 (13 × 3) | |
| | 4 | Talks | 30 (10 × 3) | |
| **Reading** | 5 | Incomplete Sentences | 30 | **75 min total** |
| | 6 | Text Completion | 16 (4 texts × 4) | |
| | 7 | Reading Comprehension (single, double, triple passages) | 54 | |

Each section is scored 5–495, so the total is **10–990**.

## 2. The 650–700 target in numbers (approximate)

Score conversion changes a little from test to test, but as a rule of thumb:

| Target | Listening (correct / 100) | Reading (correct / 100) |
|---|---|---|
| **650** | ~68–72 → ~350 | ~60–64 → ~300 |
| **700** | ~72–76 → ~370 | ~66–70 → ~330 |

➡️ For 650–700 you can miss about **25–30 listening** and **30–35 reading** questions.
**You do not need to understand everything.** You need to **win the easy and medium points**.

## 3. Where the easy points are

1. **Part 5 grammar** (≈ 15–18 of 30 are pure grammar): word form, verb tense/voice, pronouns, prepositions vs. conjunctions → this folder.
2. **Part 1 & Part 2**: short, with recognizable traps.
3. **Part 7 single passages** (questions 147–175) before the triple passages.
