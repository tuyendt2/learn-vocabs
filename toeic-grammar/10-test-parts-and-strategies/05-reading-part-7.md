# 05 — Reading Part 7 (Reading Comprehension)

## 1. Structure

- **Single passages** (Q147–175): emails, ads, notices, text-message chats, articles
- **Double passages** (Q176–185): 2 related texts × 5 questions
- **Triple passages** (Q186–200): 3 related texts × 5 questions

For 650–700: **do all single passages carefully**, then double, then triple. Never leave anything blank (there is no penalty for guessing).

## 2. Question types & how to answer

| Type | Example wording | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Main purpose** | *Why was the e-mail written?* | First 2–3 sentences, and the subject line |
| **Detail** | *What time does the event start?* | Scan for the key word / number |
| **NOT true** | *What is NOT mentioned / indicated?* | Check each option in the text; the one you **can't find** is the answer |
| **Inference** | *What is suggested / most likely…?* | Not stated directly; choose the option the text **supports** |
| **Synonym** | *The word "cover" in paragraph 2 … is closest in meaning to* | Read the sentence; replace the word with each option |
| **Sentence insertion** | *In which position [1]–[4] does this sentence belong?* | Look for pronouns / connectors in the given sentence (*This, Also, However*) |
| **Chat intention** | *At 10:15, what does Ms. Lee mean when she writes "I'm on it"?* | Read 1–2 messages **before** it |
| **Cross-reference** (double/triple) | *How much will Mr. Kim pay?* | Combine 2 texts: e.g. the price list + the coupon in the email |

## 3. Paraphrase again

The text says *"complimentary shuttle"* → the answer says *"free transportation"*.
The text says *"we will no longer accept checks"* → the answer says *"a payment method will be discontinued"*.

## 4. TOEIC-style item (single passage)

> **NOTICE TO TENANTS**
> The water supply to Building C will be shut off on Thursday, October 9, from 9:00 A.M. to 1:00 P.M. while workers replace a main pipe. We recommend filling containers with water beforehand. We apologize for the inconvenience.
> — Westwood Property Management
>
> **1.** What is the purpose of the notice?
> (A) To announce a rent increase (B) To inform residents of a temporary service interruption (C) To advertise a plumbing company (D) To request payment for repairs
>
> **2.** What are tenants advised to do?
> (A) Leave the building (B) Contact a plumber (C) Store water in advance (D) Move their cars

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: *water supply … shut off … from 9 to 1* is paraphrased as "temporary service interruption".
2. **(C)**: *filling containers with water beforehand* is paraphrased as "store water in advance".
</details>

## Review Quiz: 20 Questions (4 passages)

> **Time yourself: about 20 minutes** (≈ 1 minute per question, as in the real test). **Target: 16/20.**

**Passage 1: Advertisement**

> **GREENLEAF CATERING: Fresh Food for Every Occasion**
> From small office lunches to weddings of 300 guests, Greenleaf has served the Riverside area since 2012. All menus use locally grown ingredients. Order at least 48 hours in advance through our website. First-time customers receive 15% off orders over $200. Delivery is free within 10 miles.

1. What is being advertised? (A) A grocery store (B) A food service company (C) A farm (D) A wedding venue
2. What is indicated about Greenleaf? (A) It opened last year. (B) It has several branches. (C) It uses local ingredients. (D) It serves only weddings.
3. How can a new customer get a discount? (A) By ordering by phone (B) By picking up the food (C) By spending more than $200 (D) By ordering 10 days ahead
4. The word "occasion" in line 1 is closest in meaning to (A) season (B) event (C) reason (D) chance
5. What is NOT mentioned? (A) Advance notice (B) Ordering method (C) Delivery area (D) Payment methods

**Passage 2: Email**

> **From:** Mark Ellis, Office Manager
> **To:** All Employees
> **Date:** April 2
> **Subject:** New printer system
>
> Starting Monday, April 8, all printers will require an employee ID card to release print jobs. This change will reduce paper waste and protect confidential documents. If your card does not work, please contact the IT help desk at extension 400. Training videos are available on the intranet. Thank you for your cooperation.

6. What is the purpose of the email? (A) To introduce a new employee (B) To announce a policy change (C) To request new printers (D) To schedule a meeting
7. When will the change take effect? (A) April 2 (B) April 4 (C) April 8 (D) April 10
8. What is one reason for the change? (A) To save time (B) To reduce waste (C) To cut staff (D) To speed up printing
9. What should employees do if their card fails? (A) Call the IT help desk (B) Request a new printer (C) Visit the office manager (D) Watch a video
10. Where can employees find training? (A) In a manual (B) At a workshop (C) From Mr. Ellis (D) On the intranet

**Passage 3: Text-message chat**

> **Nina Park [9:02]:** Hi Sam, the client from Bexley Corp. just called. Their flight lands at 2 instead of 4.
> **Sam Ortiz [9:04]:** That's earlier than we planned. Can someone pick them up?
> **Nina Park [9:05]:** I have a meeting until 3.
> **Sam Ortiz [9:06]:** No problem, I'm on it. I'll also move the factory tour to 3:30.
> **Nina Park [9:07]:** Perfect. I'll let the tour guide know.

11. What problem does Ms. Park mention? (A) A meeting was moved. (B) Clients are arriving earlier. (C) A flight was cancelled. (D) The factory is closed.
12. At 9:06, what does Mr. Ortiz mean when he writes, "I'm on it"? (A) He is in a meeting. (B) He is on a plane. (C) He will pick up the clients. (D) He disagrees.
13. Why can't Ms. Park go to the airport? (A) She is sick. (B) She has a meeting. (C) She has no car. (D) She is on vacation.
14. What will Ms. Park do next? (A) Contact the tour guide (B) Go to the factory (C) Cancel the tour (D) Call the airline
15. What time will the tour probably start? (A) 2:00 (B) 3:00 (C) 3:30 (D) 4:00

**Passage 4: Notice + Email (double passage)**

> **NOTICE: Westfield Library Room Rental**
> Meeting rooms: Room A (10 people) $20/hour · Room B (25 people) $35/hour · Hall (80 people) $60/hour
> Library members receive a 20% discount. Bookings must be made at least one week in advance.

> **From:** Ana Silva
> **To:** Westfield Library
> Hello, I am a library member and would like to book a room for a workshop for about 20 people on May 20, from 1 to 4 P.M. Please let me know if it is available.

16. Which room is most suitable for Ms. Silva? (A) Room B (B) Hall (C) None of them (D) Room A
17. How many hours does Ms. Silva want to book? (A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 20
18. How much will Ms. Silva probably pay? (A) $35 (B) $84 (C) $105 (D) $180
19. What is the latest date she could make this booking? (A) May 1 (B) May 13 (C) May 19 (D) May 20
20. What does Ms. Silva ask about? (A) Availability (B) Parking (C) The price (D) Membership
