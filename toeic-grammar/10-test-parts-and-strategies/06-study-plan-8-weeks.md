## Daily routine (example)

1. **15 min:** one grammar file. Read the rule and do the TOEIC-style question and practice **before** opening the answers.
2. **20 min:** 25 new words and a review of yesterday's words.
3. **30 min:** listening practice (shadow Part 3/4 scripts after checking answers).
4. **15 min:** 15 Part 5 questions with a 20-second timer each.

## Error log (the habit that moves your score most)

Keep a notebook or spreadsheet: **question → your answer → correct answer → grammar file it belongs to → reason**.
Every weekend, reread the grammar files that appear most often in your log.
