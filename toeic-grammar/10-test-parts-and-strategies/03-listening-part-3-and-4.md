# 03 — Listening Part 3 (Conversations) & Part 4 (Talks)

## 1. The #1 skill: READ the questions BEFORE the audio

Use the time while the directions and previous audio play to read the **3 questions** (and, if you have time, the options).
Underline the key words: **who / where / what problem / what will happen next**.

## 2. The answers come in order

- Q1 (topic, place, job): usually answered in the **first 1–2 lines**
- Q2 (detail, problem, reason): **middle**
- Q3 (next action, request, offer): **last lines**. Listen for *"I'll…", "Let me…", "Could you…", "Why don't you…"*

## 3. Paraphrase: the correct answer rarely uses the same words

| You hear | The correct option says |
|---|---|
| *"My flight was cancelled."* | *A travel plan has changed.* |
| *"The copier is jammed again."* | *Some equipment is malfunctioning.* |
| *"I'll e-mail you the floor plan."* | *Send a document.* |
| *"We're out of the medium size."* | *An item is unavailable.* |

**Options that repeat the exact words you heard are often traps.**

## 4. Special question types

- **Implied meaning:** *"Why does the man say, 'I've got a meeting at two'?"* → the reason or context (e.g. he **can't stay long / is declining**).
- **Graphic questions** (schedule, map, price list): **look at the graphic before the audio**. The audio gives information that links to it ("the cheaper one", "the room next to the elevator").
- **Three speakers:** expect a question about *what the women / men say*.

## 5. Part 4 talk types and their usual questions

| Talk type | Usual questions |
|---|---|
| **Telephone message** | Who is the caller? Why is she calling? What should the listener do? |
| **Announcement (airport, store)** | Where is it heard? What has changed? What are listeners asked to do? |
| **Advertisement** | What is advertised? What is special? How can you get a discount? |
| **Radio / news / traffic** | What is the topic? What is the cause of a delay? What will be next? |
| **Meeting / tour / speech** | Who is the speaker? What is the purpose? What will happen next? |

## 6. TOEIC-style item (Part 3)

> **W:** Hi, I ordered business cards last week, but the phone number on them is wrong.
> **M:** I'm sorry about that. Let me pull up your order... I see the error was ours. **I'll reprint them today at no charge.**
> *What does the man offer to do?*
> (A) Issue a refund (B) **Replace an order for free** (C) Change a phone number (D) Deliver some cards to her office

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B).** "reprint them today at no charge" is paraphrased as "replace an order for free". (C) is a word trap using *phone number*.
</details>

## Review Quiz: 20 Questions (scripts + paraphrase)

> Read each short script as if you heard it, then answer. In the real test the script is audio only, so practise reading the questions **first**. **Target: 16/20.**

**Conversation 1**
> **M:** Hi, I'm calling about the laptop I ordered. It was supposed to arrive yesterday, but it hasn't come.
> **W:** I'm sorry. Let me check... It looks like there was a problem at our warehouse. I can send it by express shipping at no extra cost.
> **M:** That would be great. I need it for a business trip on Friday.

1. Why is the man calling? (A) To ask for a discount (B) To cancel an order (C) To report a late delivery (D) To book a trip
2. What caused the problem? (A) A payment error (B) A wrong address (C) Bad weather (D) A warehouse issue
3. What does the woman offer? (A) A store coupon (B) A refund (C) Free faster shipping (D) A different laptop
4. Why does the man need the item? (A) For a training class (B) For a business trip (C) As a gift (D) For a presentation tomorrow

**Conversation 2**
> **W:** Tom, are you going to the client dinner tonight?
> **M:** I'd like to, but I have to finish the sales report by tomorrow morning.
> **W:** Why don't you ask Ken to help? He finished his project early.
> **M:** Good idea. I'll go and talk to him now.

5. What does the man have to do? (A) Train Ken (B) Finish a report (C) Meet a client (D) Book a restaurant
6. What does the woman suggest? (A) Asking a coworker for help (B) Extending a deadline (C) Skipping dinner (D) Calling the client
7. What will the man probably do next? (A) Go to dinner (B) Go home (C) Speak with Ken (D) Email the client

**Talk 1: Announcement**
> Attention, shoppers. Our store will be closing in 15 minutes. Please bring your final purchases to the registers at the front. Also, don't forget that our weekend sale starts tomorrow, with 30% off all kitchen appliances. Thank you for shopping at Home Plus.

8. Where is the announcement being made? (A) At a factory (B) At a restaurant (C) At a store (D) At an airport
9. What are listeners asked to do? (A) Leave by the back door (B) Sign up for a card (C) Return tomorrow morning (D) Go to the checkout
10. What will happen tomorrow? (A) A kitchen will open (B) A sale will begin (C) New staff will start (D) The store will close

**Talk 2: Telephone message**
> Hi, this is Laura from Bright Dental. I'm calling to remind you of your appointment with Dr. Kim on Tuesday at 10 A.M. Unfortunately, Dr. Kim will be out that day, so we'd like to move your appointment to Wednesday at the same time. Please call us back at 555-0182 to confirm.

11. Who is the speaker? (A) A pharmacist (B) A patient (C) A dentist (D) A receptionist at a clinic
12. What is the purpose of the call? (A) To ask for payment (B) To change an appointment (C) To offer a job (D) To cancel a membership
13. What is the listener asked to do? (A) Return the call (B) Pay a fee (C) Visit on Tuesday (D) Send an email

**Paraphrase matching**: which option best matches what you hear?

14. "The elevator is out of order, so please use the stairs." (A) A machine isn't working. (B) The stairs are closed. (C) A new elevator was installed.
15. "We've sold out of the large size." (A) An item is unavailable. (B) The store is too large. (C) A sale has started.
16. "I'll e-mail you the directions." (A) Send some information (B) Draw a map now (C) Give a ride
17. "My train was delayed by an hour." (A) A meeting started early. (B) A train ticket was lost. (C) A trip took longer than planned.
18. "Could you cover my shift on Saturday?" (A) Buy a gift (B) Work in someone's place (C) Clean a room
19. "We're short-staffed this week." (A) The staff are short. (B) There aren't enough employees. (C) The week is short.
20. "The quarterly figures look much better than expected." (A) Financial results exceeded forecasts. (B) Some numbers were missing. (C) A report was late.
