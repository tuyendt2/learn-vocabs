# 01 — Gerund (V-ing) vs. Infinitive (to V)

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★★

## 1. Verbs + to V

**agree, aim, arrange, ask, attempt, decide, deserve, expect, fail, hesitate, hope, intend, manage, need, offer, plan, prepare, promise, refuse, seek, strive, tend, want, wish**
*We **aim to reduce** costs by 15%.* · *Please do not **hesitate to contact** us.*

**Verb + object + to V:** advise, allow, ask, encourage, enable, expect, invite, permit, remind, require, urge, want
*The new system **enables staff to work** remotely.*

## 2. Verbs + V-ing

**avoid, consider, delay, deny, discuss, enjoy, finish, include, involve, keep, mind, postpone, practice, recommend, resume, risk, suggest, quit**
*We **recommend booking** early.* · *Would you **mind waiting** a moment?*

## 3. ⭐ "to" as a PREPOSITION: always + V-ing

**look forward to, be committed to, be dedicated to, be used to, be accustomed to, object to, contribute to, in addition to, with a view to, be devoted to, be key to**
*We **look forward to working** with you.* (not ~~to work~~)
*The firm **is committed to reducing** waste.*

## 4. After prepositions → V-ing

*before **leaving**, after **reviewing**, by **using**, without **asking**, instead of **printing**, for **booking**, interested in **joining***

## 5. Meaning changes

| Verb | + to V | + V-ing |
|---|---|---|
| **remember** | remember **to lock** (don't forget, future) | remember **locking** (memory, past) |
| **stop** | stop **to rest** (in order to) | stop **smoking** (quit) |
| **try** | try **to fix** (make an effort) | try **restarting** (experiment) |

## 6. Purpose: in order to / so as to / to

*To **receive** a refund, please keep your receipt.* · *in order **to meet** the deadline*

## 7. TOEIC-style question

> Ms. Okafor is looking forward to ------- the new branch in Lagos.
> (A) manage  (B) managing  (C) managed  (D) be managed

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) managing.** In *look forward to*, "to" is a **preposition**, so it is followed by **V-ing**. Real TOEIC tests ask about this phrase very often.
</details>

## 8. Practice

1. The company decided ------- its headquarters to Seoul.
   (A) move (B) moving (C) to move (D) moved
2. Please avoid ------- personal calls during working hours.
   (A) to make (B) making (C) make (D) made
3. In addition to ------- discounts, the store offers free delivery.
   (A) provide (B) provided (C) providing (D) provision of

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(C)**: *decide to V*.
2. **(B)**: *avoid V-ing*.
3. **(C)**: *in addition to* + V-ing. *(D)* would need a noun after it.
</details>
