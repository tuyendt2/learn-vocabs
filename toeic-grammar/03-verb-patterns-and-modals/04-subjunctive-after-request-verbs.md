# 04 — Subjunctive after Request / Suggestion Words

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5 · **Priority:** ★★★ (a favourite "hard" item for 600+ learners)

## 1. The rule

After verbs/adjectives of **demand, request, suggestion, necessity** + **that**, use the **base form of the verb** for every subject (no -s, no tense):

**Verbs:** recommend, suggest, request, require, ask, insist, demand, propose, advise, urge
**Adjectives:** It is **essential / important / necessary / vital / imperative / mandatory** that …

- *The manager **recommended that** Mr. Lopez **attend** the workshop.* (not ~~attends~~)
- *It is **essential that** every form **be signed**.* (passive = **be + V3**)
- *They **requested that** the delivery **not be** delayed.* (negative = **not + base**)

## 2. TOEIC-style question

> The board of directors has requested that the CFO ------- a revised budget by Friday.
> (A) submits  (B) submit  (C) submitted  (D) submitting

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) submit.** *request that* + subject + **base form**, even with the singular subject *the CFO*.
</details>

## 3. Practice

1. It is necessary that all visitors ------- a security badge.
   (A) wear (B) wears (C) wore (D) wearing
2. The consultant suggested that the meeting ------- postponed.
   (A) is (B) be (C) was (D) being

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(A)**: *It is necessary that* + base form.
2. **(B)**: subjunctive passive, *be + V3*.
</details>
