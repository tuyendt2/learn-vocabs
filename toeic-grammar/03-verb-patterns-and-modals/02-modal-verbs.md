# 02 — Modal Verbs

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6, Listening Parts 2–4 · **Priority:** ★★★★

## 1. Grammar rule: modal + bare infinitive (base verb)

*must **submit*** (not ~~must to submit~~ / ~~must submits~~) · passive: *must **be submitted*** · perfect: *should **have submitted***

## 2. Meanings

| Meaning | Modals | Example |
|---|---|---|
| obligation | must, have to, be required to | *Visitors **must** sign in at reception.* |
| no obligation | don't have to, need not | *You **don't have to** bring your laptop.* |
| prohibition | must not, may not, cannot | *Food **must not** be taken into the lab.* |
| advice | should, ought to, had better | *You **should** back up your files.* |
| permission / request | can, could, may, would you mind | ***Could** you send me the file?* |
| possibility | may, might, could | *The shipment **may** be delayed.* |
| deduction | must (sure yes), can't (sure no) | *He **must** be in a meeting.* |
| past regret / criticism | should have + V3 | *We **should have ordered** more stock.* |
| past possibility | may/might/could have + V3 | *The email **might have gone** to spam.* |

## 3. Polite request & offer patterns (Listening Part 2)

- "***Would you like** me to …?*" → offer · "***Why don't** we …?*" / "***How about** V-ing?*" → suggestion
- "***Would you mind** V-ing …?*" → answer "*Not at all.*" = OK

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> All expense reports must ------- to the accounting department by the 5th of each month.
> (A) submit  (B) submitted  (C) be submitted  (D) submitting

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) be submitted.** A modal is followed by a base form. Reports *are submitted* by someone (passive), so **must be + V3**.
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. Employees ------- wear protective glasses in the factory.
   (A) must (B) must to (C) musts (D) must be
2. The meeting has been cancelled, so you ------- prepare the slides.
   (A) must not (B) don't have to (C) should (D) can't
3. We ------- booked the venue earlier; now it is fully reserved.
   (A) should (B) should have (C) must (D) would

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(A)**: modal + base verb.
2. **(B)**: no obligation.
3. **(B)**: past regret uses *should have + V3*.
</details>
