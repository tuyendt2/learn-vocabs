# 01 — Pronouns: Subject, Object, Possessive, Reflexive

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★★ (1–2 questions on every test)

## 1. The table

| Subject | Object | Possessive adj. (+ noun) | Possessive pronoun (alone) | Reflexive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself / yourselves |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | — | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

## 2. How to choose

| Slot | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| before a verb (subject) | **subject** | ***They** approved the plan.* |
| after a verb / preposition | **object** | *Please contact **us**. / for **him*** |
| before a noun | **possessive adjective** | *Please submit **your** report.* |
| stands alone, replaces "my X" | **possessive pronoun** | *Her idea was better than **mine**.* |
| subject = object | **reflexive** | *She introduced **herself**.* |
| emphasis "personally" | **reflexive** | *The CEO **himself** answered.* |
| **by + reflexive** = alone, without help | **reflexive** | *He finished it **by himself**.* |

⚠️ ***its*** (possessive) vs. ***it's*** (= it is): *The company changed **its** logo.*
⚠️ **on one's own** = by oneself: *She organized the event **on her own**.*

## 3. Part 6: who does the pronoun refer to?

Read the earlier sentences: the pronoun must match the **number** and **gender** of the antecedent.
*The **clients** visited the plant. ------- were impressed.* → **They**

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> Mr. Hassan prepared the entire presentation -------, without any help from his team.
> (A) he  (B) his  (C) him  (D) himself

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(D) himself.** The sentence is complete, and the blank emphasizes that he did it **alone**, so a **reflexive** pronoun.
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. Employees should keep ------- desks tidy.
   (A) they (B) them (C) their (D) theirs
2. If you have questions, please contact Ms. Wu or -------.
   (A) I (B) me (C) my (D) mine
3. Our prices are lower than ------- of our competitors.
   (A) that (B) those (C) them (D) it

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(C)**: possessive adjective before the noun *desks*.
2. **(B)**: object after a verb (*contact … me*).
3. **(B)**: *those* replaces the plural noun *prices* (a comparison pattern).
</details>
