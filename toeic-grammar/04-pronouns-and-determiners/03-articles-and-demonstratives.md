# 03 — Articles (a / an / the / Ø) and this / that / these / those

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★

## 1. Articles

| Article | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **a / an** | one, not specific, first mention; **an** before a vowel **sound** | *a report, **an** hour, **a** university, **an** MBA* |
| **the** | specific, already mentioned, unique, superlatives, ordinals | ***the** report I sent, **the** CEO, **the** best, **the** first* |
| **Ø (no article)** | general plural / uncountable | *Prices are rising. Information is power.* |

Fixed phrases: *on **a** daily basis, at **the** end of, in **the** meantime, **as a** result, **a** variety of, **a** wide range of, **the** number of / **a** number of, on **the** other hand, by **the** way.*

## 2. this / that / these / those

- **this / that** + singular · **these / those** + plural
- **those who** + plural verb = people who: ***Those who** wish to attend should register.*
- Comparison replacement: *The **price** of this model is lower than **that** of the old one.* / *Our **rates** are lower than **those** of our rivals.*

## 3. TOEIC-style question

> ------- interested in the volunteer program should contact Ms. Novak by Friday.
> (A) Whoever  (B) Those  (C) Anyone who  (D) They

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) Those.** *Those (who are) interested* = the people interested. *Anyone who* would need *is* after it. *Whoever* would need a verb.
</details>

## 4. Practice

1. The salaries in this region are higher than ------- in the capital.
   (A) that (B) those (C) it (D) them
2. Mr. Park has worked here for ------- hour and a half.
   (A) a (B) an (C) the (D) Ø

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: replaces the plural *salaries*.
2. **(B)**: *hour* starts with a vowel sound.
</details>
