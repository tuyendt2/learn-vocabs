# 05 — Noun Clauses (that / whether / if / what / wh-words)

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★

## 1. What is a noun clause?

A full clause that works as a **subject or object**.

| Word | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **that** | statement (fact) | *We confirmed **that** the order had shipped.* |
| **whether / if** | yes/no question | *Please let us know **whether** you can attend.* |
| **what** | the thing that (missing subject/object inside) | ***What** impressed me was the service.* |
| **who, when, where, why, how** | wh-question | *I'm not sure **when** the meeting starts.* |

## 2. Rules

- **Word order** is statement order (no question inversion): *Do you know where ~~is the office~~ → **the office is**?*
- **whether** (not *if*) at the start of a sentence, after a preposition, and before **or not / to V**:
  *The decision depends **on whether** funding is approved.* · *We are deciding **whether to** expand.*
- **that** vs **what**: *that* + **complete** clause; *what* + clause **missing a noun**.
  *He said **that** the plan works.* / *He explained **what** the plan involves.*

## 3. TOEIC-style question

> The manager has not yet decided ------- to extend the deadline.
> (A) that  (B) whether  (C) what  (D) if

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) whether.** Before **to + V**, use **whether** (not *if*). It is a yes/no meaning: extend or not.
</details>

## 4. Practice

1. ------- the merger will be approved is still uncertain.
   (A) That (B) Whether (C) What (D) If
2. Please tell me ------- the conference will take place.
   (A) where (B) what (C) which (D) that
3. The survey shows ------- customers prefer online shopping.
   (A) what (B) that (C) which (D) whether

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: a yes/no subject clause at the start of the sentence uses *whether*.
2. **(A)**: a place, followed by a complete clause.
3. **(B)**: a fact and a complete clause.
</details>
