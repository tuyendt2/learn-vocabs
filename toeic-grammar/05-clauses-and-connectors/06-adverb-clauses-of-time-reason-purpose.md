# 06 — Adverb Clauses: Time, Reason, Purpose, Contrast

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★

## 1. Time

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **as soon as / once** | immediately after | ***Once** the payment **is received**, we will ship your order.* |
| **by the time** | before / no later than | ***By the time** you arrive, the room will be ready.* |
| **until / till** | up to a point | *Please wait **until** your name **is called**.* |
| **while / as** | at the same time | ***While** you were out, a client called.* |
| **since** | from a past point | *It has rained **since** we arrived.* |

Remember: **present tense for future** in these clauses (see [Future forms](../02-verbs-and-tenses/03-future-forms.md)).

## 2. Reason / purpose / result

- **because / since / as / now that** + clause: ***Now that** the renovation is complete, we can reopen.*
- **so that / in order that** + clause (purpose, often with *can / will / could*): *Arrive early **so that** you **can** get a good seat.*
- **so … that / such … that** (result)

## 3. Contrast

- **although / even though / though** + clause
- **while / whereas** (comparison of two facts): *Sales rose in Asia, **whereas** they fell in Europe.*
- **even if** (hypothetical): *We'll proceed **even if** it rains.*

## 4. Reduced adverb clauses (conjunction + V-ing / V3)

- ***When using** the machine, wear gloves.* (= when you use)
- ***Once approved**, the plan will take effect.* (= once it is approved)
- ***Before submitting** the form, check all fields.*

## 5. TOEIC-style question

> Please keep your receipt ------- you need to return the item.
> (A) in case  (B) so that  (C) even though  (D) unless

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(A) in case.** Keep the receipt as a **precaution**: it might be needed.
</details>

## 6. Practice

1. ------- completed, the application should be sent to HR.
   (A) Since (B) Once (C) Because (D) Unless
2. We hired more staff ------- we could handle the holiday rush.
   (A) so that (B) even if (C) whereas (D) because of
3. Mr. Ruiz prefers email, ------- his partner prefers phone calls.
   (A) whereas (B) despite (C) so (D) because

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: a reduced clause: *once (it is) completed*.
2. **(A)**: purpose.
3. **(A)**: a comparison of two facts.
</details>
