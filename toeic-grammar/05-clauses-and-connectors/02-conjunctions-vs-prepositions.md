# 02 — Conjunction vs. Preposition vs. Adverb (because / because of / therefore)

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★★ (the most important structure point for 650+)

## 1. The golden rule

| Type | Followed by | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Conjunction** | a **clause** (subject + verb) | because, since, as, although, though, even though, while, whereas, if, unless, once, when, before, after, until, so that |
| **Preposition** | a **noun / V-ing** | because of, due to, owing to, on account of, despite, in spite of, during, in case of, regardless of, notwithstanding, prior to, following, instead of |
| **Adverb (transition)** | starts a **new sentence**, usually with a comma | however, therefore, consequently, as a result, nevertheless, moreover, furthermore, in addition, otherwise, meanwhile, instead |

## 2. Meaning groups

| Meaning | Conjunction (+ clause) | Preposition (+ noun) | Adverb (new sentence) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **reason** | because, since, as, now that | because of, due to, owing to | — |
| **contrast** | although, though, even though, while, whereas | despite, in spite of | however, nevertheless, nonetheless |
| **result** | so (that) | — | therefore, thus, consequently, as a result |
| **condition** | if, unless, provided (that), as long as, in case | in case of, in the event of | otherwise |
| **time** | while, when, before, after, until, once, as soon as | during, before, after, until, following, prior to | meanwhile, afterward, then |
| **addition** | and | in addition to, besides | moreover, furthermore, in addition, also |

## 3. Examples

- ***Although** the weather was bad, the event was a success.*
- ***Despite** the bad weather, the event was a success.*
- *The weather was bad. **However**, the event was a success.*
- *The flight was delayed **due to** fog.* / *…**because** there was fog.*

## 4. Part 6 tip

For the **transition word** blank at the start of a sentence, read the **sentence before**:
contrast → *However*, result → *Therefore / As a result*, extra point → *In addition / Moreover*.

## 5. TOEIC-style question

> ------- the heavy rain, the outdoor concert went ahead as planned.
> (A) Although  (B) Despite  (C) Because  (D) However

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) Despite.** *the heavy rain* is a **noun phrase**, not a clause, so you need a **preposition**. The meaning is contrast, so **despite**.
</details>

## 6. Practice

1. The store will close early ------- the national holiday.
   (A) because (B) due to (C) although (D) so
2. ------- sales increased, profits remained flat.
   (A) Despite (B) In spite of (C) Although (D) Nevertheless
3. The printer is out of toner. -------, we cannot print the handouts.
   (A) Because (B) Therefore (C) Although (D) Despite

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: a noun phrase and a reason.
2. **(C)**: a clause (*sales increased*) and contrast.
3. **(B)**: a new sentence and a result.
</details>
