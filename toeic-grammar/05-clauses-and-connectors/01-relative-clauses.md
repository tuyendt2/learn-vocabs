# 01 — Relative Clauses (who / which / that / whose / whom / where / when)

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★★

## 1. Choosing the relative pronoun

| Antecedent | Subject of clause | Object of clause | Possessive |
|---|---|---|---|
| person | **who / that** | **whom / who / that** (or omitted) | **whose** + noun |
| thing | **which / that** | **which / that** (or omitted) | **whose** + noun |
| place | — | **where** (= in/at which) | |
| time | — | **when** (= on/in which) | |

- *Applicants **who** speak Spanish are preferred.* (who + verb)
- *The consultant **whom** we hired was excellent.* (whom + subject + verb)
- *The company **whose** CEO resigned…* (**whose + noun**, very common on TOEIC)
- *The hotel **where** we stayed…* (where + full clause)

## 2. Quick decision

1. After the blank comes a **verb** → *who / which / that*
2. After the blank comes a **noun + verb** and the noun "belongs" to the antecedent → **whose**
3. After the blank comes **subject + verb**, and the clause has a missing object → *whom / which / that*
4. After the blank comes a **complete clause** → *where / when* (or *preposition + which*)

## 3. Commas and "that"

- **No "that" after a comma or a preposition:** *The report, ~~that~~ → **which** was published…* / *the office **in which** I work*
- **what** = the thing(s) that (no antecedent): ***What** customers want is reliability.*

## 4. Reduced relative clauses

- active → **V-ing**: *Staff **wishing** to attend…* (= who wish)
- passive → **V3**: *The items **ordered** last week…* (= which were ordered)

## 5. TOEIC-style question

> Ms. Kaur, ------- team won the innovation award, will speak at the conference.
> (A) who  (B) whom  (C) whose  (D) which

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) whose.** After the blank comes the **noun** *team* that belongs to Ms. Kaur, followed by a verb, so **whose + noun**.
</details>

## 6. Practice

1. The printer ------- was installed yesterday is already broken.
   (A) who (B) which (C) where (D) whose
2. The restaurant ------- we held the reception has closed.
   (A) which (B) where (C) when (D) whose
3. Customers ------- purchases exceed $100 receive free shipping.
   (A) who (B) whom (C) whose (D) that

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: a thing, and a verb follows.
2. **(B)**: a place, and a complete clause follows (*we held the reception*).
3. **(C)**: *whose purchases* (possession).
</details>
