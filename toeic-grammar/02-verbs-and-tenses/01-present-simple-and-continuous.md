# 01 — Present Simple & Present Continuous

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6, Listening Part 2 · **Priority:** ★★★★

## 1. Uses

| Tense | Form | Use | Signal words |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Present simple** | V / V-s | habits, facts, schedules/timetables (even future) | every, usually, always, often, generally, regularly, each month |
| **Present continuous** | am/is/are + V-ing | happening now, temporary situations, **fixed future arrangements** | now, currently, at the moment, this week, presently |

- *The shuttle bus **leaves** every 20 minutes.* (timetable)
- *The train **departs** at 9:15 tomorrow.* (schedule = present simple for future)
- *We **are currently reviewing** your application.*
- *Mr. Lee **is meeting** the clients on Friday.* (arrangement)

## 2. Stative verbs: not normally used in -ing

**know, believe, understand, belong, own, need, want, prefer, cost, contain, include, consist, seem, appear (= seem), require, depend**
*The package ~~is containing~~ → **contains** a user manual.*

## 3. Subject–verb agreement check (very common trap)

He/She/It/singular noun → **V-s**: *The manager **approves** all requests.*
*Each / every / everyone / the number of* → **singular**.

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> Ms. Rivera ------- the quarterly sales report every three months.
> (A) prepare  (B) prepares  (C) is preparing  (D) have prepared

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) prepares.** *Every three months* means a habit, so **present simple**. The subject is singular, so add **-s**.
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. The company ------- expanding into the Asian market at present.
   (A) is (B) are (C) be (D) has
2. The fee ------- breakfast and airport transfers.
   (A) is including (B) include (C) includes (D) including
3. The museum ------- at 9 A.M. on weekdays.
   (A) open (B) opens (C) opening (D) is opened

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(A)**: present continuous; *at present* = now; singular subject.
2. **(C)**: *include* is stative; singular subject.
3. **(B)**: a schedule; active voice (the museum opens).
</details>
