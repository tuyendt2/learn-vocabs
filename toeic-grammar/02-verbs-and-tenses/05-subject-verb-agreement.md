# 05 — Subject–Verb Agreement

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★★

## 1. Find the real subject: ignore what is in between

*The **list** of approved suppliers **is** posted online.* (subject = list, not suppliers)
*The **results** of the survey **were** surprising.*
*The **manager**, along with her assistants, **is** attending.* (*along with / as well as / together with* do **not** make it plural)

## 2. Singular vs. plural rules

| Singular verb | Plural verb |
|---|---|
| each / every / either / neither + noun | both … and … |
| everyone, someone, nobody, anything | a number of + plural noun (= many) |
| **the number of** + plural noun | several, many, few, a few + plural noun |
| uncountable nouns (information, equipment) | the police, people |
| amounts as one unit: *Five hundred dollars **is** a fair price.* | |
| gerund subject: ***Reviewing** contracts **takes** time.* | |

- ***The number of** applicants **has** increased.* vs. ***A number of** applicants **have** called.*

## 3. either/or, neither/nor, not only/but also: agree with the **nearer** subject

*Neither the manager nor the **employees were** informed.*
*Either the employees or the **manager is** responsible.*

## 4. Relative clauses: the verb agrees with the antecedent

*Customers **who purchase** two items receive a discount.*
*The **printer** that **is** on the third floor needs repair.*

## 5. TOEIC-style question

> The information on our website ------- updated every Monday morning.
> (A) are  (B) is  (C) were  (D) be

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(B) is.** *Information* is **uncountable**, so it takes a singular verb. *Every Monday* means a habit, so the present tense.
</details>

## 6. Practice

1. Each of the candidates ------- an interview with the director.
   (A) have (B) has (C) are having (D) were
2. A number of employees ------- requested flexible working hours.
   (A) has (B) have (C) is (D) was
3. The cost of the repairs ------- higher than expected.
   (A) were (B) are (C) was (D) have been

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: *Each of* takes a singular verb.
2. **(B)**: *A number of* = many, so plural.
3. **(C)**: the subject is *cost* (singular).
</details>
