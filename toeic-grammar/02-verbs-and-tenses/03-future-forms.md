# 03 — Future Forms & Future in Time Clauses

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★★

## 1. Ways to talk about the future

| Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **will + V** | decisions, predictions, promises, formal announcements | *The store **will close** at 6 P.M. on Friday.* |
| **be going to + V** | plans, evidence-based predictions | *We**'re going to hire** two more engineers.* |
| **present continuous** | personal arrangements | *I'**m flying** to Tokyo on Monday.* |
| **present simple** | timetables | *The seminar **starts** at 10.* |
| **be + to V / be scheduled to / be expected to / be due to** | official plans (very common in TOEIC) | *The CEO **is scheduled to speak** at noon.* |
| **will be + V-ing** | in progress at a future time | *This time tomorrow, I**'ll be traveling**.* |
| **will have + V3** | completed before a future time | ***By** next June, we **will have finished** the renovation.* |

## 2. ⭐ Future in time/condition clauses: use PRESENT, not *will*

After **when, before, after, as soon as, once, until, by the time, if, unless, provided that**, use **present simple / present perfect** for future meaning:

- *Please call me **as soon as** the shipment **arrives**.* (not ~~will arrive~~)
- ***Once** you **have completed** the form, return it to reception.*
- *The project will be delayed **unless** we **receive** more funding.*

This pattern appears on **almost every real TOEIC test**.

## 3. TOEIC-style question

> When Mr. Daniels ------- from his business trip, he will review the contract.
> (A) returns  (B) will return  (C) returned  (D) has been returned

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(A) returns.** The main clause is future (*will review*), but after **when** the time clause uses the **present simple**.
</details>

## 4. Practice

1. By the end of this year, the company ------- its 100th store.
   (A) opens (B) will have opened (C) has opened (D) opened
2. The new manager ------- to arrive next Monday.
   (A) expects (B) is expected (C) expecting (D) has expected
3. Please do not leave the building until the fire alarm -------.
   (A) will stop (B) stopped (C) stops (D) stopping

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: *By + future time* means future perfect.
2. **(B)**: *be expected to V*.
3. **(C)**: after *until*, use the present for future meaning.
</details>
