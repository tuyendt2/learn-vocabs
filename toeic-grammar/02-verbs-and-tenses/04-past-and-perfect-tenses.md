# 04 — Past Continuous, Past Perfect & Tense Sequence

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6, Part 7 · **Priority:** ★★★

## 1. Forms

| Tense | Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Past continuous** | was/were + V-ing | in progress at a past moment, often interrupted | *I **was preparing** the slides **when** the power went out.* |
| **Past perfect** | had + V3 | happened **before** another past action | ***By the time** we arrived, the meeting **had** already **started**.* |
| **Past perfect continuous** | had been + V-ing | duration up to a past point | *She **had been working** there for ten years before she retired.* |

## 2. Signal words

- **by the time + past simple** → main clause **past perfect**
- **before / after / already / prior to** often signal a past-perfect sequence
- **while / when + past continuous**

## 3. Reported speech: shift the tense back

*"The order **will ship** tomorrow."* → *She said the order **would ship** the next day.*
*"We **have finished**."* → *They said they **had finished**.*

## 4. TOEIC-style question

> By the time the technician arrived, the staff ------- the problem themselves.
> (A) solve  (B) have solved  (C) had solved  (D) are solving

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) had solved.** *By the time + past* → the earlier action is in the **past perfect**.
</details>

## 5. Practice

1. While Ms. Grant ------- the report, she found several errors.
   (A) reviews (B) was reviewing (C) has reviewed (D) review
2. The client said that the payment ------- the following week.
   (A) will arrive (B) would arrive (C) arrives (D) arriving
3. Before joining our firm, Mr. Ito ------- for a major bank in London.
   (A) has worked (B) had worked (C) works (D) is working

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: past continuous with *while*.
2. **(B)**: reported speech: *will* becomes *would*.
3. **(B)**: an action completed before another past situation, so past perfect.
</details>
