# 06 — Active vs. Passive Voice

> **TOEIC parts:** Part 5, Part 6 · **Priority:** ★★★★★ (1–3 questions on every test)

## 1. Form: be + past participle (V3)

| Tense | Passive |
|---|---|
| present simple | is/are **checked** |
| present continuous | is/are **being checked** |
| past simple | was/were **checked** |
| present perfect | has/have **been checked** |
| future | will **be checked** |
| modal | must/can/should **be checked** |
| infinitive | to **be checked** |

## 2. How to decide in 3 seconds

1. Is there an **object after the blank**? → **Active**.
   *The committee ------- **the proposal**.* → *approved*
2. **No object**, and the subject **receives** the action? → **Passive**.
   *The proposal ------- yesterday.* → *was approved*
3. Is there **by + agent**? → Usually **passive**.

⚠️ **Intransitive verbs** (no object) can **never** be passive:
**occur, happen, arrive, rise, fall, exist, emerge, remain, appear, proceed, expire, decline**
*The problem ~~was occurred~~ → **occurred**.* *Prices ~~were risen~~ → **rose**.*

## 3. Passive + preposition pairs that TOEIC loves

| be ___ in | be ___ with | be ___ to | be ___ for | be ___ of / on / about |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| interested in, involved in, engaged in | satisfied with, pleased with, equipped with, associated with, faced with | committed to, dedicated to, devoted to, entitled to, exposed to, related to, scheduled to | known for, responsible for, eligible for, designed for | aware of, composed of, based on, concerned about |

## 4. Passive with two objects / reporting verbs

- *Employees **will be given** a bonus.* (give someone something)
- *The CEO **is expected to** announce the merger.*
- *It **is reported** that sales fell.*

## 5. TOEIC-style question

> The annual awards ceremony ------- at the Grand Hotel next month.
> (A) holds  (B) will hold  (C) will be held  (D) is holding

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C) will be held.** There is no object after the blank, and a ceremony *is held* by someone, so **passive**. *Next month* means future.
</details>

## 6. Practice

1. All employees ------- to attend the safety workshop.
   (A) require (B) are required (C) requiring (D) have required
2. Several problems ------- during the system upgrade.
   (A) occurred (B) were occurred (C) have been occurred (D) occurring
3. The marketing department ------- a new advertising campaign last week.
   (A) launched (B) was launched (C) has been launched (D) launching

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **(B)**: employees *are required to* (passive, no object).
2. **(A)**: *occur* is intransitive and can never be passive.
3. **(A)**: there is an object (*a new advertising campaign*), so active; *last week* means past.
</details>
