# 08 — Marketing & Advertising

### 1. marketing
- **IPA:** /ˈmɑːkɪtɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The activity of promoting and selling products or services.
  - *Example:* The **marketing** team is planning a spring [campaign](08-marketing-and-advertising.md#3-campaign).

### 2. advertisement
- **IPA:** /ədˈvɜːtɪsmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A notice or short film that promotes a product, service, or job.
  - *Example:* They placed an **advertisement** in the local paper.

### 3. campaign
- **IPA:** /kæmˈpeɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A planned set of activities to achieve a goal, such as selling a product.
  - *Example:* The ad **campaign** ran for six weeks.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To work in an organised way toward a goal.
  - *Example:* The group is **campaigning** for cleaner packaging.

### 4. brand
- **IPA:** /brænd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A name or design that identifies a company's products.
  - *Example:* It is now the best-known **brand** in the region.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To give a product a particular name and image.
  - *Example:* The drink was **branded** as a healthy option.

### 5. target market
- **IPA:** /ˈtɑːɡɪt ˌmɑːkɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The specific group of people a product is aimed at.
  - *Example:* Our **target market** is city workers aged 25–40.

### 6. consumer
- **IPA:** /kənˈsjuːmə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who buys goods or services for their own use.
  - *Example:* **Consumers** are choosing cheaper brands this year.

### 7. promote
- **IPA:** /prəˈməʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To try to increase sales or public awareness of something.
  - *Example:* [Influencers](../toeic-3000/24-advertising-pr-and-media.md#influencer) were paid to **promote** the app.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To move someone to a higher rank or job.
  - *Example:* He was **promoted** to sales director.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To support or encourage something to develop.
  - *Example:* The scheme **promotes** [recycling](../toeic-3000/28-environment-and-sustainability.md#recycling) in offices.

### 8. slogan
- **IPA:** /ˈsləʊɡən/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A short, memorable phrase used in advertising.
  - *Example:* The new **slogan** is printed on every package.

### 9. launch
- **IPA:** /lɔːntʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To introduce a new product or service to the public.
  - *Example:* The company will **launch** the phone in October.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The occasion when something new is made available.
  - *Example:* The product **launch** attracted a lot of press.

### 10. survey
- **IPA:** /ˈsɜːveɪ/ (n) · /səˈveɪ/ (v)
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A set of questions used to find out people's opinions or habits.
  - *Example:* The customer **survey** showed high [satisfaction](20-customer-service-and-complaints.md#12-satisfaction).
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To ask people questions to gather information.
  - *Example:* We **surveyed** 500 [shoppers](../toeic-3000/35-shopping-and-consumer-life.md#shopper) about the new layout.

### 11. demographic
- **IPA:** /ˌdeməˈɡræfɪk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A group of people with shared characteristics such as age or income.
  - *Example:* The [magazine](../toeic-3000/32-media-news-and-publishing.md#magazine) appeals to a young, urban **demographic**.

### 12. endorsement
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈdɔːsmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A public statement of support, often by a famous person, for a product.
  - *Example:* The athlete's **endorsement** boosted sales sharply.

### 13. outlet
- **IPA:** /ˈaʊtlet/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A shop or other place where goods are sold.
  - *Example:* The brand now has 40 retail **outlets**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A way of expressing or releasing feelings or energy.
  - *Example:* Sport is a good **outlet** for stress.

### 14. publicity
- **IPA:** /pʌbˈlɪsəti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Public attention given to someone or something through the media.
  - *Example:* The recall generated a lot of bad **publicity**.

### 15. branding
- **IPA:** /ˈbrændɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The work of giving a product a distinct name, look, and image.
  - *Example:* Consistent **branding** across every store built customer trust.

### 16. discount
- **IPA:** /ˈdɪskaʊnt/ (n) · /dɪsˈkaʊnt/ (v)
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A reduction in the usual price.
  - *Example:* Members get a 15% **discount** on all items.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To reduce the price of something.
  - *Example:* Last season's stock is heavily **discounted**.

### 17. loyalty
- **IPA:** /ˈlɔɪəlti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The quality of continuing to support a brand or person.
  - *Example:* The **loyalty** card gives points on every [purchase](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#1-purchase).

### 18. billboard
- **IPA:** /ˈbɪlbɔːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large outdoor board used for advertising.
  - *Example:* A huge **billboard** for the film stood by the motorway.

### 19. word of mouth
- **IPA:** /ˌwɜːd əv ˈmaʊθ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The passing of information or recommendations from person to person.
  - *Example:* The restaurant grew mostly by **word of mouth**.

### 20. market share
- **IPA:** /ˈmɑːkɪt ʃeə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The portion of total sales in a market held by one company.
  - *Example:* The firm gained two points of **market share** last year.

### 21. testimonial
- **IPA:** /ˌtestɪˈməʊniəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written or spoken statement praising a product or person.
  - *Example:* The website [features](../toeic-3000/32-media-news-and-publishing.md#feature) **testimonials** from happy clients.

### 22. circulation
- **IPA:** /ˌsɜːkjəˈleɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The number of copies of a newspaper or magazine sold.
  - *Example:* The trade [journal](../toeic-3000/27-science-and-research.md#journal) has a **circulation** of 50,000.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The movement of something around a system or group.
  - *Example:* The memo is in **circulation** among all managers.

### 23. exposure
- **IPA:** /ɪkˈspəʊʒə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of public attention something receives.
  - *Example:* [Sponsoring](08-marketing-and-advertising.md#28-sponsor) the event gave the brand wide **exposure**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The state of being unprotected from something harmful.
  - *Example:* Long **exposure** to sunlight [damages](../toeic-3000/14-law-and-compliance.md#damages) the packaging.

### 24. niche
- **IPA:** /niːʃ/ or /nɪtʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small, specialised part of a market.
  - *Example:* They found a profitable **niche** in [eco-friendly](../toeic-3000/28-environment-and-sustainability.md#eco-friendly) toys.

### 25. rollout
- **IPA:** /ˈrəʊlaʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The official introduction of a product or service, often in stages.
  - *Example:* The national **rollout** begins next month.

### 26. tagline
- **IPA:** /ˈtæɡlaɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A short phrase that sums up a brand or product.
  - *Example:* "Built to last" is the company's **tagline**.

### 27. prospect
- **IPA:** /ˈprɒspekt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A potential customer.
  - *Example:* The sales team has a list of 30 warm **prospects**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The possibility that something will happen.
  - *Example:* There is little **prospect** of prices falling soon.

### 28. sponsor
- **IPA:** /ˈspɒnsə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A company that pays for an event in return for advertising.
  - *Example:* A car maker is the main **sponsor** of the race.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To pay for an event or activity, usually for publicity.
  - *Example:* The bank **sponsors** a youth football league.

### 29. focus group
- **IPA:** /ˈfəʊkəs ɡruːp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small group brought together to give opinions on a product or idea.
  - *Example:* The **focus group** disliked the new logo.

### 30. viral
- **IPA:** /ˈvaɪrəl/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Spreading very quickly and widely on the internet.
  - *Example:* The ad went **viral** and reached millions overnight.
