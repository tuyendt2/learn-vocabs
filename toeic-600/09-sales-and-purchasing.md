# 09 — Sales & Purchasing

### 1. purchase
- **IPA:** /ˈpɜːtʃəs/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To buy something.
  - *Example:* The company **purchased** new [software](13-technology-and-computers.md#1-software) for the whole office.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Something that has been bought.
  - *Example:* Keep your [receipt](03-accounting-and-budgeting.md#5-receipt) to return a **purchase**.

### 2. supplier
- **IPA:** /səˈplaɪə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A company or person that provides goods or materials.
  - *Example:* We switched **suppliers** to get a better price.

### 3. vendor
- **IPA:** /ˈvendə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person or company that sells goods or services.
  - *Example:* Three **vendors** submitted bids for the contract.

### 4. quotation
- **IPA:** /kwəʊˈteɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A stated price for a job or goods before work is done.
  - *Example:* We asked for a **quotation** before agreeing to the repair.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A group of words taken from a text or speech.
  - *Example:* The [article](../toeic-3000/32-media-news-and-publishing.md#article) opens with a **quotation** from the CEO.

### 5. order
- **IPA:** /ˈɔːdə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A request for goods to be supplied.
  - *Example:* Your **order** will be shipped within two days.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To ask for goods or services to be supplied.
  - *Example:* We **ordered** 500 units for the new store.
- **Meaning 3 (n):** The arrangement of things in relation to each other.
  - *Example:* File the [invoices](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#6-invoice) in date **order**.

### 6. invoice
- **IPA:** /ˈɪnvɔɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A bill listing goods sold and the amount owed.
  - *Example:* Payment is due 30 days from the **invoice** date.

### 7. retail
- **IPA:** /ˈriːteɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The sale of goods to the public in small quantities.
  - *Example:* She has 15 years' [experience](04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#8-experience) in **retail**.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Relating to selling goods to the public.
  - *Example:* The **retail** price is twice the [wholesale](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#8-wholesale) price.

### 8. wholesale
- **IPA:** /ˈhəʊlseɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The sale of goods in large quantities, usually to shops.
  - *Example:* They buy at **wholesale** and sell online.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Done on a large scale.
  - *Example:* The plan calls for a **wholesale** change in [suppliers](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#2-supplier).

### 9. merchandise
- **IPA:** /ˈmɜːtʃəndaɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Goods that are bought and sold.
  - *Example:* Damaged **merchandise** should be set aside.

### 10. commission
- **IPA:** /kəˈmɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A payment based on the value of goods sold.
  - *Example:* Sales staff earn a 5% **commission** on each deal.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To formally ask for a piece of work to be done.
  - *Example:* The firm **commissioned** a report on office design.

### 11. negotiation
- **IPA:** /nɪˌɡəʊʃiˈeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Formal discussion aimed at reaching an agreement.
  - *Example:* Price **negotiations** with the [supplier](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#2-supplier) are ongoing.

### 12. bargain
- **IPA:** /ˈbɑːɡɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something bought for less than its usual price.
  - *Example:* The floor model was a real **bargain**.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To discuss the terms or price of something.
  - *Example:* They **bargained** hard over the delivery schedule.

### 13. procurement
- **IPA:** /prəˈkjʊəmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of obtaining supplies or equipment for a business.
  - *Example:* The **procurement** [department](../toeic-3000/31-government-and-public-services.md#department) handles all large orders.

### 14. requisition
- **IPA:** /ˌrekwɪˈzɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official request for supplies or equipment.
  - *Example:* Submit a **requisition** form to order new laptops.

### 15. inventory
- **IPA:** /ˈɪnvəntri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The goods a business currently has in stock.
  - *Example:* We keep **inventory** low to save on storage.

### 16. restock
- **IPA:** /ˌriːˈstɒk/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To fill up a supply of goods again.
  - *Example:* Staff **restock** the shelves before opening.

### 17. price list
- **IPA:** /ˈpraɪs lɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A document showing the prices of all the goods a company sells.
  - *Example:* The [supplier](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#2-supplier) sent an updated **price list** for the new year.

### 18. markup
- **IPA:** /ˈmɑːkʌp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount added to the cost price to set the selling price.
  - *Example:* The [standard](12-quality-control-and-standards.md#3-standard) **markup** on accessories is 60%.

### 19. purchase order
- **IPA:** /ˈpɜːtʃəs ˌɔːdə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official document a buyer sends to a seller to request goods.
  - *Example:* No goods are shipped without a **purchase order**.

### 20. bulk
- **IPA:** /bʌlk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large quantity or mass.
  - *Example:* Buying in **bulk** lowers the unit cost.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The main or largest part of something.
  - *Example:* The **bulk** of our sales come from repeat customers.

### 21. clearance
- **IPA:** /ˈklɪərəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sale to get rid of old stock at low prices.
  - *Example:* Winter coats are on the **clearance** rack.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Official permission for something to happen.
  - *Example:* The [shipment](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#1-shipment) is waiting for [customs](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#13-customs) **clearance**.

### 22. credit terms
- **IPA:** /ˈkredɪt tɜːmz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The conditions on which a buyer is allowed to pay later, such as "30 days".
  - *Example:* New customers are offered 14-day **credit terms** at first.

### 23. exchange
- **IPA:** /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To give one thing and receive another in return.
  - *Example:* Can I **exchange** this shirt for a larger size?
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The act of giving and receiving things of the same type.
  - *Example:* We offer an **exchange** but not a cash refund.

### 24. outstanding
- **IPA:** /aʊtˈstændɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Not yet paid or dealt with.
  - *Example:* There is still an **outstanding** balance on the account.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Extremely good; excellent.
  - *Example:* The team's sales results were **outstanding**.

### 25. lead time
- **IPA:** /ˈliːd taɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The time between placing an order and receiving the goods.
  - *Example:* The **lead time** for custom parts is six weeks.

### 26. bid
- **IPA:** /bɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An offer to do work or supply goods for a stated price.
  - *Example:* Their **bid** was the lowest of the four.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To offer a price for something, especially at an auction or tender.
  - *Example:* Five firms **bid** for the cleaning contract.

### 27. tender
- **IPA:** /ˈtendə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A formal written offer to supply goods or do work at a fixed price.
  - *Example:* The [council](../toeic-3000/31-government-and-public-services.md#council) invited **tenders** for the new bridge.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To make a formal offer to do work or supply goods.
  - *Example:* Three companies **tendered** for the project.

### 28. warranty claim
- **IPA:** /ˈwɒrənti kleɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A request for free repair or replacement under a warranty.
  - *Example:* The **warranty claim** was [approved](../toeic-3000/03-management-and-leadership.md#approve) within a week.

### 29. stock up
- **IPA:** /ˌstɒk ˈʌp/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To buy a large amount of something for future use.
  - *Example:* Stores **stock up** on gifts before the holidays.

### 30. sales representative
- **IPA:** /ˈseɪlz ˌreprɪˈzentətɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person whose job is to sell a company's products, often by visiting customers.
  - *Example:* A **sales representative** will call you to arrange a demo.
