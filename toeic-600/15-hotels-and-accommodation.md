# 15 — Hotels & Accommodation

### 1. accommodation
- **IPA:** /əˌkɒməˈdeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A place where someone lives or stays.
  - *Example:* The hotel offers **accommodation** for up to 300 guests.

### 2. reception desk
- **IPA:** /rɪˈsepʃn desk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The place in a hotel where guests check in and get help.
  - *Example:* Leave your key at the **reception desk** when you go out.

### 3. concierge
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒnsiˈeəʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A hotel employee who helps guests with bookings, tickets, and advice.
  - *Example:* The **concierge** arranged a taxi to the airport.

### 4. vacancy
- **IPA:** /ˈveɪkənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A room that is available in a hotel.
  - *Example:* They had one **vacancy** left for the weekend.

### 5. check-out
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃekaʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The time by which guests must leave their room.
  - *Example:* **Check-out** is at 11 a.m.

### 6. amenities
- **IPA:** /əˈmiːnətiz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Useful or pleasant features and services in a place.
  - *Example:* The room **amenities** include a safe and a coffee machine.

### 7. suite
- **IPA:** /swiːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A set of connected rooms in a hotel, usually with a sitting room.
  - *Example:* The manager upgraded us to a **suite**.

### 8. housekeeping
- **IPA:** /ˈhaʊskiːpɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The hotel department responsible for cleaning rooms.
  - *Example:* Call **housekeeping** if you need extra towels.

### 9. complimentary
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒmplɪˈmentri/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Given free of charge.
  - *Example:* Breakfast is **complimentary** for all guests.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Expressing praise.
  - *Example:* The reviews were highly **complimentary** about the staff.

### 10. occupancy
- **IPA:** /ˈɒkjəpənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The proportion of rooms that are being used.
  - *Example:* Summer **occupancy** reached 95%.

### 11. rate
- **IPA:** /reɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fixed price paid for a service.
  - *Example:* The corporate **rate** is lower than the walk-in price.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The speed or frequency at which something happens.
  - *Example:* The cancellation **rate** rose during the storm.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To judge the quality or standard of something.
  - *Example:* Guests **rated** the hotel four stars.

### 12. lobby
- **IPA:** /ˈlɒbi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The large open area near the entrance of a hotel.
  - *Example:* I'll wait for you in the **lobby** at eight.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To try to persuade officials to support a cause.
  - *Example:* Hotel owners **lobbied** against the new tax.

### 13. front desk
- **IPA:** /ˌfrʌnt ˈdesk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The main service counter in a hotel or office.
  - *Example:* Any messages will be left at the **front desk**.

### 14. bellhop
- **IPA:** /ˈbelhɒp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A hotel employee who carries guests' luggage.
  - *Example:* The **bellhop** took our bags up to the room.

### 15. room service
- **IPA:** /ˈruːm ˌsɜːvɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A hotel service that brings food and drink to guests' rooms.
  - *Example:* We ordered **room service** after the late meeting.

### 16. booking
- **IPA:** /ˈbʊkɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement to have a room, seat, or table at a future time.
  - *Example:* Your **booking** is confirmed for three nights.

### 17. deposit
- **IPA:** /dɪˈpɒzɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sum of money paid in advance and returned later if nothing is damaged.
  - *Example:* A $50 **deposit** is charged at check-in.

### 18. no-show
- **IPA:** /ˈnəʊ ʃəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who has a booking but does not arrive.
  - *Example:* **No-shows** are charged for the first night.

### 19. upgrade
- **IPA:** /ˈʌpɡreɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A move to a better room or class of service.
  - *Example:* Loyalty members often get a free **upgrade**.

### 20. checkout time
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃekaʊt taɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The latest time a guest may stay in the room without extra charge.
  - *Example:* We asked for a late **checkout time** of 2 p.m.

### 21. en suite
- **IPA:** /ˌɒn ˈswiːt/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** (of a bathroom) Joined directly to a bedroom.
  - *Example:* All rooms have an **en suite** bathroom.

### 22. porter
- **IPA:** /ˈpɔːtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person employed to carry luggage or do general tasks in a hotel.
  - *Example:* The **porter** showed us to our room.

### 23. hospitality
- **IPA:** /ˌhɒspɪˈtæləti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Friendly and generous treatment of guests.
  - *Example:* We thanked our hosts for their **hospitality**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The business of providing food, drink, and lodging.
  - *Example:* She has worked in **hospitality** for twenty years.

### 24. rate plan
- **IPA:** /ˈreɪt plæn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A set of prices and conditions offered for a room.
  - *Example:* The flexible **rate plan** allows free cancellation.

### 25. adjoining
- **IPA:** /əˈdʒɔɪnɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Next to and connected with something.
  - *Example:* The family booked two **adjoining** rooms.

### 26. front-of-house
- **IPA:** /ˌfrʌnt əv ˈhaʊs/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Relating to the parts of a hotel or restaurant that guests see.
  - *Example:* **Front-of-house** staff need strong people skills.

### 27. turndown service
- **IPA:** /ˈtɜːndaʊn ˌsɜːvɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An evening service in which staff prepare the bed for sleeping.
  - *Example:* **Turndown service** is offered between 6 and 8 p.m.

### 28. walk-in
- **IPA:** /ˈwɔːk ɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A guest who arrives without a reservation.
  - *Example:* We can usually fit in a few **walk-ins** on weekdays.

### 29. minibar
- **IPA:** /ˈmɪnibɑː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small fridge in a hotel room stocked with drinks and snacks for sale.
  - *Example:* Items taken from the **minibar** are added to your bill.

### 30. lodging
- **IPA:** /ˈlɒdʒɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A temporary place to stay.
  - *Example:* The grant covers travel and **lodging**.
