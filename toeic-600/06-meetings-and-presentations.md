# 06 — Meetings & Presentations

### 1. meeting
- **IPA:** /ˈmiːtɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An organised occasion when people come together to discuss something.
  - *Example:* The weekly team **meeting** starts at 9 a.m.

### 2. conference
- **IPA:** /ˈkɒnfərəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large formal event where people discuss a subject over one or more days.
  - *Example:* She gave a talk at the [marketing](08-marketing-and-advertising.md#1-marketing) **conference** in Berlin.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A private discussion between a small number of people.
  - *Example:* The lawyers are in **conference** with their client.

### 3. attendee
- **IPA:** /əˌtenˈdiː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who is present at an event.
  - *Example:* Each **attendee** received a printed programme.

### 4. presentation
- **IPA:** /ˌpreznˈteɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A talk in which an idea or product is shown and explained to an audience.
  - *Example:* His **presentation** on the new app impressed the board.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The way in which something is shown or arranged.
  - *Example:* Marks are given for content and **presentation**.

### 5. slide
- **IPA:** /slaɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A single page of a set of images shown on a screen during a talk.
  - *Example:* Go back to the **slide** with the sales figures.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To move smoothly along a surface.
  - *Example:* The door **slides** open automatically.

### 6. handout
- **IPA:** /ˈhændaʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A printed sheet given to people at a talk or class.
  - *Example:* There's a **handout** [summarising](06-meetings-and-presentations.md#10-summarise) the key points.

### 7. attendance
- **IPA:** /əˈtendəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The number of people present, or the act of being present.
  - *Example:* **Attendance** at the training [session](../toeic-3000/25-information-technology.md#session) is compulsory.

### 8. participant
- **IPA:** /pɑːˈtɪsɪpənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who takes part in an activity or event.
  - *Example:* **Participants** were split into groups of four.

### 9. brainstorm
- **IPA:** /ˈbreɪnstɔːm/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To think of many ideas quickly as a group before judging them.
  - *Example:* Let's **brainstorm** names for the new product.

### 10. summarise
- **IPA:** /ˈsʌməraɪz/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To give the main points of something in a short form.
  - *Example:* Could you **summarise** the report in one paragraph?

### 11. outline
- **IPA:** /ˈaʊtlaɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To describe the main features of something without full detail.
  - *Example:* She **outlined** the plan in five minutes.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A short description of the main points of something.
  - *Example:* Send me an **outline** of the [proposal](06-meetings-and-presentations.md#13-proposal) by Friday.
- **Meaning 3 (n):** The outer edge or shape of something.
  - *Example:* You could see the **outline** of the building in the fog.

### 12. objective
- **IPA:** /əbˈdʒektɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something you are trying to achieve; a goal.
  - *Example:* Our main **objective** this year is to enter the Asian market.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Based on facts rather than feelings or opinions.
  - *Example:* We need an **objective** [assessment](../toeic-3000/30-education-and-training.md#assessment) of the risks.

### 13. proposal
- **IPA:** /prəˈpəʊzl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A formal suggestion or plan put forward for consideration.
  - *Example:* The [committee](../toeic-3000/02-company-organization-and-departments.md#committee) rejected the **proposal** on cost grounds.

### 14. feedback
- **IPA:** /ˈfiːdbæk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Comments about how well or badly someone is doing something.
  - *Example:* Thank you for your **feedback** on the draft.

### 15. consensus
- **IPA:** /kənˈsensəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** General agreement among a group of people.
  - *Example:* The team reached a **consensus** after a long discussion.

### 16. chairperson
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃeəpɜːsn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The person who leads a meeting or committee.
  - *Example:* The **chairperson** called the [meeting](06-meetings-and-presentations.md#1-meeting) to order.

### 17. adjourn
- **IPA:** /əˈdʒɜːn/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To pause or end a meeting, to continue later.
  - *Example:* The [meeting](06-meetings-and-presentations.md#1-meeting) was **adjourned** until next Tuesday.

### 18. clarify
- **IPA:** /ˈklærɪfaɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To make something clearer or easier to understand.
  - *Example:* Could you **clarify** what you mean by "soon"?

### 19. venue
- **IPA:** /ˈvenjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The place where an event or meeting happens.
  - *Example:* We changed the **venue** because more people signed up.

### 20. keynote
- **IPA:** /ˈkiːnəʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The main speech at a conference, setting its central theme.
  - *Example:* A well-known economist gave the **keynote** address.

### 21. agenda item
- **IPA:** /əˈdʒendə ˌaɪtəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A single subject listed for discussion at a meeting.
  - *Example:* We'll deal with [staffing](04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#30-staffing) as the next **agenda item**.

### 22. minute-taker
- **IPA:** /ˈmɪnɪt ˌteɪkə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The person who writes the official record of a meeting.
  - *Example:* Priya volunteered to be the **minute-taker**.

### 23. quorum
- **IPA:** /ˈkwɔːrəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The smallest number of people needed for a meeting's decisions to be valid.
  - *Example:* Without a **quorum**, no vote can be taken.

### 24. recap
- **IPA:** /ˈriːkæp/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To repeat the main points of what has been said.
  - *Example:* Let me **recap** the three [action items](06-meetings-and-presentations.md#30-action-item).
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A short summary of what has been said.
  - *Example:* Here's a quick **recap** of last week's decisions.

### 25. visual aid
- **IPA:** /ˈvɪʒuəl eɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A picture, chart, or object used to help explain something.
  - *Example:* The chart was a helpful **visual aid** during the talk.

### 26. delegate (to)
- **IPA:** /ˈdelɪɡeɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To give a task or authority to someone else.
  - *Example:* The chair **delegated** the follow-up to the finance team.

### 27. postpone
- **IPA:** /pəˈspəʊn/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To arrange for something to happen at a later time than planned.
  - *Example:* The review [meeting](06-meetings-and-presentations.md#1-meeting) was **postponed** to next month.

### 28. address
- **IPA:** /əˈdres/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To deal with a problem or question.
  - *Example:* The report **addresses** the safety concerns directly.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To speak formally to a group of people.
  - *Example:* The director **addressed** the whole staff at 10 a.m.
- **Meaning 3 (n):** A formal speech given to an audience.
  - *Example:* Her closing **address** lasted twenty minutes.

### 29. participant list
- **IPA:** /pɑːˈtɪsɪpənt lɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written record of everyone taking part in a meeting or event.
  - *Example:* Add the two new hires to the **participant list**.

### 30. action item
- **IPA:** /ˈækʃn ˌaɪtəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A task that someone agrees to do after a meeting.
  - *Example:* Each **action item** has an owner and a [due date](../toeic-3000/06-projects-and-deadlines.md#due-date).
