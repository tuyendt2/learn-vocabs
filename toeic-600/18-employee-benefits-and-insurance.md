# 18 — Employee Benefits & Insurance

### 1. benefit
- **IPA:** /ˈbenɪfɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An advantage or payment given to employees in addition to wages.
  - *Example:* The job comes with excellent health **benefits**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A helpful or good effect.
  - *Example:* Flexible hours are a real **benefit** for parents.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To be helped by something; to gain an advantage.
  - *Example:* Everyone **benefits** from clearer instructions.

### 2. premium
- **IPA:** /ˈpriːmiəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An amount paid regularly for an insurance policy.
  - *Example:* The monthly **premium** rose by 8% this year.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Of high quality and usually higher price.
  - *Example:* The plan offers access to **premium** clinics.

### 3. coverage
- **IPA:** /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The protection or range of things included in an insurance policy.
  - *Example:* Dental care is not part of the basic **coverage**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The reporting of an event by news media.
  - *Example:* The merger got wide **coverage** in the press.

### 4. policy
- **IPA:** /ˈpɒləsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written contract of insurance.
  - *Example:* Read your **policy** to see what is excluded.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A plan or set of rules agreed by a business or government.
  - *Example:* Company **policy** allows two days of remote work.

### 5. claim
- **IPA:** /kleɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A formal request for payment under an insurance policy.
  - *Example:* File your **claim** within 30 days of the incident.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To ask for money that you have a right to.
  - *Example:* You can **claim** back the cost of your glasses.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To say that something is true without proof.
  - *Example:* The advert **claims** the product lasts twice as long.

### 6. deductible
- **IPA:** /dɪˈdʌktəbl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount you must pay yourself before insurance covers the rest.
  - *Example:* The plan has a $500 annual **deductible**.

### 7. pension
- **IPA:** /ˈpenʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Regular money paid to someone after they retire.
  - *Example:* The company matches your **pension** contributions.

### 8. entitlement
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈtaɪtlmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something you have an official right to receive.
  - *Example:* Your holiday **entitlement** is 25 days a year.

### 9. reimbursement
- **IPA:** /ˌriːɪmˈbɜːsmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The payment back of money that someone has spent.
  - *Example:* **Reimbursement** for tuition is available after one year of service.

### 10. compensation
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒmpenˈseɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money paid to someone for loss, injury, or suffering.
  - *Example:* He received **compensation** for the injury at work.

### 11. beneficiary
- **IPA:** /ˌbenɪˈfɪʃəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who receives money or benefits, especially from insurance or a will.
  - *Example:* Name a **beneficiary** on your life insurance form.

### 12. enrollment
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈrəʊlmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of officially joining a plan or programme.
  - *Example:* Open **enrollment** for health plans runs through November.

### 13. eligibility
- **IPA:** /ˌelɪdʒəˈbɪləti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The state of being allowed to have or do something.
  - *Example:* **Eligibility** for the bonus starts after six months.

### 14. waiting period
- **IPA:** /ˈweɪtɪŋ ˌpɪəriəd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A set time before benefits or coverage begin.
  - *Example:* There is a 90-day **waiting period** for dental cover.

### 15. dependent
- **IPA:** /dɪˈpendənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person, such as a child, who relies on someone else for financial support.
  - *Example:* You can add **dependents** to your health plan.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Needing someone or something for support.
  - *Example:* The bonus is **dependent** on company profits.

### 16. co-payment
- **IPA:** /ˌkəʊ ˈpeɪmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fixed amount you pay for a service, with insurance covering the rest.
  - *Example:* There is a $20 **co-payment** for each visit.

### 17. gross pay
- **IPA:** /ˌɡrəʊs ˈpeɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Total pay before tax and other deductions.
  - *Example:* Your **gross pay** is listed at the top of the slip.

### 18. net pay
- **IPA:** /ˌnet ˈpeɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of pay left after all deductions.
  - *Example:* **Net pay** is what actually reaches your account.

### 19. allowance
- **IPA:** /əˈlaʊəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An amount of money given regularly for a particular purpose.
  - *Example:* Staff receive a travel **allowance** each month.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** An amount of something that is permitted.
  - *Example:* The baggage **allowance** is 23 kilograms.

### 20. vested
- **IPA:** /ˈvestɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** (of a right or benefit) Fully earned and belonging to you.
  - *Example:* Your pension is fully **vested** after five years.

### 21. maternity leave
- **IPA:** /məˈtɜːnəti liːv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Time off work allowed to a mother before and after childbirth.
  - *Example:* She is on **maternity leave** until March.

### 22. flextime
- **IPA:** /ˈflekstaɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A system that lets employees choose their working hours within limits.
  - *Example:* **Flextime** lets him start at seven and leave at three.

### 23. underwriter
- **IPA:** /ˈʌndəraɪtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person or company that assesses risk and issues insurance.
  - *Example:* The **underwriter** asked for a medical report.

### 24. exclusion
- **IPA:** /ɪkˈskluːʒn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something that a policy does not cover.
  - *Example:* Damage from flooding is a common **exclusion**.

### 25. renew
- **IPA:** /rɪˈnjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To extend the period of an agreement, licence, or policy.
  - *Example:* Remember to **renew** the policy before it lapses.

### 26. lapse
- **IPA:** /læps/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** (of a contract or right) To end because it was not renewed or used.
  - *Example:* The cover **lapsed** when the payment was missed.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A short failure in behaviour, memory, or concentration.
  - *Example:* A brief **lapse** in attention caused the error.

### 27. contribution
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒntrɪˈbjuːʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A regular payment made toward a pension or fund.
  - *Example:* Increase your **contribution** to lower your taxable income.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Something you give or do to help achieve something.
  - *Example:* Her **contribution** to the project was significant.

### 28. severance package
- **IPA:** /ˈsevərəns ˌpækɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The pay and benefits given to an employee who is let go.
  - *Example:* The **severance package** included six months' salary.

### 29. perk
- **IPA:** /pɜːk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An extra benefit given to employees beyond their pay.
  - *Example:* Free lunches are a popular **perk**.

### 30. liability insurance
- **IPA:** /ˌlaɪəˈbɪləti ɪnˌʃʊərəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Insurance that covers legal costs and damages if you are found responsible for harm.
  - *Example:* All contractors must carry **liability insurance**.
