# 20 — Customer Service & Complaints

### 1. complaint
- **IPA:** /kəmˈpleɪnt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A statement that you are not satisfied with something.
  - *Example:* We received a **complaint** about the late delivery.

### 2. refund
- **IPA:** /ˈriːfʌnd/ (n) · /rɪˈfʌnd/ (v)
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money paid back to a customer.
  - *Example:* She asked for a full **refund**.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To give money back to a customer.
  - *Example:* We will **refund** you within five working days.

### 3. inquiry
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈkwaɪəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A question asking for information.
  - *Example:* Direct all **inquiries** to the support team.

### 4. resolve
- **IPA:** /rɪˈzɒlv/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To find a solution to a problem or difficulty.
  - *Example:* The issue was **resolved** within a day.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To make a firm decision to do something.
  - *Example:* The team **resolved** to reply to every email within an hour.

### 5. dissatisfied
- **IPA:** /dɪsˈsætɪsfaɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Not pleased or happy with something.
  - *Example:* A **dissatisfied** customer rarely comes back.

### 6. warranty
- **IPA:** /ˈwɒrənti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A promise to repair or replace a product within a set time.
  - *Example:* The fault is covered by the **warranty**.

### 7. representative
- **IPA:** /ˌreprɪˈzentətɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who acts or speaks for a company, especially with customers.
  - *Example:* A customer service **representative** will call you back.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Typical of a particular group or thing.
  - *Example:* The sample is **representative** of our whole customer base.

### 8. apologize
- **IPA:** /əˈpɒlədʒaɪz/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To say sorry for something that has caused a problem.
  - *Example:* We **apologize** for the inconvenience caused by the delay.

### 9. feedback
- **IPA:** /ˈfiːdbæk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Information about how good or bad a product or service is.
  - *Example:* Customer **feedback** led to a redesign of the app.

### 10. escalate
- **IPA:** /ˈeskəleɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To pass a problem to someone more senior for action.
  - *Example:* If it isn't fixed today, **escalate** it to the manager.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To become or make more serious.
  - *Example:* A small delay **escalated** into a major dispute.

### 11. compensate
- **IPA:** /ˈkɒmpenseɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To give someone money or something else to make up for a loss or problem.
  - *Example:* The airline **compensated** passengers for the cancellation.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To balance an effect by an opposite one.
  - *Example:* Extra staff on weekends **compensate** for the higher demand.

### 12. satisfaction
- **IPA:** /ˌsætɪsˈfækʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The pleasant feeling you get when you are happy with something.
  - *Example:* Customer **satisfaction** scores rose after the change.

### 13. courteous
- **IPA:** /ˈkɜːtiəs/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Polite and considerate toward others.
  - *Example:* Staff must remain **courteous** even with angry callers.

### 14. helpline
- **IPA:** /ˈhelplaɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A telephone service that gives help or advice.
  - *Example:* The **helpline** is open from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.

### 15. exchange
- **IPA:** /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To replace one thing with another, especially in a shop.
  - *Example:* You may **exchange** the item for a different colour.

### 16. faulty
- **IPA:** /ˈfɔːlti/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Not working correctly because of a defect.
  - *Example:* The **faulty** charger was replaced free of charge.

### 17. reimburse
- **IPA:** /ˌriːɪmˈbɜːs/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To pay back money that someone has spent or lost.
  - *Example:* We will **reimburse** the return postage.

### 18. loyalty
- **IPA:** /ˈlɔɪəlti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The tendency of customers to keep buying from the same company.
  - *Example:* Fast responses build customer **loyalty**.

### 19. grievance
- **IPA:** /ˈɡriːvəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A real or imagined cause for complaint, especially unfair treatment.
  - *Example:* There is a formal process for raising a **grievance**.

### 20. acknowledge
- **IPA:** /əkˈnɒlɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To confirm that you have received or noticed something.
  - *Example:* We **acknowledge** every complaint within 24 hours.

### 21. rectify
- **IPA:** /ˈrektɪfaɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To correct something that is wrong.
  - *Example:* We sent a technician to **rectify** the problem.

### 22. goodwill
- **IPA:** /ˌɡʊdˈwɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A friendly, helpful attitude that builds good relationships.
  - *Example:* The free upgrade was a **goodwill** gesture.

### 23. patron
- **IPA:** /ˈpeɪtrən/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A regular customer of a shop, restaurant, or service.
  - *Example:* Long-standing **patrons** were sent a thank-you card.

### 24. turnaround
- **IPA:** /ˈtɜːnəraʊnd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The time needed to deal with something and return it.
  - *Example:* Our repair **turnaround** is three working days.

### 25. queue
- **IPA:** /kjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A line of people waiting for something.
  - *Example:* The phone **queue** was 20 minutes long.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To wait in a line.
  - *Example:* Customers **queued** outside before the sale.

### 26. inconvenience
- **IPA:** /ˌɪnkənˈviːniəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Trouble or difficulty that causes discomfort or delay.
  - *Example:* We are sorry for any **inconvenience** this causes.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To cause trouble or difficulty for someone.
  - *Example:* We don't want to **inconvenience** our regular customers.

### 27. prompt
- **IPA:** /prɒmpt/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Quick and without delay.
  - *Example:* Customers expect a **prompt** response to emails.

### 28. dispute
- **IPA:** /dɪˈspjuːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A disagreement or argument.
  - *Example:* A billing **dispute** was settled in the customer's favour.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To say that something is not true or valid.
  - *Example:* She **disputed** the amount on the final invoice.

### 29. courtesy
- **IPA:** /ˈkɜːtəsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Polite behaviour; a polite action or remark.
  - *Example:* As a **courtesy**, we call ahead before every visit.

### 30. retain
- **IPA:** /rɪˈteɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To keep something rather than lose it or give it up.
  - *Example:* Good service helps the company **retain** customers.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To continue to hold or contain something.
  - *Example:* Please **retain** your receipt as proof of purchase.
