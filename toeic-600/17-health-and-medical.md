# 17 — Health & Medical

### 1. waiting room
- **IPA:** /ˈweɪtɪŋ ruːm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A room where patients sit until the doctor is ready to see them.
  - *Example:* The **waiting room** was full, so we sat for half an hour.

### 2. symptom
- **IPA:** /ˈsɪmptəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sign that you have an illness.
  - *Example:* A headache can be a **symptom** of stress.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A sign that something is wrong in a wider situation.
  - *Example:* Rising [complaints](20-customer-service-and-complaints.md#1-complaint) are a **symptom** of poor training.

### 3. prescription
- **IPA:** /prɪˈskrɪpʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written order from a doctor for medicine.
  - *Example:* Take this **prescription** to the [pharmacy](17-health-and-medical.md#6-pharmacy).

### 4. diagnosis
- **IPA:** /ˌdaɪəɡˈnəʊsɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The identification of an illness after examining a patient.
  - *Example:* The **diagnosis** was a minor [infection](../toeic-3000/29-health-medicine-and-wellness.md#infection).

### 5. treatment
- **IPA:** /ˈtriːtmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Medical care given to a patient for an illness or injury.
  - *Example:* She is receiving **treatment** for a back injury.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A way of behaving toward or dealing with someone or something.
  - *Example:* The staff praised the fair **treatment** they received.

### 6. pharmacy
- **IPA:** /ˈfɑːməsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A shop or part of a shop where medicines are sold.
  - *Example:* The **pharmacy** is open until midnight.

### 7. clinic
- **IPA:** /ˈklɪnɪk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A place where people get medical treatment or advice, often for a specific need.
  - *Example:* The company runs a health **clinic** for its staff.

### 8. checkup
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃekʌp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A general medical examination to check a person's health.
  - *Example:* Employees get a free annual **checkup**.

### 9. insurance
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈʃʊərəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement where you pay money regularly and are paid if you are ill or have an accident.
  - *Example:* Health **insurance** covers most of the cost.

### 10. dose
- **IPA:** /dəʊs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A measured amount of medicine to be taken at one time.
  - *Example:* Do not take more than the stated **dose**.

### 11. physician
- **IPA:** /fɪˈzɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A doctor, especially one who is not a surgeon.
  - *Example:* Consult your **physician** before starting the programme.

### 12. recover
- **IPA:** /rɪˈkʌvə/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To become well again after an illness or injury.
  - *Example:* He is **recovering** well after the operation.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To get back something lost or taken.
  - *Example:* The firm **recovered** most of the stolen data.

### 13. injury
- **IPA:** /ˈɪndʒəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Physical harm to the body.
  - *Example:* Report any workplace **injury** to your [supervisor](04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#24-supervisor).

### 14. immunization
- **IPA:** /ˌɪmjunaɪˈzeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of protecting someone against a disease, usually by vaccination.
  - *Example:* **Immunization** records are required for the trip.

### 15. specialist
- **IPA:** /ˈspeʃəlɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A doctor who is an expert in one area of medicine.
  - *Example:* Your GP can refer you to a **specialist**.

### 16. chronic
- **IPA:** /ˈkrɒnɪk/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** (of an illness) Lasting a long time; not easily cured.
  - *Example:* She manages a **chronic** condition with daily [medication](17-health-and-medical.md#18-medication).
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** (of a problem) Continuing for a long time and hard to solve.
  - *Example:* The region has a **chronic** [shortage](../toeic-3000/11-economics-and-markets.md#shortage) of nurses.

### 17. outpatient
- **IPA:** /ˈaʊtpeɪʃnt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A patient who receives treatment without staying overnight in hospital.
  - *Example:* Most of these [procedures](../toeic-3000/27-science-and-research.md#procedure) are done on an **outpatient** basis.

### 18. medication
- **IPA:** /ˌmedɪˈkeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Medicine used to treat an illness.
  - *Example:* Keep your **medication** in your [carry-on](14-travel-and-transportation.md#7-carry-on) bag.

### 19. first aid
- **IPA:** /ˌfɜːst ˈeɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Basic medical help given before full treatment is available.
  - *Example:* Two staff members are trained in **first aid**.

### 20. sick leave
- **IPA:** /ˈsɪk liːv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Time away from work that is allowed because of illness.
  - *Example:* He is on **sick leave** for the rest of the week.

### 21. referral
- **IPA:** /rɪˈfɜːrəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of sending a patient to another doctor or service.
  - *Example:* You need a **referral** to see the heart [specialist](17-health-and-medical.md#15-specialist).

### 22. dizzy
- **IPA:** /ˈdɪzi/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Feeling that everything is spinning and that you might fall.
  - *Example:* She felt **dizzy** after standing up too fast.

### 23. nausea
- **IPA:** /ˈnɔːziə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The feeling that you are going to vomit.
  - *Example:* **Nausea** is a common [side effect](17-health-and-medical.md#27-side-effect) of the medicine.

### 24. rehabilitation
- **IPA:** /ˌriːəˌbɪlɪˈteɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of helping someone return to health or normal life.
  - *Example:* His **rehabilitation** included several weeks of [physiotherapy](../toeic-3000/29-health-medicine-and-wellness.md#physiotherapy).

### 25. contagious
- **IPA:** /kənˈteɪdʒəs/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** (of a disease) Able to be passed from one person to another by contact.
  - *Example:* Stay home while the illness is still **contagious**.

### 26. dosage
- **IPA:** /ˈdəʊsɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount and frequency of a medicine to be taken.
  - *Example:* The doctor reduced the **dosage** after a month.

### 27. side effect
- **IPA:** /ˈsaɪd ɪˌfekt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An unwanted effect of a medicine or treatment.
  - *Example:* Drowsiness is a possible **side effect**.

### 28. practitioner
- **IPA:** /prækˈtɪʃənə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who works in a profession such as medicine or law.
  - *Example:* Ask a qualified **practitioner** for advice.

### 29. wellness
- **IPA:** /ˈwelnəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The state of being in good health, especially as something you actively work at.
  - *Example:* The company launched a **wellness** programme with [gym membership](../toeic-3000/39-leisure-hobbies-and-sports.md#gym-membership).

### 30. discharge
- **IPA:** /dɪsˈtʃɑːdʒ/ (v) · /ˈdɪstʃɑːdʒ/ (n)
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To officially allow a patient to leave hospital.
  - *Example:* She was **discharged** two days after [surgery](../toeic-3000/29-health-medicine-and-wellness.md#surgery).
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To release or send out something, such as liquid or gas.
  - *Example:* The plant must not **discharge** waste into the river.
- **Meaning 3 (n):** The act of releasing someone or something.
  - *Example:* He was given his **discharge** papers in the morning.
