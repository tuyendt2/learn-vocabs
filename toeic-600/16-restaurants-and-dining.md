# 16 — Restaurants & Dining

### 1. table booking
- **IPA:** /ˈteɪbl ˌbʊkɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement to have a table kept for you at a restaurant.
  - *Example:* I made a **table booking** for four at eight o'clock.

### 2. menu
- **IPA:** /ˈmenjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A list of the food and drinks available in a restaurant.
  - *Example:* The lunch **menu** changes every week.

### 3. beverage
- **IPA:** /ˈbevərɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A drink, especially one other than water.
  - *Example:* The set meal includes a hot **beverage**.

### 4. appetizer
- **IPA:** /ˈæpɪtaɪzə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small dish served before the main course.
  - *Example:* We shared two **appetizers** while we waited.

### 5. entrée
- **IPA:** /ˈɒntreɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The main dish of a meal (in North American use).
  - *Example:* Each **entrée** comes with a side salad.

### 6. catering
- **IPA:** /ˈkeɪtərɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The business of providing food and drink for events.
  - *Example:* An outside firm handled the **catering** for the launch.

### 7. cuisine
- **IPA:** /kwɪˈziːn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A style of cooking, especially from a particular country or region.
  - *Example:* The new place serves modern Vietnamese **cuisine**.

### 8. gratuity
- **IPA:** /ɡrəˈtjuːəti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money given to a server for good service; a tip.
  - *Example:* A **gratuity** of 15% is added for large groups.

### 9. waiter
- **IPA:** /ˈweɪtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who brings food and drink to customers in a restaurant.
  - *Example:* The **waiter** recommended the fish.

### 10. side order
- **IPA:** /ˈsaɪd ˌɔːdə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small extra dish ordered to go with a main dish.
  - *Example:* He asked for a **side order** of fries with the burger.

### 11. reservation book
- **IPA:** /ˌrezəˈveɪʃn bʊk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The record a restaurant keeps of its bookings.
  - *Example:* The host checked the **reservation book** at the door.

### 12. seating
- **IPA:** /ˈsiːtɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The arrangement of places for people to sit.
  - *Example:* Outdoor **seating** is available in good weather.

### 13. portion
- **IPA:** /ˈpɔːʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of food served to one person.
  - *Example:* The **portions** here are very generous.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A part of something larger.
  - *Example:* A **portion** of the profits goes to charity.

### 14. dine
- **IPA:** /daɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To eat dinner, especially in a formal way.
  - *Example:* The [delegates](04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#26-delegate) **dined** at a restaurant near the hotel.

### 15. tab
- **IPA:** /tæb/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A record of what you owe for food and drink.
  - *Example:* Put the drinks on my **tab**.

### 16. specials
- **IPA:** /ˈspeʃlz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Dishes offered on a particular day in addition to the usual menu.
  - *Example:* Today's **specials** are written on the board.

### 17. bistro
- **IPA:** /ˈbiːstrəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small, informal restaurant serving moderately priced meals.
  - *Example:* The **bistro** on the corner is popular for business lunches.

### 18. buffet
- **IPA:** /ˈbʊfeɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A meal where people serve themselves from a range of dishes.
  - *Example:* The hotel puts on a breakfast **buffet** until ten.

### 19. host
- **IPA:** /həʊst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who greets and seats guests at a restaurant.
  - *Example:* The **host** led us to a table by the window.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To provide the space and arrangements for an event.
  - *Example:* The firm will **host** a dinner for its top clients.

### 20. dietary
- **IPA:** /ˈdaɪətəri/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Relating to the food a person eats.
  - *Example:* Please tell us about any **dietary** [requirements](../toeic-3000/06-projects-and-deadlines.md#requirement).

### 21. refreshments
- **IPA:** /rɪˈfreʃmənts/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Light food and drinks served at an event.
  - *Example:* **Refreshments** will be served during the break.

### 22. takeout
- **IPA:** /ˈteɪkaʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Food bought at a restaurant to eat somewhere else.
  - *Example:* We ordered **takeout** for the whole team.

### 23. banquet
- **IPA:** /ˈbæŋkwɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large formal meal for many people, often for a special occasion.
  - *Example:* The awards **banquet** seats 400 guests.

### 24. diner
- **IPA:** /ˈdaɪnə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who is eating a meal in a restaurant.
  - *Example:* Regular **diners** are offered a table by the window.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A small, informal restaurant.
  - *Example:* We stopped at a roadside **diner** for breakfast.

### 25. establishment
- **IPA:** /ɪˈstæblɪʃmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A business such as a restaurant, shop, or hotel.
  - *Example:* It is one of the oldest dining **establishments** in the city.

### 26. corkage
- **IPA:** /ˈkɔːkɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A charge for serving wine that customers bring themselves.
  - *Example:* The restaurant charges $15 **corkage** per bottle.

### 27. head chef
- **IPA:** /ˌhed ˈʃef/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The cook in charge of a restaurant kitchen.
  - *Example:* The **head chef** designs a new menu each season.

### 28. seasonal
- **IPA:** /ˈsiːzənl/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Available or happening only at a certain time of year.
  - *Example:* The menu uses **seasonal** vegetables.

### 29. cover charge
- **IPA:** /ˈkʌvə tʃɑːdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fixed amount added to each customer's bill, separate from the food.
  - *Example:* There is a small **cover charge** for the bread and [table service](../toeic-3000/38-food-cooking-and-dining-out.md#table-service).

### 30. service charge
- **IPA:** /ˈsɜːvɪs tʃɑːdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An amount added to a bill to pay for service.
  - *Example:* A 10% **service charge** is included in the total.
