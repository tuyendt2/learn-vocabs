# 12 — Quality Control & Standards

### 1. quality control
- **IPA:** /ˈkwɒləti kənˌtrəʊl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The system of checking that products meet a required standard.
  - *Example:* Every unit passes through **quality control** before shipping.

### 2. inspection
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈspekʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A careful examination to check for faults or compliance.
  - *Example:* The factory passed its safety **inspection**.

### 3. standard
- **IPA:** /ˈstændəd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A required or agreed level of quality.
  - *Example:* The parts must meet international **standards**.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Usual or normal; not special.
  - *Example:* This is the **standard** size for shipping boxes.

### 4. compliance
- **IPA:** /kəmˈplaɪəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of meeting rules, laws, or standards.
  - *Example:* The audit checks **compliance** with fire [regulations](../toeic-3000/14-law-and-compliance.md#regulation).

### 5. defective
- **IPA:** /dɪˈfektɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Having a fault; not working correctly.
  - *Example:* **Defective** items are returned to the [supplier](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#2-supplier).

### 6. flaw
- **IPA:** /flɔː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fault or weakness, especially one that is hard to see.
  - *Example:* A small **flaw** in the seal caused leaks.

### 7. tolerance
- **IPA:** /ˈtɒlərəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The allowed amount of variation in a measurement.
  - *Example:* The part must be within a **tolerance** of 0.1 mm.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The ability to accept behaviour or conditions you dislike.
  - *Example:* The company has zero **tolerance** for harassment.

### 8. quality manual
- **IPA:** /ˈkwɒləti ˌmænjuəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A document that sets out an organisation's quality rules and procedures.
  - *Example:* The **quality manual** describes how every [complaint](20-customer-service-and-complaints.md#1-complaint) must be recorded.

### 9. benchmark
- **IPA:** /ˈbentʃmɑːk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A standard point of reference against which things are measured.
  - *Example:* Their delivery speed is the industry **benchmark**.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To measure something against a standard.
  - *Example:* We **benchmark** our prices against three competitors.

### 10. certification
- **IPA:** /ˌsɜːtɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Official approval that something meets a standard.
  - *Example:* The plant gained ISO **certification** last year.

### 11. quality check
- **IPA:** /ˈkwɒləti tʃek/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An examination to make sure a product meets the required standard.
  - *Example:* Every unit gets a final **quality check** before it is packed.

### 12. reject
- **IPA:** /rɪˈdʒekt/ (v) · /ˈriːdʒekt/ (n)
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To refuse to accept something because it is not good enough.
  - *Example:* [Inspectors](../toeic-3000/15-taxation-and-regulation.md#inspector) **rejected** the whole batch.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A product that has been refused for not meeting the standard.
  - *Example:* **Rejects** are sold at a [discount](08-marketing-and-advertising.md#16-discount) or scrapped.

### 13. consistency
- **IPA:** /kənˈsɪstənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The quality of always being the same or behaving the same way.
  - *Example:* Customers value **consistency** in taste and size.

### 14. sample
- **IPA:** /ˈsɑːmpl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small part taken from a larger amount to be tested.
  - *Example:* A **sample** from each [shipment](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#1-shipment) is tested in the lab.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To take and examine a small amount of something.
  - *Example:* [Technicians](../toeic-3000/17-manufacturing-and-engineering.md#technician) **sample** the water every hour.

### 15. calibrate
- **IPA:** /ˈkælɪbreɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To set or adjust a measuring instrument so it is accurate.
  - *Example:* The scales are **calibrated** each morning.

### 16. durability
- **IPA:** /ˌdjʊərəˈbɪləti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The ability to last a long time without damage.
  - *Example:* The material was chosen for its **durability**.

### 17. assurance
- **IPA:** /əˈʃʊərəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A promise or guarantee that something is true or will happen.
  - *Example:* We were given an **assurance** that the fault was fixed.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Confidence in your own abilities.
  - *Example:* She answered every question with quiet **assurance**.

### 18. deviation
- **IPA:** /ˌdiːviˈeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A difference from what is normal, expected, or required.
  - *Example:* Any **deviation** from the recipe must be recorded.

### 19. verify
- **IPA:** /ˈverɪfaɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To check that something is true or accurate.
  - *Example:* Please **verify** the serial number before [sign-off](../toeic-3000/06-projects-and-deadlines.md#sign-off).

### 20. defect rate
- **IPA:** /ˈdiːfekt reɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The proportion of products that are faulty.
  - *Example:* The **defect rate** fell below 1% after training.

### 21. traceability
- **IPA:** /ˌtreɪsəˈbɪləti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The ability to follow the history of a product through production and supply.
  - *Example:* [Barcodes](../toeic-3000/16-retail-and-store-operations.md#barcode) give full **traceability** back to the [raw materials](10-manufacturing-and-production.md#6-raw-material).

### 22. non-conformance
- **IPA:** /ˌnɒn kənˈfɔːməns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A failure to meet a required standard or specification.
  - *Example:* Each **non-conformance** is logged and investigated.

### 23. rework
- **IPA:** /ˌriːˈwɜːk/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To do work again in order to correct faults.
  - *Example:* Faulty units are **reworked** rather than thrown away.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The process of correcting faulty work.
  - *Example:* Too much **rework** is slowing the line.

### 24. threshold
- **IPA:** /ˈθreʃhəʊld/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The level at which something starts to happen or take effect.
  - *Example:* If waste passes a set **threshold**, the line is stopped.

### 25. accreditation
- **IPA:** /əˌkredɪˈteɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Official recognition that an organisation meets required standards.
  - *Example:* The lab lost its **accreditation** after the review.

### 26. robust
- **IPA:** /rəʊˈbʌst/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Strong and able to withstand difficult conditions.
  - *Example:* The design proved **robust** under heavy use.

### 27. batch number
- **IPA:** /ˈbætʃ ˌnʌmbə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A code identifying a group of products made together.
  - *Example:* The recall affects one **batch number** only.

### 28. checklist
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃeklɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A list of items to be checked or done.
  - *Example:* [Operators](../toeic-3000/26-telecommunications-and-networks.md#operator) complete a start-up **checklist** each shift.

### 29. corrective action
- **IPA:** /kəˈrektɪv ˈækʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Steps taken to remove the cause of a fault or problem.
  - *Example:* The report recommends three **corrective actions**.

### 30. gauge
- **IPA:** /ɡeɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An instrument for measuring amount, size, or pressure.
  - *Example:* The pressure **gauge** showed a reading above the limit.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To judge or measure something.
  - *Example:* We ran a trial to **gauge** customer interest.
