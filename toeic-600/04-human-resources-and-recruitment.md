# 04 — Human Resources & Recruitment

### 1. recruit
- **IPA:** /rɪˈkruːt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To find and hire new people to work for an organisation.
  - *Example:* We are **recruiting** three engineers this [quarter](03-accounting-and-budgeting.md#12-quarter).
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A person who has recently joined an organisation.
  - *Example:* The new **recruits** start their training on Monday.

### 2. applicant
- **IPA:** /ˈæplɪkənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who formally asks for a job, place, or grant.
  - *Example:* We received over 200 **applicants** for the role.

### 3. candidate
- **IPA:** /ˈkændɪdət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person being considered for a job or position.
  - *Example:* Three **candidates** were invited to a second [interview](04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#6-interview).
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A person or thing likely to have or get a particular quality.
  - *Example:* This [department](../toeic-3000/31-government-and-public-services.md#department) is a strong **candidate** for extra funding.

### 4. résumé
- **IPA:** /ˈrezjumeɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A short written summary of your education and work experience.
  - *Example:* Attach your **résumé** and a [cover letter](04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#5-cover-letter).

### 5. cover letter
- **IPA:** /ˈkʌvə ˌletə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A letter sent with a résumé to explain your interest in a job.
  - *Example:* Her **cover letter** clearly matched her skills to the role.

### 6. interview
- **IPA:** /ˈɪntəvjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A formal meeting where someone is asked questions to see if they are suitable for a job.
  - *Example:* The **interview** lasted about 45 minutes.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To ask someone questions in a formal meeting.
  - *Example:* The panel will **interview** [candidates](04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#3-candidate) all week.

### 7. qualification
- **IPA:** /ˌkwɒlɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A skill, quality, or exam pass that makes you suitable for a job.
  - *Example:* The post requires a **qualification** in accounting.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A limit or condition added to a statement.
  - *Example:* I can recommend him without **qualification**.

### 8. experience
- **IPA:** /ɪkˈspɪəriəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Skill or knowledge gained by doing a job over time.
  - *Example:* She has ten years' **experience** in [logistics](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#7-logistics).
- **Meaning 2 (n):** An event or activity that affects you.
  - *Example:* Working abroad was a valuable **experience**.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To feel or go through something.
  - *Example:* The company **experienced** rapid growth after 2020.

### 9. reference
- **IPA:** /ˈrefrəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A statement from a former employer about your character or ability.
  - *Example:* Please provide two professional **references**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A mention of something, or a source pointed to.
  - *Example:* The report makes several **references** to the 2023 study.

### 10. hire
- **IPA:** /ˈhaɪə/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To give someone a job.
  - *Example:* They **hired** her as head of design.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To pay to use something for a short time.
  - *Example:* We **hired** a van for the move.

### 11. onboarding
- **IPA:** /ˈɒnbɔːdɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of helping a new employee settle into a job.
  - *Example:* **Onboarding** now includes a week of shadowing a [colleague](../toeic-3000/04-teamwork-and-workplace-relationships.md#colleague).

### 12. probation
- **IPA:** /prəˈbeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A trial period at the start of a job during which performance is watched.
  - *Example:* You are on six months' **probation** before the contract is confirmed.

### 13. promotion
- **IPA:** /prəˈməʊʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A move to a more senior job or rank.
  - *Example:* She earned a **promotion** to regional manager.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Activity to increase sales or public awareness of a product.
  - *Example:* The **promotion** offers 20% off for new customers.

### 14. resignation
- **IPA:** /ˌrezɪɡˈneɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of formally leaving a job.
  - *Example:* He handed in his **resignation** on Friday.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The acceptance of something unpleasant that cannot be changed.
  - *Example:* She accepted the extra work with **resignation**.

### 15. dismissal
- **IPA:** /dɪsˈmɪsl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of removing someone from their job.
  - *Example:* Repeated lateness can lead to **dismissal**.

### 16. layoff
- **IPA:** /ˈleɪɒf/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The ending of a worker's job, usually because there is not enough work.
  - *Example:* The factory announced 300 **layoffs**.

### 17. severance
- **IPA:** /ˈsevərəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money paid to an employee whose job is ended by the employer.
  - *Example:* Staff were offered three months' **severance** pay.

### 18. vacancy
- **IPA:** /ˈveɪkənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A job that is available for someone to take.
  - *Example:* There is a **vacancy** in the accounts [department](../toeic-3000/31-government-and-public-services.md#department).
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A room available in a hotel.
  - *Example:* The sign said "No **Vacancies**."

### 19. compensation
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒmpenˈseɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The total pay and benefits an employee receives.
  - *Example:* The **compensation** package includes stock [options](../toeic-3000/12-banking-and-investment.md#options).
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Money paid to make up for loss, injury, or damage.
  - *Example:* She received **compensation** for the delayed flight.

### 20. appraisal
- **IPA:** /əˈpreɪzl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A formal review of an employee's work performance.
  - *Example:* Annual **appraisals** take place every January.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A judgement of the value or quality of something.
  - *Example:* The bank ordered an **appraisal** of the [property](19-real-estate-and-property.md#1-property).

### 21. workforce
- **IPA:** /ˈwɜːkfɔːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** All the people who work for a company or in a country.
  - *Example:* Half the **workforce** is now based overseas.

### 22. retention
- **IPA:** /rɪˈtenʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The ability of a company to keep its employees or customers.
  - *Example:* Better [benefits](18-employee-benefits-and-insurance.md#1-benefit) improved staff **retention**.

### 23. personnel
- **IPA:** /ˌpɜːsəˈnel/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The people employed by an organisation.
  - *Example:* All **personnel** must complete safety training.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The department that deals with employees (older term for HR).
  - *Example:* Contact **personnel** about your leave request.

### 24. supervisor
- **IPA:** /ˈsuːpəvaɪzə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who watches over and directs the work of others.
  - *Example:* Report any faults to your shift **supervisor**.

### 25. subordinate
- **IPA:** /səˈbɔːdɪnət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who is lower in rank and under someone's authority.
  - *Example:* A good manager listens to their **subordinates**.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** Lower in rank or less important.
  - *Example:* Safety concerns should never be **subordinate** to speed.

### 26. delegate
- **IPA:** /ˈdelɪɡət/ (n) · /ˈdelɪɡeɪt/ (v)
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To give a task or responsibility to someone else.
  - *Example:* Learn to **delegate** routine work to junior staff.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A person sent to represent a group at a meeting.
  - *Example:* Each office sent two **delegates** to the [conference](06-meetings-and-presentations.md#2-conference).

### 27. incentive
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈsentɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something that encourages a person to do something.
  - *Example:* Bonuses are an **incentive** to hit sales targets.

### 28. workload
- **IPA:** /ˈwɜːkləʊd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of work a person or team is expected to do.
  - *Example:* Her **workload** doubled after two [colleagues](../toeic-3000/04-teamwork-and-workplace-relationships.md#colleague) left.

### 29. job description
- **IPA:** /ˈdʒɒb dɪˌskrɪpʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written statement of the duties and requirements of a job.
  - *Example:* The **job description** lists five main responsibilities.

### 30. staffing
- **IPA:** /ˈstɑːfɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The provision of workers for an organisation; the level of workers available.
  - *Example:* Weekend **staffing** is too low to handle demand.
