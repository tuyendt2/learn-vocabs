# 02 — Finance & Banking

### 1. account
- **IPA:** /əˈkaʊnt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement with a bank to keep and use your money.
  - *Example:* She opened a savings **account** at the local branch.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A written or spoken report of events.
  - *Example:* He gave a full **account** of what happened at the meeting.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To be the explanation or cause of something (*account for*).
  - *Example:* Bad weather **accounts** for the drop in sales.

### 2. deposit
- **IPA:** /dɪˈpɒzɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sum of money paid into a bank account.
  - *Example:* I made a **deposit** of $500 this morning.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A first payment given to reserve something, with the rest paid later.
  - *Example:* They paid a 10% **deposit** on the apartment.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To put money into a bank account.
  - *Example:* Your salary is **deposited** on the last day of the month.

### 3. withdraw
- **IPA:** /wɪðˈdrɔː/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To take money out of a bank account.
  - *Example:* You can **withdraw** cash from any of our machines.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To remove something, or to stop taking part.
  - *Example:* The company **withdrew** the product after complaints.

### 4. balance
- **IPA:** /ˈbæləns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of money in an account.
  - *Example:* Please check your **balance** before making a transfer.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The amount still owed after part of a debt is paid.
  - *Example:* The **balance** of the invoice is due next week.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To keep or put something in a steady, even state.
  - *Example:* Managers must **balance** cost against quality.

### 5. interest
- **IPA:** /ˈɪntrəst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money charged for borrowing, or paid for saving.
  - *Example:* The loan carries an **interest** rate of 6%.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A feeling of wanting to know about or take part in something.
  - *Example:* She showed great **interest** in the new project.
- **Meaning 3 (n):** A share or legal right in a business or property.
  - *Example:* He sold his **interest** in the company last year.

### 6. loan
- **IPA:** /ləʊn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sum of money that is borrowed and must be paid back.
  - *Example:* They took out a **loan** to buy new equipment.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To lend something, especially money.
  - *Example:* The bank **loaned** the firm $50,000.

### 7. mortgage
- **IPA:** /ˈmɔːɡɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A long-term loan used to buy property, with the property as security.
  - *Example:* Their monthly **mortgage** payment went up this year.

### 8. credit
- **IPA:** /ˈkredɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement to buy now and pay later.
  - *Example:* The store offers six months of interest-free **credit**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Money added to an account, or a sum in your favour.
  - *Example:* A **credit** of $20 was applied to your bill.
- **Meaning 3 (n):** Public praise or recognition for something done.
  - *Example:* She deserves **credit** for turning the branch around.

### 9. debit
- **IPA:** /ˈdebɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sum taken out of a bank account.
  - *Example:* The rent is paid by direct **debit** each month.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To take money out of an account.
  - *Example:* The fee will be **debited** from your account on the 1st.

### 10. transaction
- **IPA:** /trænˈzækʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An instance of buying or selling, or of moving money.
  - *Example:* Every **transaction** over $10,000 must be reported.

### 11. transfer
- **IPA:** /ˈtrænsfɜː/ (n) · /trænsˈfɜː/ (v)
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The movement of money from one account to another.
  - *Example:* The bank **transfer** should clear within two days.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To move someone or something from one place to another.
  - *Example:* She was **transferred** to the Singapore office.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To move money between accounts.
  - *Example:* Please **transfer** the funds to our supplier by Friday.

### 12. currency
- **IPA:** /ˈkʌrənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The system of money used in a particular country.
  - *Example:* The euro is the **currency** of most EU countries.

### 13. exchange rate
- **IPA:** /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ reɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The value of one currency compared with another.
  - *Example:* A weak **exchange rate** makes exports cheaper abroad.

### 14. investment
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈvestmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money put into a business or asset to earn a return.
  - *Example:* The new plant is a long-term **investment**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The act of spending time or effort on something worthwhile.
  - *Example:* Training staff is an **investment** in the future.

### 15. dividend
- **IPA:** /ˈdɪvɪdend/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A share of a company's profit paid to shareholders.
  - *Example:* The board raised the annual **dividend** by 5%.

### 16. asset
- **IPA:** /ˈæset/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something valuable that a person or company owns.
  - *Example:* The building is the firm's most valuable **asset**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A useful or valuable quality, skill, or person.
  - *Example:* Her language skills are a real **asset** to the team.

### 17. capital
- **IPA:** /ˈkæpɪtl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money or property used to start or run a business.
  - *Example:* They need more **capital** to expand overseas.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The most important city of a country or region.
  - *Example:* The head office moved to the **capital**.
- **Meaning 3 (adj):** Referring to a large letter (A, B, C…).
  - *Example:* Write your name in **capital** letters.

### 18. fund
- **IPA:** /fʌnd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sum of money saved or set aside for a purpose.
  - *Example:* The company set up a **fund** for staff emergencies.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To provide money for something.
  - *Example:* The research is **funded** by the government.

### 19. revenue
- **IPA:** /ˈrevənjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The total income a business receives from its activities.
  - *Example:* Annual **revenue** rose to $4 million.

### 20. expense
- **IPA:** /ɪkˈspens/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The cost of something, or money spent on it.
  - *Example:* Travel is our biggest **expense** this quarter.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Money spent for work that the employer pays back (usually plural).
  - *Example:* Submit your **expenses** with receipts attached.

### 21. profit
- **IPA:** /ˈprɒfɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money gained after costs are subtracted from income.
  - *Example:* The store made a small **profit** in its first year.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To gain a benefit or advantage from something.
  - *Example:* We can all **profit** from the training.

### 22. loss
- **IPA:** /lɒs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount by which costs are greater than income.
  - *Example:* The division reported a **loss** of $200,000.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The fact of no longer having something, or having less of it.
  - *Example:* The **loss** of two key clients hurt the business.

### 23. debt
- **IPA:** /det/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money that is owed to someone.
  - *Example:* The company is struggling to pay off its **debt**.

### 24. bankruptcy
- **IPA:** /ˈbæŋkrʌptsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The legal state of being unable to pay your debts.
  - *Example:* The airline filed for **bankruptcy** last month.

### 25. collateral
- **IPA:** /kəˈlætərəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Property promised to a lender that can be taken if a loan is not repaid.
  - *Example:* They used their warehouse as **collateral** for the loan.

### 26. installment
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈstɔːlmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of several regular payments made to pay off a debt.
  - *Example:* You can pay in twelve monthly **installments**.

### 27. statement
- **IPA:** /ˈsteɪtmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A printed record of all transactions on an account over a period.
  - *Example:* Your bank **statement** is available online each month.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Something said or written formally and clearly.
  - *Example:* The CEO issued a **statement** about the merger.

### 28. overdraft
- **IPA:** /ˈəʊvədrɑːft/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement that lets you spend more than the money in your account.
  - *Example:* The bank agreed to a £1,000 **overdraft**.

### 29. remittance
- **IPA:** /rɪˈmɪtəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sum of money sent as payment, often to another country.
  - *Example:* Please enclose a **remittance** for the full amount.

### 30. audit
- **IPA:** /ˈɔːdɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official examination of financial records.
  - *Example:* The annual **audit** found no serious problems.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To officially inspect an organisation's accounts.
  - *Example:* An outside firm **audits** our books every year.
