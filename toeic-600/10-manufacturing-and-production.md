# 10 — Manufacturing & Production

### 1. manufacture
- **IPA:** /ˌmænjuˈfæktʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To make goods in large quantities using machines.
  - *Example:* The plant **manufactures** car parts for export.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The process of making goods on a large scale.
  - *Example:* The **manufacture** of the new model starts in June.

### 2. assembly line
- **IPA:** /əˈsembli laɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A line of workers and machines that put together a product step by step.
  - *Example:* A fault on the **assembly line** halted [production](10-manufacturing-and-production.md#3-production) for an hour.

### 3. production
- **IPA:** /prəˈdʌkʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of making or growing things to be sold.
  - *Example:* **Production** rose by 12% after the [upgrade](13-technology-and-computers.md#4-upgrade).
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The amount of something that is made.
  - *Example:* Daily **production** is around 4,000 units.

### 4. output
- **IPA:** /ˈaʊtpʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of something produced by a person, machine, or factory.
  - *Example:* The new machine doubled the plant's **output**.

### 5. capacity
- **IPA:** /kəˈpæsəti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The largest amount that something can produce or hold.
  - *Example:* The factory is running at full **capacity**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A particular role or position.
  - *Example:* She attended in her **capacity** as safety officer.

### 6. raw material
- **IPA:** /ˌrɔː məˈtɪəriəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A basic substance used to make a product.
  - *Example:* Rising **raw material** costs pushed up prices.

### 7. component
- **IPA:** /kəmˈpəʊnənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of the parts that make up a whole machine or system.
  - *Example:* A single faulty **component** caused the recall.

### 8. plant
- **IPA:** /plɑːnt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A factory or place where an industrial process happens.
  - *Example:* The company opened a new **plant** in Mexico.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A living thing that grows in the ground.
  - *Example:* There are **plants** along the office windows.
- **Meaning 3 (v):** To put seeds or plants in the ground to grow.
  - *Example:* They **planted** trees around the car park.

### 9. factory floor
- **IPA:** /ˈfæktri flɔː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The area of a factory where the machines are and the products are made.
  - *Example:* Managers spend an hour a day on the **factory floor**.

### 10. defect
- **IPA:** /ˈdiːfekt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fault or imperfection in a product.
  - *Example:* Any unit with a **defect** is removed from the line.

### 11. assemble
- **IPA:** /əˈsembl/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To put together the parts of something.
  - *Example:* Workers **assemble** each unit by hand.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To come together in one place as a group.
  - *Example:* Staff **assemble** in the yard during a fire drill.

### 12. machinery
- **IPA:** /məˈʃiːnəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Machines as a group, especially large ones used in industry.
  - *Example:* The **machinery** is serviced every three months.

### 13. operate
- **IPA:** /ˈɒpəreɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To control the working of a machine or system.
  - *Example:* Only trained staff may **operate** the press.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** (of a business or system) To work or function.
  - *Example:* The factory **operates** three shifts a day.

### 14. shift
- **IPA:** /ʃɪft/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fixed period of working time, especially in a factory.
  - *Example:* The morning **shift** ends at 2 p.m.

### 15. downtime
- **IPA:** /ˈdaʊntaɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Time when a machine or system is not working.
  - *Example:* Better [maintenance](05-office-procedures-and-equipment.md#25-maintenance) reduced **downtime** by half.

### 16. workshop
- **IPA:** /ˈwɜːkʃɒp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A room or building where things are made or repaired.
  - *Example:* The broken tools were sent to the **workshop**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A meeting where people learn a skill through practical work.
  - *Example:* She ran a **workshop** on safe lifting.

### 17. streamline
- **IPA:** /ˈstriːmlaɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To make a process simpler and more efficient.
  - *Example:* The redesign **streamlined** the packing process.

### 18. throughput
- **IPA:** /ˈθruːpʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of material or items passing through a system in a given time.
  - *Example:* The [upgrade](13-technology-and-computers.md#4-upgrade) raised **throughput** to 900 boxes an hour.

### 19. batch
- **IPA:** /bætʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A quantity of goods produced or processed together.
  - *Example:* Each **batch** is tested before it leaves the plant.

### 20. conveyor belt
- **IPA:** /kənˈveɪə belt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A continuous moving band that carries objects from one place to another.
  - *Example:* Boxes travel along the **conveyor belt** to the [loading dock](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#11-loading-dock).

### 21. fabricate
- **IPA:** /ˈfæbrɪkeɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To make or build a product, especially from prepared parts.
  - *Example:* The frames are **fabricated** from steel tubing.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To invent false information.
  - *Example:* He was accused of **fabricating** the test results.

### 22. specification
- **IPA:** /ˌspesɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A detailed description of how something should be made or done.
  - *Example:* The parts must match the customer's **specification** exactly.

### 23. prototype
- **IPA:** /ˈprəʊtətaɪp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The first model of a product, made to test the design.
  - *Example:* The **prototype** passed all the [stress tests](../toeic-3000/17-manufacturing-and-engineering.md#stress-test).

### 24. yield
- **IPA:** /jiːld/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of usable product that a process produces.
  - *Example:* The new method improved the **yield** to 95%.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To produce or provide a result or amount.
  - *Example:* The trial **yielded** promising data.

### 25. maintenance crew
- **IPA:** /ˈmeɪntənəns kruː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A team responsible for keeping machines and buildings working.
  - *Example:* The **maintenance crew** works overnight to avoid stopping [production](10-manufacturing-and-production.md#3-production).

### 26. hazard
- **IPA:** /ˈhæzəd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something that could be dangerous or cause harm.
  - *Example:* Loose cables on the floor are a trip **hazard**.

### 27. protective gear
- **IPA:** /prəˈtektɪv ɡɪə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Clothing and equipment worn to prevent injury.
  - *Example:* **Protective gear** must be worn on the [factory floor](10-manufacturing-and-production.md#9-factory-floor).

### 28. scrap
- **IPA:** /skræp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Material left over or rejected, often reused or sold.
  - *Example:* Metal **scrap** is collected and recycled.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To get rid of something that is no longer useful.
  - *Example:* The company **scrapped** the outdated line.

### 29. tooling
- **IPA:** /ˈtuːlɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The tools and equipment needed to make a particular product.
  - *Example:* New **tooling** is required for the redesigned casing.

### 30. lean
- **IPA:** /liːn/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** (of a production system) Designed to avoid waste of time and materials.
  - *Example:* The plant adopted **lean** methods to cut costs.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To bend or move from an upright position.
  - *Example:* Do not **lean** on the safety railing.
