# 07 — Correspondence & Email

### 1. correspondence
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒrəˈspɒndəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Letters and emails sent and received.
  - *Example:* Keep all **correspondence** with the client on file.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A close similarity or connection between two things.
  - *Example:* There is a clear **correspondence** between the two data sets.

### 2. attachment
- **IPA:** /əˈtætʃmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A file sent with an email.
  - *Example:* You forgot to include the **attachment**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A strong feeling of affection or connection.
  - *Example:* She has a strong **attachment** to her first employer.

### 3. recipient
- **IPA:** /rɪˈsɪpiənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who receives something.
  - *Example:* Add the finance team as **recipients** of the report.

### 4. sender
- **IPA:** /ˈsendə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The person who sends a letter or message.
  - *Example:* The email's **sender** was not someone I recognised.

### 5. inquiry
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈkwaɪəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A request for information.
  - *Example:* We received several **inquiries** about the new service.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** An official investigation.
  - *Example:* There will be an **inquiry** into the delay.

### 6. reply
- **IPA:** /rɪˈplaɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To answer a message or question.
  - *Example:* Please **reply** by the end of the day.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** An answer to a message or question.
  - *Example:* I'm still waiting for a **reply** to my last email.

### 7. forward
- **IPA:** /ˈfɔːwəd/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To send a received message on to someone else.
  - *Example:* Could you **forward** me the schedule she sent?
- **Meaning 2 (adv):** Toward the front or the future.
  - *Example:* We are moving the deadline **forward** to Monday.

### 8. draft
- **IPA:** /drɑːft/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A version of a message that is written but not yet sent.
  - *Example:* The email is still sitting in my **drafts**.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To write a first version of something.
  - *Example:* I'll **draft** a reply for you to check.

### 9. enclosed
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈkləʊzd/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Included inside an envelope or package with a letter.
  - *Example:* Please find the invoice **enclosed**.

### 10. formal
- **IPA:** /ˈfɔːml/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Following accepted rules of polite, official language or behaviour.
  - *Example:* A job application should use **formal** language.

### 11. informal
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈfɔːml/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Relaxed and friendly; not official.
  - *Example:* The two managers had an **informal** chat over coffee.

### 12. subject line
- **IPA:** /ˈsʌbdʒɪkt laɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The short title at the top of an email saying what it is about.
  - *Example:* Put the order number in the **subject line**.

### 13. acknowledge
- **IPA:** /əkˈnɒlɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To tell someone you have received their message.
  - *Example:* Please **acknowledge** receipt of this email.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To accept that something is true.
  - *Example:* The company **acknowledged** the mistake and apologised.

### 14. notify
- **IPA:** /ˈnəʊtɪfaɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To formally tell someone about something.
  - *Example:* **Notify** the supplier if the shipment is damaged.

### 15. regarding
- **IPA:** /rɪˈɡɑːdɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (prep):** About; concerning.
  - *Example:* I'm writing **regarding** your order from last week.

### 16. attach
- **IPA:** /əˈtætʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To add a file to an email.
  - *Example:* I've **attached** the signed contract.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To fasten or join one thing to another.
  - *Example:* **Attach** the label to the front of the box.

### 17. cc (carbon copy)
- **IPA:** /ˌsiːˈsiː/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To send someone a copy of an email sent mainly to another person.
  - *Example:* Please **cc** me on all messages to the client.

### 18. thread
- **IPA:** /θred/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A series of connected email messages on one topic.
  - *Example:* The answer is buried somewhere in this email **thread**.

### 19. signature block
- **IPA:** /ˈsɪɡnətʃə blɒk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The standard set of contact details at the end of an email.
  - *Example:* Update your phone number in your **signature block**.

### 20. urgent
- **IPA:** /ˈɜːdʒənt/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Needing immediate attention or action.
  - *Example:* Mark the email **urgent** so it isn't missed.

### 21. follow up
- **IPA:** /ˌfɒləʊ ˈʌp/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To take further action after an earlier message or meeting.
  - *Example:* I'll **follow up** with them if we don't hear back by Friday.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A further action or message after an earlier one (*follow-up*).
  - *Example:* This email is a **follow-up** to our call yesterday.

### 22. sincerely
- **IPA:** /sɪnˈsɪəli/
- **Meaning 1 (adv):** In an honest and genuine way; used to end a formal letter.
  - *Example:* The letter ended, "Yours **sincerely**, A. Brown."

### 23. attach herewith
- **IPA:** /əˈtætʃ ˌhɪəˈwɪð/
- **Meaning 1 (phrase):** A formal way to say "attached to this message."
  - *Example:* Please find **attached herewith** the quarterly report.

### 24. bounce back
- **IPA:** /ˌbaʊns ˈbæk/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** (of an email) To be returned because it could not be delivered.
  - *Example:* The message **bounced back** — the address must be wrong.

### 25. confidential
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒnfɪˈdenʃl/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Meant to be kept secret or shared only with certain people.
  - *Example:* This report is **confidential** and should not be forwarded.

### 26. distribution list
- **IPA:** /ˌdɪstrɪˈbjuːʃn lɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A saved group of email addresses used to send one message to many people.
  - *Example:* Add the new starters to the department **distribution list**.

### 27. prompt
- **IPA:** /prɒmpt/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Done quickly and without delay.
  - *Example:* Thank you for your **prompt** reply.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To cause someone to do something.
  - *Example:* The complaint **prompted** a review of our returns policy.

### 28. courteous
- **IPA:** /ˈkɜːtiəs/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Polite and respectful in manner.
  - *Example:* Even difficult emails should get a **courteous** reply.

### 29. queries
- **IPA:** /ˈkwɪəriz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Questions, especially ones asking for information or expressing doubt.
  - *Example:* Send any **queries** to the support address.

### 30. dispatch
- **IPA:** /dɪˈspætʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To send something to a destination.
  - *Example:* The parcel was **dispatched** this morning.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The act of sending something off.
  - *Example:* Goods are ready for **dispatch** within 24 hours.
