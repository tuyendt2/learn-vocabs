# 14 — Travel & Transportation

### 1. itinerary
- **IPA:** /aɪˈtɪnərəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A detailed plan of a journey, listing times and places.
  - *Example:* The travel agent emailed the full **itinerary**.

### 2. reservation
- **IPA:** /ˌrezəˈveɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement to keep a seat, room, or table for someone.
  - *Example:* I have a **reservation** for two nights.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A feeling of doubt about a plan or idea.
  - *Example:* She had some **reservations** about the route.

### 3. departure
- **IPA:** /dɪˈpɑːtʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of leaving, especially to start a journey.
  - *Example:* **Departure** is at 7:15 from Gate 12.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A change from what is usual or expected.
  - *Example:* The new design is a **departure** from tradition.

### 4. arrival
- **IPA:** /əˈraɪvl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of reaching a place at the end of a journey.
  - *Example:* On **arrival**, please go straight to the front desk.

### 5. boarding pass
- **IPA:** /ˈbɔːdɪŋ pɑːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A card that allows a passenger to get on a plane.
  - *Example:* Have your **boarding pass** and ID ready.

### 6. layover
- **IPA:** /ˈleɪəʊvə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A short stay somewhere between two parts of a journey.
  - *Example:* We have a three-hour **layover** in Dubai.

### 7. carry-on
- **IPA:** /ˈkæri ɒn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small bag you take onto the plane rather than check in.
  - *Example:* Liquids in your **carry-on** must be under 100 ml.

### 8. customs
- **IPA:** /ˈkʌstəmz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The place at a border where officials check what travellers bring in.
  - *Example:* We were stopped at **customs** to open our bags.

### 9. commute
- **IPA:** /kəˈmjuːt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To travel regularly between home and work.
  - *Example:* She **commutes** an hour each way by train.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The regular journey between home and work.
  - *Example:* His **commute** got shorter after the office moved.

### 10. fare
- **IPA:** /feə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The money paid to travel by bus, train, taxi, or plane.
  - *Example:* The train **fare** went up in January.

### 11. terminal
- **IPA:** /ˈtɜːmɪnl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A building where air or bus passengers arrive and depart.
  - *Example:* International flights leave from **Terminal** 2.
- **Meaning 2 (adj):** (of an illness) Leading to death; final.
  - *Example:* The machine reached the end of its **terminal** phase and shut down.

### 12. delay
- **IPA:** /dɪˈleɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A period of waiting caused by a problem.
  - *Example:* There was a two-hour **delay** because of fog.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To make something late or slow.
  - *Example:* Bad weather **delayed** the connecting flight.

### 13. shuttle
- **IPA:** /ˈʃʌtl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A bus or train that travels frequently between two places.
  - *Example:* A free **shuttle** runs from the hotel to the airport.

### 14. baggage claim
- **IPA:** /ˈbæɡɪdʒ kleɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The area in an airport where passengers collect their checked bags.
  - *Example:* Meet me at **baggage claim** after you land.

### 15. one-way
- **IPA:** /ˌwʌn ˈweɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** (of a ticket) For travel in one direction only.
  - *Example:* He booked a **one-way** ticket to Tokyo.

### 16. round trip
- **IPA:** /ˌraʊnd ˈtrɪp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A journey to a place and back again.
  - *Example:* The **round trip** takes about five hours.

### 17. transit
- **IPA:** /ˈtrænzɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of travelling or being moved from one place to another.
  - *Example:* Passengers in **transit** wait in a separate lounge.

### 18. excursion
- **IPA:** /ɪkˈskɜːʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A short trip, usually for pleasure and with a group.
  - *Example:* The conference included an **excursion** to the old town.

### 19. voucher
- **IPA:** /ˈvaʊtʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A printed paper that can be used instead of money for a specific purpose.
  - *Example:* The airline gave us a meal **voucher** during the delay.

### 20. destination
- **IPA:** /ˌdestɪˈneɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The place to which someone or something is going.
  - *Example:* Bangkok is a popular **destination** for trade fairs.

### 21. board
- **IPA:** /bɔːd/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To get on a plane, ship, train, or bus.
  - *Example:* We will start to **board** at 6:40.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A group of people who manage a company.
  - *Example:* The **board** approved the travel budget.

### 22. check in
- **IPA:** /ˌtʃek ˈɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To report your arrival at an airport or hotel.
  - *Example:* You can **check in** online 24 hours before the flight.

### 23. connecting flight
- **IPA:** /kəˈnektɪŋ flaɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A second flight that a passenger takes to reach a final destination.
  - *Example:* We missed our **connecting flight** by ten minutes.

### 24. accommodation
- **IPA:** /əˌkɒməˈdeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A place to stay, such as a hotel room.
  - *Example:* The company arranges **accommodation** for visiting staff.

### 25. per diem
- **IPA:** /pə ˈdiːem/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fixed daily amount paid to cover expenses during work travel.
  - *Example:* The **per diem** covers meals and local transport.

### 26. overbook
- **IPA:** /ˌəʊvəˈbʊk/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To sell more tickets or rooms than there is space for.
  - *Example:* The flight was **overbooked**, so two passengers were rebooked.

### 27. detour
- **IPA:** /ˈdiːtʊə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A longer route taken to avoid a problem on the usual road.
  - *Example:* Roadworks forced a **detour** through the village.

### 28. expense report
- **IPA:** /ɪkˈspens rɪˌpɔːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A form listing money spent on business travel, to be paid back.
  - *Example:* Submit your **expense report** within a week of returning.

### 29. transfer
- **IPA:** /ˈtrænsfɜː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Transport arranged between an airport and a hotel or other point.
  - *Example:* The package includes airport **transfers**.

### 30. valid
- **IPA:** /ˈvælɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Legally or officially acceptable; able to be used.
  - *Example:* Your passport must be **valid** for six months after arrival.
