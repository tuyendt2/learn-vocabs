# 19 — Real Estate & Property

### 1. property
- **IPA:** /ˈprɒpəti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A building and the land belonging to it.
  - *Example:* The company owns several **properties** in the city centre.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A thing or things that someone owns.
  - *Example:* Personal **property** is not covered by the office policy.
- **Meaning 3 (n):** A quality or feature of something.
  - *Example:* The material has useful insulating **properties**.

### 2. lease
- **IPA:** /liːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A legal agreement to rent property for a fixed period.
  - *Example:* The **lease** runs for five years with an option to renew.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To rent property under a legal agreement.
  - *Example:* We **lease** the ground floor to a coffee shop.

### 3. tenant
- **IPA:** /ˈtenənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who pays rent to use a property.
  - *Example:* The new **tenant** moves in next month.

### 4. landlord
- **IPA:** /ˈlændlɔːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person or company that rents out property to others.
  - *Example:* The **landlord** is responsible for major repairs.

### 5. mortgage
- **IPA:** /ˈmɔːɡɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A loan used to buy property, secured against that property.
  - *Example:* They paid off the **mortgage** after 20 years.

### 6. premises
- **IPA:** /ˈpremɪsɪz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The building and land used by a business.
  - *Example:* The firm is looking for larger **premises**.

### 7. rent
- **IPA:** /rent/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money paid regularly to use a property owned by someone else.
  - *Example:* The **rent** is due on the first of each month.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To pay to use something owned by someone else.
  - *Example:* They **rent** an office near the station.

### 8. commercial
- **IPA:** /kəˈmɜːʃl/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Relating to business and the buying and selling of goods.
  - *Example:* The building is zoned for **commercial** use.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** An advertisement on television or radio.
  - *Example:* The **commercial** airs during the evening news.

### 9. residential
- **IPA:** /ˌrezɪˈdenʃl/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Consisting of or suitable for homes rather than businesses.
  - *Example:* The area is mostly **residential**, with few shops.

### 10. appraisal
- **IPA:** /əˈpreɪzl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An expert estimate of the value of a property.
  - *Example:* The bank ordered an **appraisal** before approving the loan.

### 11. deed
- **IPA:** /diːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A legal document showing who owns a property.
  - *Example:* The **deed** is kept at the lawyer's office.

### 12. vacant
- **IPA:** /ˈveɪkənt/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Empty; not being used or lived in.
  - *Example:* The unit has been **vacant** for three months.

### 13. occupant
- **IPA:** /ˈɒkjəpənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who lives or works in a particular place.
  - *Example:* The previous **occupant** left the office in good condition.

### 14. utilities
- **IPA:** /juːˈtɪlətiz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Basic services such as water, gas, and electricity.
  - *Example:* The rent does not include **utilities**.

### 15. square footage
- **IPA:** /ˌskweə ˈfʊtɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The area of a space measured in square feet.
  - *Example:* We need more **square footage** for the growing team.

### 16. zoning
- **IPA:** /ˈzəʊnɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Official rules about how land in an area may be used.
  - *Example:* **Zoning** laws do not allow a factory on that street.

### 17. sublet
- **IPA:** /ˌsʌbˈlet/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To rent to someone else a property that you rent from another person.
  - *Example:* We **sublet** two desks to a small start-up.

### 18. renovate
- **IPA:** /ˈrenəveɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To repair and improve an old building.
  - *Example:* They **renovated** the lobby over the summer.

### 19. down payment
- **IPA:** /ˈdaʊn ˌpeɪmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The first, partial payment made when buying something expensive.
  - *Example:* They saved for years to make the **down payment** on the office.

### 20. realtor
- **IPA:** /ˈriːəltə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person whose job is to arrange the selling and renting of property.
  - *Example:* The **realtor** showed us four units in one afternoon.

### 21. amenities
- **IPA:** /əˈmiːnətiz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Useful features of a building or area that make it pleasant to use.
  - *Example:* Building **amenities** include parking and a gym.

### 22. inspection
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈspekʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A careful examination of a property before buying or renting.
  - *Example:* The **inspection** found a problem with the roof.

### 23. leasehold
- **IPA:** /ˈliːshəʊld/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The right to use property for a set number of years under a lease.
  - *Example:* They bought the **leasehold** rather than the freehold.

### 24. tenancy
- **IPA:** /ˈtenənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The period or arrangement of renting a property.
  - *Example:* The **tenancy** ends in December.

### 25. eviction
- **IPA:** /ɪˈvɪkʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The legal removal of a tenant from a property.
  - *Example:* Non-payment of rent can lead to **eviction**.

### 26. escrow
- **IPA:** /ˈeskrəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money or documents held by a third party until a deal is complete.
  - *Example:* The deposit is held in **escrow** until closing.

### 27. relocation
- **IPA:** /ˌriːləʊˈkeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of moving a business or person to a new place.
  - *Example:* The **relocation** to the new building takes place in May.

### 28. floor plan
- **IPA:** /ˈflɔː plæn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A drawing showing the layout of rooms in a building, seen from above.
  - *Example:* The **floor plan** shows an open-plan work area.

### 29. lease term
- **IPA:** /ˈliːs tɜːm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The length of time a lease agreement lasts.
  - *Example:* We negotiated a shorter **lease term** of two years.

### 30. freehold
- **IPA:** /ˈfriːhəʊld/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Full ownership of a property and the land it stands on, for an unlimited time.
  - *Example:* Buying the **freehold** removed the yearly ground rent.
