# 05 — Office Procedures & Equipment

### 1. photocopier
- **IPA:** /ˈfəʊtəʊkɒpiə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A machine that makes paper copies of documents.
  - *Example:* The **photocopier** on the third floor is jammed again.

### 2. stapler
- **IPA:** /ˈsteɪplə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small device that fastens sheets of paper together with wire staples.
  - *Example:* Can I borrow your **stapler** for a second?

### 3. shredder
- **IPA:** /ˈʃredə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A machine that cuts paper into thin strips so it cannot be read.
  - *Example:* [Confidential](07-correspondence-and-email.md#25-confidential) files must go in the **shredder**.

### 4. scanner
- **IPA:** /ˈskænə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A device that copies documents or images into a computer.
  - *Example:* Use the **scanner** to send a digital copy of the [invoice](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#6-invoice).

### 5. projector
- **IPA:** /prəˈdʒektə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A device that displays images or slides onto a screen or wall.
  - *Example:* The **projector** bulb needs replacing before the [presentation](06-meetings-and-presentations.md#4-presentation).

### 6. stationery
- **IPA:** /ˈsteɪʃənri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Materials for writing, such as paper, pens, and envelopes.
  - *Example:* Order more **stationery** from the usual [supplier](09-sales-and-purchasing.md#2-supplier).

### 7. supplies
- **IPA:** /səˈplaɪz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Items needed for regular use in an office or activity.
  - *Example:* We're running low on office **supplies**.

### 8. memo
- **IPA:** /ˈmeməʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A short written message sent within an organisation.
  - *Example:* The manager sent a **memo** about the new parking rules.

### 9. agenda
- **IPA:** /əˈdʒendə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A list of items to be discussed at a meeting.
  - *Example:* The budget is the first item on the **agenda**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A set of aims or plans that someone wants to achieve.
  - *Example:* [Cost-cutting](../toeic-3000/36-personal-finance-and-household-budget.md#cost-cutting) is high on the new director's **agenda**.

### 10. minutes
- **IPA:** /ˈmɪnɪts/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written record of what was said and decided at a meeting.
  - *Example:* Please [circulate](../toeic-3000/08-business-communication.md#circulate) the **minutes** within two days.

### 11. schedule
- **IPA:** /ˈʃedjuːl/ or /ˈskedʒuːl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A plan showing when tasks or events will happen.
  - *Example:* The project is two weeks behind **schedule**.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To arrange for something to happen at a particular time.
  - *Example:* The call is **scheduled** for 3 p.m. on Thursday.

### 12. appointment
- **IPA:** /əˈpɔɪntmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement to meet someone at a particular time.
  - *Example:* I have an **appointment** with the dentist at noon.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The act of choosing someone for a job or position.
  - *Example:* The **appointment** of the new CEO was announced today.

### 13. folder
- **IPA:** /ˈfəʊldə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A cover or holder for keeping loose papers together.
  - *Example:* Put the signed forms in the blue **folder**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A place on a computer where files are stored together.
  - *Example:* Save the report in the "Q3" **folder**.

### 14. binder
- **IPA:** /ˈbaɪndə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A stiff cover with rings for holding punched sheets of paper.
  - *Example:* The training manual comes in a ring **binder**.

### 15. envelope
- **IPA:** /ˈenvələʊp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A flat paper container for a letter or document.
  - *Example:* Seal the contract in a padded **envelope**.

### 16. fax machine
- **IPA:** /ˈfæks məˌʃiːn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A machine that sends and receives copies of documents over a phone line.
  - *Example:* The signed form came through on the **fax machine**.

### 17. reception
- **IPA:** /rɪˈsepʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The area near the entrance where visitors are greeted.
  - *Example:* Please sign in at **reception**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The way people react to something.
  - *Example:* The new policy got a cool **reception** from staff.
- **Meaning 3 (n):** A formal social event to welcome someone.
  - *Example:* There will be a **reception** for the [delegates](04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#26-delegate) at 6 p.m.

### 18. cubicle
- **IPA:** /ˈkjuːbɪkl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small partly enclosed workspace in an office.
  - *Example:* She decorated her **cubicle** with family photos.

### 19. workstation
- **IPA:** /ˈwɜːksteɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A desk and computer where one person works.
  - *Example:* Each **workstation** has two [monitors](../toeic-3000/06-projects-and-deadlines.md#monitor).

### 20. intercom
- **IPA:** /ˈɪntəkɒm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A system that lets people in different rooms speak to each other.
  - *Example:* Reception called the [warehouse](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#15-warehouse) over the **intercom**.

### 21. whiteboard
- **IPA:** /ˈwaɪtbɔːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A smooth white surface for writing on with markers.
  - *Example:* He sketched the process on the **whiteboard**.

### 22. clipboard
- **IPA:** /ˈklɪpbɔːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small board with a clip at the top for holding papers.
  - *Example:* The [inspector](../toeic-3000/15-taxation-and-regulation.md#inspector) walked around with a **clipboard**.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A temporary storage area for copied computer data.
  - *Example:* Copy the address to the **clipboard** and paste it below.

### 23. toner
- **IPA:** /ˈtəʊnə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The powder used in laser printers and photocopiers to form text and images.
  - *Example:* The printer is out of **toner**.

### 24. cartridge
- **IPA:** /ˈkɑːtrɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small container that fits into a machine, especially one holding ink.
  - *Example:* Replace the ink **cartridge** when the light flashes.

### 25. maintenance
- **IPA:** /ˈmeɪntənəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The work of keeping equipment or buildings in good condition.
  - *Example:* The lifts are closed for **maintenance** this weekend.

### 26. equipment
- **IPA:** /ɪˈkwɪpmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The tools or machines needed for a particular activity.
  - *Example:* All safety **equipment** is checked monthly.

### 27. facility
- **IPA:** /fəˈsɪləti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A building or place provided for a particular purpose.
  - *Example:* The new **facility** can process twice the volume.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A service or feature that makes something possible or easier.
  - *Example:* The account offers an [overdraft](02-finance-and-banking.md#28-overdraft) **facility**.

### 28. letterhead
- **IPA:** /ˈletəhed/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The printed name and address of a company at the top of its writing paper.
  - *Example:* Official letters must be printed on company **letterhead**.

### 29. filing cabinet
- **IPA:** /ˈfaɪlɪŋ ˌkæbɪnət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A piece of office furniture with drawers for storing documents.
  - *Example:* The signed contracts are kept in the locked **filing cabinet**.

### 30. logbook
- **IPA:** /ˈlɒɡbʊk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A book with an official record of events, use, or maintenance.
  - *Example:* Sign the **logbook** each time you use the company car.
