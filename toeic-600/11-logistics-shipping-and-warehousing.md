# 11 — Logistics, Shipping & Warehousing

### 1. shipment
- **IPA:** /ˈʃɪpmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A quantity of goods sent together to a destination.
  - *Example:* The **shipment** cleared [customs](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#13-customs) this morning.

### 2. freight
- **IPA:** /freɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Goods transported in bulk by ship, plane, train, or truck.
  - *Example:* Air **freight** is faster but more expensive.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The cost of transporting goods.
  - *Example:* The price includes **freight** to the port.

### 3. cargo
- **IPA:** /ˈkɑːɡəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The goods carried by a ship, plane, or vehicle.
  - *Example:* The ship's **cargo** was mainly grain.

### 4. consignment
- **IPA:** /kənˈsaɪnmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A batch of goods sent to someone.
  - *Example:* A **consignment** of spare parts arrived a day early.

### 5. carrier
- **IPA:** /ˈkæriə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A company that transports goods or passengers.
  - *Example:* We use a national **carrier** for overnight delivery.

### 6. distribution
- **IPA:** /ˌdɪstrɪˈbjuːʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of supplying goods to shops and customers.
  - *Example:* The firm has a strong **distribution** [network](13-technology-and-computers.md#5-network) in Europe.

### 7. logistics
- **IPA:** /ləˈdʒɪstɪks/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The organisation of moving, storing, and supplying goods.
  - *Example:* **Logistics** costs rose sharply after the fuel price increase.

### 8. dispatch
- **IPA:** /dɪˈspætʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To send goods or people to a destination.
  - *Example:* Orders placed before noon are **dispatched** the same day.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The act of sending something off.
  - *Example:* The goods are awaiting **dispatch** in the loading bay.

### 9. pallet
- **IPA:** /ˈpælət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A flat wooden or plastic base used for stacking and moving goods.
  - *Example:* Each **pallet** holds 48 cartons.

### 10. forklift
- **IPA:** /ˈfɔːklɪft/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small vehicle with metal forks for lifting and moving heavy loads.
  - *Example:* Only licensed staff may drive the **forklift**.

### 11. loading dock
- **IPA:** /ˈləʊdɪŋ dɒk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A raised platform where goods are loaded onto or off trucks.
  - *Example:* Trucks queue at the **loading dock** every morning.

### 12. tracking number
- **IPA:** /ˈtrækɪŋ ˌnʌmbə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A code used to follow the progress of a parcel.
  - *Example:* Use the **tracking number** to see where your order is.

### 13. customs
- **IPA:** /ˈkʌstəmz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The government department that checks goods entering a country and collects duties.
  - *Example:* The crates were held at **customs** for [inspection](12-quality-control-and-standards.md#2-inspection).

### 14. duty
- **IPA:** /ˈdjuːti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A tax paid on goods brought into or taken out of a country.
  - *Example:* Import **duty** on electronics is 8%.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** Something you must do as part of your job or role.
  - *Example:* It is the guard's **duty** to check every visitor.

### 15. warehouse
- **IPA:** /ˈweəhaʊs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large building where goods are stored.
  - *Example:* The **warehouse** is fully [automated](13-technology-and-computers.md#30-automate).

### 16. stockroom
- **IPA:** /ˈstɒkruːm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A room where goods and supplies are kept.
  - *Example:* Check the **stockroom** before ordering more.

### 17. handling
- **IPA:** /ˈhændlɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of picking up, moving, and packing goods.
  - *Example:* Fragile items need careful **handling**.

### 18. courier
- **IPA:** /ˈkʊriə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A company or person that delivers parcels quickly.
  - *Example:* A **courier** will collect the package at 4 p.m.

### 19. bill of lading
- **IPA:** /ˌbɪl əv ˈleɪdɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A document listing goods shipped and serving as a receipt.
  - *Example:* The **bill of lading** must match the packing list.

### 20. in transit
- **IPA:** /ɪn ˈtrænzɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (phrase):** Being transported from one place to another.
  - *Example:* Your order is currently **in transit**.

### 21. expedite
- **IPA:** /ˈekspədaɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To make a process happen faster.
  - *Example:* We paid extra to **expedite** the [shipment](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#1-shipment).

### 22. backlog
- **IPA:** /ˈbæklɒɡ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A build-up of work or orders not yet dealt with.
  - *Example:* The strike created a **backlog** of deliveries.

### 23. replenish
- **IPA:** /rɪˈplenɪʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To fill something up again; to restore a supply.
  - *Example:* Shelves are **replenished** overnight.

### 24. haulage
- **IPA:** /ˈhɔːlɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The business of transporting goods by road.
  - *Example:* A local **haulage** firm handles our deliveries.

### 25. perishable
- **IPA:** /ˈperɪʃəbl/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Likely to decay or go bad quickly.
  - *Example:* **Perishable** goods travel in refrigerated trucks.

### 26. fulfilment
- **IPA:** /fʊlˈfɪlmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of packing and sending customer orders.
  - *Example:* A third-party company handles order **fulfilment**.

### 27. dimensions
- **IPA:** /daɪˈmenʃnz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The measurements of something, such as length, width, and height.
  - *Example:* Enter the parcel's **dimensions** to get a quote.

### 28. surcharge
- **IPA:** /ˈsɜːtʃɑːdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An extra charge added to the usual price.
  - *Example:* A fuel **surcharge** applies to all international [shipments](11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#1-shipment).

### 29. transit time
- **IPA:** /ˈtrænzɪt taɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The time taken for goods to travel from sender to receiver.
  - *Example:* **Transit time** to the west coast is three days.

### 30. warehouse management system
- **IPA:** /ˈweəhaʊs ˈmænɪdʒmənt ˌsɪstəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Software used to control and track the storage and movement of goods.
  - *Example:* The new **warehouse management system** cut picking errors.
