### inventory check
- **IPA:** /ˈɪnvəntri tʃek/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A record of the condition and contents of a rented property at the start and end of a tenancy.
  - *Example:* The **inventory check** noted a mark on the kitchen wall.

### rent arrears
- **IPA:** /ˈrent əˌrɪəz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Unpaid rent that has built up.
  - *Example:* Two months of **rent arrears** led to a warning letter.

### notice period
- **IPA:** /ˈnəʊtɪs ˌpɪəriəd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The length of warning that must be given before ending a tenancy or job.
  - *Example:* The [tenancy](../toeic-600/19-real-estate-and-property.md#24-tenancy) has a one-month **notice period** on each side.

### eviction notice
- **IPA:** /ɪˈvɪkʃn ˌnəʊtɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A formal document telling a tenant they must leave a property.
  - *Example:* An **eviction notice** must follow the correct legal steps.

### lease renewal
- **IPA:** /ˈliːs rɪˌnjuːəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The extension of a lease for a further period.
  - *Example:* At **lease renewal**, the [landlord](../toeic-600/19-real-estate-and-property.md#4-landlord) raised the rent by 8%.

### lodger
- **IPA:** /ˈlɒdʒə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who pays to rent a room in someone's home, where the owner also lives.
  - *Example:* Taking in a **lodger** helped cover the [mortgage](../toeic-600/19-real-estate-and-property.md#5-mortgage).

### freeholder
- **IPA:** /ˈfriːhəʊldə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The person who owns a property and the land it stands on outright.
  - *Example:* The **freeholder** is responsible for the roof and outside walls.

### leaseholder
- **IPA:** /ˈliːshəʊldə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who owns the right to use a property for a fixed number of years.
  - *Example:* As a **leaseholder**, she pays an annual charge to the [freeholder](37-home-housing-and-maintenance.md#freeholder).

### ground rent
- **IPA:** /ˈɡraʊnd rent/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small yearly payment a leaseholder makes to the freeholder for the land.
  - *Example:* The **ground rent** was fixed at £150 a year for the whole term.

### mortgage repayment
- **IPA:** /ˈmɔːɡɪdʒ rɪˌpeɪmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A regular payment made toward a home loan.
  - *Example:* Rising rates pushed the monthly **mortgage repayment** up by £200.

### remortgage
- **IPA:** /ˌriːˈmɔːɡɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To take out a new mortgage on a property you already own, usually for a better rate.
  - *Example:* They **remortgaged** to [release](25-information-technology.md#release) money for an [extension](26-telecommunications-and-networks.md#extension).

### equity release
- **IPA:** /ˈekwəti rɪˌliːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A way for older owners to get cash from their home's value without moving out.
  - *Example:* **Equity release** gave them income but reduced what the children would inherit.

### conveyancer
- **IPA:** /kənˈveɪənsə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who handles the legal transfer of property between owners.
  - *Example:* The **conveyancer** found a boundary [problem](10-problem-solving-and-decision-making.md#problem) before [completion](06-projects-and-deadlines.md#completion).

### survey report
- **IPA:** /ˈsɜːveɪ rɪˌpɔːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written assessment of a property's condition, made before purchase.
  - *Example:* The **survey report** flagged damp and an ageing roof.

### damp
- **IPA:** /dæmp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Unwanted moisture in the walls or floors of a building.
  - *Example:* **Damp** in the corner had lifted the wallpaper.

### subsidence
- **IPA:** /səbˈsaɪdəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The sinking of the ground beneath a building, causing cracks.
  - *Example:* **Subsidence** made the [insurance](../toeic-600/17-health-and-medical.md#9-insurance) much harder to arrange.

### woodworm
- **IPA:** /ˈwʊdwɜːm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The larvae of beetles that bore holes in wood, damaging it.
  - *Example:* **Woodworm** was found in the roof timbers.

### draught
- **IPA:** /drɑːft/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A current of cold air coming into a room through a gap.
  - *Example:* A **draught** under the door made the hallway cold.

### boiler service
- **IPA:** /ˈbɔɪlə ˌsɜːvɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An annual check and clean of a heating boiler to keep it safe and efficient.
  - *Example:* An annual **boiler service** is a condition of the [warranty](../toeic-600/01-contracts-and-agreements.md#17-warranty).

### radiator
- **IPA:** /ˈreɪdieɪtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A metal panel that heats a room using hot water from the boiler.
  - *Example:* One **radiator** stayed cold and needed bleeding.

### thermostat
- **IPA:** /ˈθɜːməstæt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A device that controls the temperature by switching heating on and off.
  - *Example:* Turning the **thermostat** down by one degree cut the bill noticeably.

### fuse box
- **IPA:** /ˈfjuːz bɒks/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The panel where a building's electrical circuits are protected and can be switched off.
  - *Example:* The power went off and a switch in the **fuse box** had tripped.

### circuit breaker
- **IPA:** /ˈsɜːkɪt ˌbreɪkə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A switch that cuts off an electrical circuit automatically if there is a fault.
  - *Example:* The **circuit breaker** tripped every time the kettle was used.

### rewire
- **IPA:** /ˌriːˈwaɪə/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To replace all the electrical wiring in a building.
  - *Example:* The house had to be **rewired** before it could be let.

### plumbing leak
- **IPA:** /ˈplʌmɪŋ liːk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Water escaping from a pipe or fitting.
  - *Example:* A slow **plumbing leak** under the sink rotted the cupboard floor.

### blocked drain
- **IPA:** /ˌblɒkt ˈdreɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A pipe carrying away waste water that is stopped up.
  - *Example:* A **blocked drain** made the sink empty very slowly.

### downpipe
- **IPA:** /ˈdaʊnpaɪp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A vertical pipe that carries rainwater from the gutter to the ground.
  - *Example:* A cracked **downpipe** was soaking the wall below.

### roof tile
- **IPA:** /ˈruːf taɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of the flat pieces that cover and waterproof a roof.
  - *Example:* Two **roof tiles** blew off in the storm.

### flashing
- **IPA:** /ˈflæʃɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Thin metal fitted where a roof meets a wall or chimney to keep water out.
  - *Example:* Failed **flashing** around the chimney let rain into the loft.

### pointing
- **IPA:** /ˈpɔɪntɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The mortar between bricks or stones at the surface of a wall.
  - *Example:* Crumbling **pointing** on the front wall needed raking out and replacing.

### skirting board
- **IPA:** /ˈskɜːtɪŋ bɔːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A board fixed along the bottom of an interior wall where it meets the floor.
  - *Example:* The **skirting board** was scuffed and due for repainting.

### floorboard
- **IPA:** /ˈflɔːbɔːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of the long boards that make up a wooden floor.
  - *Example:* A loose **floorboard** creaked with every step.

### underlay
- **IPA:** /ˈʌndəleɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A layer of soft material laid under a carpet for comfort and insulation.
  - *Example:* Good **underlay** made the old carpet feel new.

### damp-proof course
- **IPA:** /ˈdæmp pruːf kɔːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A barrier built into a wall to stop moisture rising from the ground.
  - *Example:* The **damp-proof course** had failed on the north wall.

### extractor fan
- **IPA:** /ɪkˈstræktə fæn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fan that removes steam and smells from a kitchen or bathroom.
  - *Example:* A working **extractor fan** cut the [condensation](37-home-housing-and-maintenance.md#condensation) in the bathroom.

### ventilation
- **IPA:** /ˌventɪˈleɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The movement of fresh air through a room or building.
  - *Example:* Poor **ventilation** let damp build up in the bedrooms.

### mould
- **IPA:** /məʊld/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fungus that grows in damp places, often as black spots on walls.
  - *Example:* Black **mould** spread across the ceiling above the shower.

### condensation
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒndenˈseɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Drops of water that form when warm, moist air meets a cold surface.
  - *Example:* **Condensation** ran down the inside of the single-glazed windows.

### decorating
- **IPA:** /ˈdekəreɪtɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The work of painting and papering the inside of a home.
  - *Example:* They did the **decorating** themselves to save money.

### undercoat

### filler
- **IPA:** /ˈfɪlə/

### sealant
- **IPA:** /ˈsiːlənt/

  - *Example:* Fresh **sealant** around the bath stopped the leak into the room below.

### grouting
- **IPA:** /ˈɡraʊtɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The material filling the gaps between tiles.
  - *Example:* Discoloured **grouting** made the old tiles look worse than they were.

### fixtures and fittings
- **IPA:** /ˌfɪkstʃəz ən ˈfɪtɪŋz/

### flat-pack
