# 38 — Food, Cooking & Dining Out

### ingredient
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈɡriːdiənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of the foods used to make a particular dish.
  - *Example:* The recipe has just five **ingredients**.

### recipe
- **IPA:** /ˈresəpi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A set of instructions for preparing a dish.
  - *Example:* She halved the **recipe** to cook for two.

### serving
- **IPA:** /ˈsɜːvɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An amount of food for one person.
  - *Example:* The pack makes four **servings**.

### pantry
- **IPA:** /ˈpæntri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small room or cupboard for storing food.
  - *Example:* The **pantry** was stocked with tins, pasta, and rice.

### larder
- **IPA:** /ˈlɑːdə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A cool cupboard or small room for keeping food.
  - *Example:* Cheese and cured meat were kept in the **larder**.

### condiment
- **IPA:** /ˈkɒndɪmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sauce or seasoning added to food at the table, such as ketchup or mustard.
  - *Example:* A tray of **condiments** sat on every table.

### seasoning
- **IPA:** /ˈsiːzənɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Salt, pepper, herbs, or spices added to improve the taste of food.
  - *Example:* The soup needed more **seasoning**.

### marinade
- **IPA:** /ˌmærɪˈneɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A mixture of oil, acid, and spices in which food is soaked before cooking.
  - *Example:* The chicken sat in a lemon **marinade** overnight.

### garnish
- **IPA:** /ˈɡɑːnɪʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small amount of food used to decorate a dish.
  - *Example:* A sprig of parsley was the only **garnish**.

### batter
- **IPA:** /ˈbætə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A liquid mixture of flour, eggs, and milk used to make pancakes or coat food for frying.
  - *Example:* Dip the fish in **batter** before frying.

### dough
- **IPA:** /dəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A thick mixture of flour and liquid, kneaded and used to make bread or pastry.
  - *Example:* Leave the **dough** to rise in a warm place.

### knead
- **IPA:** /niːd/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To press and stretch dough with your hands to make it smooth.
  - *Example:* **Knead** the dough for ten minutes.

### whisk
- **IPA:** /wɪsk/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To beat eggs, cream, or a mixture quickly to add air.
  - *Example:* **Whisk** the eggs until pale and thick.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** A kitchen tool of looped wire used for whisking.
  - *Example:* Use a balloon **whisk** for the cream.

### fold
- **IPA:** /fəʊld/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To gently mix a light ingredient into a heavier one without knocking out the air.
  - *Example:* **Fold** the egg whites into the batter carefully.

### simmer
- **IPA:** /ˈsɪmə/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook liquid gently, just below boiling.
  - *Example:* Let the sauce **simmer** for twenty minutes.

### sauté
- **IPA:** /ˈsəʊteɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To fry food quickly in a little hot fat.
  - *Example:* **Sauté** the onions until soft.

### braise
- **IPA:** /breɪz/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook meat or vegetables slowly in a small amount of liquid in a covered pot.
  - *Example:* **Braise** the beef for three hours until it falls apart.

### poach
- **IPA:** /pəʊtʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook food gently in barely simmering liquid.
  - *Example:* **Poach** the eggs for three minutes.

### roast
- **IPA:** /rəʊst/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook food in an oven or over a fire with fat.
  - *Example:* **Roast** the potatoes until crisp and golden.

### grill
- **IPA:** /ɡrɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook food with direct strong heat from above or below.
  - *Example:* **Grill** the fish for four minutes on each side.

### broil
- **IPA:** /brɔɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook food with heat from directly above (mainly North American).
  - *Example:* **Broil** the cheese on top until it bubbles.

### blanch
- **IPA:** /blɑːntʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To put vegetables briefly into boiling water, then into cold water.
  - *Example:* **Blanch** the beans before [freezing](20-agriculture-and-food-supply.md#freezing) them.

### caramelize
- **IPA:** /ˈkærəməlaɪz/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To heat sugar or a sugary food until it browns and turns sweet and sticky.
  - *Example:* Slowly **caramelize** the onions over a low heat.

### reduce
- **IPA:** /rɪˈdjuːs/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To boil a liquid so that some evaporates and the flavour becomes stronger.
  - *Example:* **Reduce** the stock by half before adding the cream.

### drain
- **IPA:** /dreɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To let liquid flow away from food.
  - *Example:* **Drain** the pasta and return it to the pan.

### dice
- **IPA:** /daɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cut food into small cubes.
  - *Example:* **Dice** the carrots finely.

### mince
- **IPA:** /mɪns/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cut or grind food, especially meat, into very small pieces.
  - *Example:* **Mince** two cloves of garlic.

### grate
- **IPA:** /ɡreɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To rub food against a rough surface to make small shreds.
  - *Example:* **Grate** the cheese over the top.

### zest
- **IPA:** /zest/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The thin coloured outer skin of a citrus fruit, used for flavour.
  - *Example:* Add the **zest** of one lemon to the batter.

### preheat
- **IPA:** /ˌpriːˈhiːt/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To heat an oven to the required temperature before putting food in.
  - *Example:* **Preheat** the oven to 200 degrees.

### baste
- **IPA:** /beɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To spoon fat or juices over meat while it roasts to keep it moist.
  - *Example:* **Baste** the turkey every thirty minutes.

### rest
- **IPA:** /rest/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To leave cooked meat for a few minutes before cutting so the juices settle.
  - *Example:* Let the steak **rest** before you slice it.

### plate up
- **IPA:** /ˌpleɪt ˈʌp/
- **Meaning 1 (phr v):** To arrange food on plates ready to serve.
  - *Example:* The kitchen **plated up** all forty meals within ten minutes.

### leftovers
- **IPA:** /ˈleftəʊvəz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Food that has not been eaten at a meal and is kept for later.
  - *Example:* Tonight's dinner is **leftovers** from the roast.

### batch cook
- **IPA:** /ˈbætʃ kʊk/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook large quantities of a dish at once to use over several days.
  - *Example:* On Sundays she **batch cooks** meals for the week.

### meal prep
- **IPA:** /ˈmiːl prep/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The preparing of meals or ingredients in advance.
  - *Example:* An hour of **meal prep** saves cooking every evening.

### food processor
- **IPA:** /ˈfuːd ˌprəʊsesə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An electric machine that chops, slices, and mixes food quickly.
  - *Example:* Blitz the breadcrumbs in the **food processor**.

### blender
- **IPA:** /ˈblendə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An electric machine that mixes or purées food and liquid.
  - *Example:* Put the soup through the **blender** until smooth.

### colander
- **IPA:** /ˈkʌləndə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A bowl with holes for draining water from food.
  - *Example:* Tip the pasta into a **colander** in the sink.

### ladle
- **IPA:** /ˈleɪdl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large deep spoon with a long handle, used for serving soup.
  - *Example:* Serve the stew with a **ladle** straight from the pot.

### spatula
- **IPA:** /ˈspætʃələ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A kitchen tool with a broad flat blade for lifting or spreading food.
  - *Example:* Flip the pancake with a **spatula**.

### chopping board
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃɒpɪŋ bɔːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A flat board on which food is cut.
  - *Example:* Use a separate **chopping board** for raw meat.

### non-stick
- **IPA:** /ˌnɒn ˈstɪk/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Having a coating that stops food sticking to it.
  - *Example:* Eggs slide straight out of the **non-stick** pan.

### ovenproof
- **IPA:** /ˈʌvnpruːf/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Able to be used safely in a hot oven.
  - *Example:* Transfer the pan to the oven, if it is **ovenproof**.

### staple food
- **IPA:** /ˈsteɪpl fuːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A basic food eaten regularly that forms a large part of the diet.
  - *Example:* Rice is the **staple food** across much of the region.

### dietary requirement
- **IPA:** /ˈdaɪətəri rɪˌkwaɪəmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A restriction or need in what a person can or should eat.
  - *Example:* The form asks guests to list any **dietary requirements**.

### allergen
- **IPA:** /ˈælədʒən/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A substance, such as nuts or shellfish, that can cause an allergic reaction.
  - *Example:* The menu marks every dish that contains a common **allergen**.

### gluten-free
- **IPA:** /ˌɡluːtn ˈfriː/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Not containing gluten, a protein found in wheat.
  - *Example:* The café offers a **gluten-free** version of most cakes.

### dairy-free
- **IPA:** /ˌdeəri ˈfriː/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Not containing milk or products made from milk.
  - *Example:* A **dairy-free** spread was used in the whole batch.

### plant-based
- **IPA:** /ˌplɑːnt ˈbeɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Made mainly or entirely from plants rather than animal products.
  - *Example:* The chain added three **plant-based** burgers to the menu.

### vegan
- **IPA:** /ˈviːɡən/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Containing no animal products at all, including eggs, milk, and honey.
  - *Example:* The dessert is **vegan**, using coconut cream instead of dairy.

### vegetarian
- **IPA:** /ˌvedʒəˈteəriən/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Containing no meat or fish.
  - *Example:* Half the guests chose the **vegetarian** option.

### organic produce
- **IPA:** /ɔːˌɡænɪk ˈprɒdjuːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Fruit and vegetables grown without artificial chemicals.
  - *Example:* The box scheme [delivers](06-projects-and-deadlines.md#deliver) **organic produce** every Friday.

### farmers market
- **IPA:** /ˈfɑːməz ˌmɑːkɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A market where local growers sell food directly to the public.
  - *Example:* The **farmers market** runs on the square every Saturday morning.

### deli counter
- **IPA:** /ˈdeli ˌkaʊntə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A counter in a shop selling cooked meats, cheeses, and prepared foods.
  - *Example:* She ordered sliced ham at the **deli counter**.

### table service
- **IPA:** /ˈteɪbl ˌsɜːvɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A system where staff bring food and drink to customers at their table.
  - *Example:* The pub switched to **table service** during busy periods.

### set menu
- **IPA:** /ˌset ˈmenjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fixed selection of dishes offered together for one price.
  - *Example:* The lunch **set menu** is two courses for £15.

### à la carte
- **IPA:** /ˌɑː lə ˈkɑːt/
- **Meaning 1 (adj/adv):** With each dish ordered and priced separately, rather than as a set meal.
  - *Example:* In the evening the restaurant is **à la carte** only.

### daily special
- **IPA:** /ˌdeɪli ˈspeʃl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A dish offered on a particular day in addition to the usual menu.
  - *Example:* The **daily special** was written on a board by the door.

### side dish
- **IPA:** /ˈsaɪd dɪʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A smaller dish served alongside the main course.
  - *Example:* The steak comes with a **side dish** of your choice.

### main course
- **IPA:** /ˌmeɪn ˈkɔːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The largest and most important dish of a meal.
  - *Example:* For the **main course** he chose the roast lamb.

### dessert menu
- **IPA:** /dɪˈzɜːt ˌmenjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A list of sweet dishes offered at the end of a meal.
  - *Example:* The waiter brought the **dessert menu** with the coffee.

### house wine
- **IPA:** /ˌhaʊs ˈwaɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The cheapest wine a restaurant offers by the glass or carafe, not named on the list.
  - *Example:* A carafe of the **house wine** came to £12.

### service included
- **IPA:** /ˈsɜːvɪs ɪnˌkluːdɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (phr):** Showing that a charge for service is already added to the bill.
  - *Example:* The [receipt](../toeic-600/03-accounting-and-budgeting.md#5-receipt) said **service included**, so they left no extra tip.

### split the bill
- **IPA:** /ˌsplɪt ðə ˈbɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (phr):** To divide the cost of a meal among the people at the table.
  - *Example:* They agreed to **split the bill** evenly six ways.

### doggy bag
- **IPA:** /ˈdɒɡi bæɡ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A container for taking home food left over from a restaurant meal.
  - *Example:* She asked for a **doggy bag** for the half of pizza she couldn't finish.

### bake
- **IPA:** /beɪk/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook food in an oven using dry heat, especially bread and cakes.
  - *Example:* **Bake** the loaf for 40 minutes until it sounds hollow.

### boil
- **IPA:** /bɔɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook food in water that is bubbling hard.
  - *Example:* **Boil** the eggs for seven minutes for a firm centre.

### fry
- **IPA:** /fraɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To cook food in hot fat or oil.
  - *Example:* **Fry** the bacon until crisp.

### chef
- **IPA:** /ʃef/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A professional cook, especially the person in charge of a restaurant kitchen.
  - *Example:* The **chef** changes the menu with the seasons.

### cookware
- **IPA:** /ˈkʊkweə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Pots, pans, and dishes used for cooking.
  - *Example:* A basic set of **cookware** covers most home recipes.

### tablecloth
- **IPA:** /ˈteɪblklɒθ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A cloth spread over a table, especially for meals.
  - *Example:* A white linen **tablecloth** was laid for the dinner.

### food hygiene
- **IPA:** /ˈfuːd ˌhaɪdʒiːn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The practices that keep food safe to eat and free from harmful bacteria.
  - *Example:* Staff take a **food hygiene** course before working in the kitchen.

### signature dish
- **IPA:** /ˈsɪɡnətʃə dɪʃ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The dish a restaurant or cook is best known for.
  - *Example:* The **signature dish** is a slow-cooked lamb shoulder.

### tasting menu
- **IPA:** /ˈteɪstɪŋ ˌmenjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A set meal of many small courses chosen by the chef.
  - *Example:* The **tasting menu** ran to nine courses over three hours.
