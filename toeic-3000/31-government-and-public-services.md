# 31 — Government & Public Services

> Cross-links jump to another entry in the 3000-word set. **Ctrl/Cmd + click** to follow.
> Words from `../toeic-600/` are left as plain text.

### government
- **IPA:** /ˈɡʌvənmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The group of people with authority to run a country or state.
  - *Example:* The **government** announced new [spending](35-shopping-and-consumer-life.md#spending) on roads and rail.

### cabinet
- **IPA:** /ˈkæbɪnət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The group of senior ministers who make government policy.
  - *Example:* The plan was [approved](03-management-and-leadership.md#approve) at the weekly **cabinet** [meeting](../toeic-600/06-meetings-and-presentations.md#1-meeting).

### ministry
- **IPA:** /ˈmɪnɪstri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A government department responsible for a particular area, such as health or defence.
  - *Example:* The **ministry** of transport [oversees](03-management-and-leadership.md#oversee) the whole rail [network](../toeic-600/13-technology-and-computers.md#5-network).

### department
- **IPA:** /dɪˈpɑːtmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A part of a government responsible for a specific area of policy.
  - *Example:* The **department** for education sets the national [curriculum](30-education-and-training.md#curriculum).

### agency
- **IPA:** /ˈeɪdʒənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A government body set up to carry out a particular function.
  - *Example:* An **agency** was created to manage the country's borders.

### bureau
- **IPA:** /ˈbjʊərəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A government department or office dealing with a specific matter.
  - *Example:* The [statistics](07-data-reports-and-analysis.md#statistic) **bureau** publishes the monthly jobs figures.

### council
- **IPA:** /ˈkaʊnsl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A group elected to govern a town, city, or region.
  - *Example:* The **council** voted to pedestrianise the market square.

### municipality
- **IPA:** /mjuːˌnɪsɪˈpæləti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A town or city with its own local government.
  - *Example:* Each **municipality** collects its own [property tax](15-taxation-and-regulation.md#property-tax).

### legislature
- **IPA:** /ˈledʒɪslətʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The part of government that makes laws.
  - *Example:* The bill passed the **legislature** after three readings.

### parliament
- **IPA:** /ˈpɑːləmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The elected body that makes a country's laws and holds the government to account.
  - *Example:* **Parliament** debated the budget for two days.

### congress
- **IPA:** /ˈkɒŋɡres/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The national law-making body in some countries, such as the United States.
  - *Example:* The measure still needs the [approval](06-projects-and-deadlines.md#approval) of **congress**.

### senate
- **IPA:** /ˈsenət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of the two parts of a law-making body in many countries.
  - *Example:* The **senate** blocked the bill in a close vote.

### representative
- **IPA:** /ˌreprɪˈzentətɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person elected to speak and act for others in a legislature.
  - *Example:* Voters wrote to their **representative** about the factory closure.

### constituency
- **IPA:** /kənˈstɪtjuənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An area whose voters elect one representative; those voters as a group.
  - *Example:* The new road runs through three **constituencies**.

### electorate
- **IPA:** /ɪˈlektərət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** All the people entitled to vote in an election.
  - *Example:* Turnout was low, with only half the **electorate** voting.

### ballot
- **IPA:** /ˈbælət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A system of secret voting, or the paper used for it.
  - *Example:* Members will decide the issue in a postal **ballot**.

### referendum
- **IPA:** /ˌrefəˈrendəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A direct vote by the public on a single political question.
  - *Example:* A **referendum** was held on whether to join the trade bloc.

### mandate
- **IPA:** /ˈmændeɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The authority to act, given by voters to an elected government.
  - *Example:* The party claimed a clear **mandate** for the reforms.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To officially require something.
  - *Example:* The law **mandates** seat belts in all coaches.

### coalition
- **IPA:** /ˌkəʊəˈlɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A government formed by two or more parties working together.
  - *Example:* The **coalition** held a narrow [majority](10-problem-solving-and-decision-making.md#majority) for four years.

### opposition
- **IPA:** /ˌɒpəˈzɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The political parties that are not in government.
  - *Example:* The **opposition** demanded an [inquiry](../toeic-600/07-correspondence-and-email.md#5-inquiry) into the [spending](35-shopping-and-consumer-life.md#spending).

### bill
- **IPA:** /bɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written proposal for a new law, before it is passed.
  - *Example:* The housing **bill** goes to a vote next week.

### act
- **IPA:** /ækt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A law that has been formally passed by a legislature.
  - *Example:* The Clean Air **Act** set limits on factory [emissions](28-environment-and-sustainability.md#emissions).

### decree
- **IPA:** /dɪˈkriː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official order that has the force of law, issued by a ruler or government.
  - *Example:* Prices were frozen by [government](31-government-and-public-services.md#government) **decree**.

### ratification
- **IPA:** /ˌrætɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Formal approval that makes an agreement or treaty official.
  - *Example:* The trade deal awaits **ratification** by both [parliaments](31-government-and-public-services.md#parliament).

### filibuster
- **IPA:** /ˈfɪlɪbʌstə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A long speech used to delay or block a vote in a legislature.
  - *Example:* A **filibuster** kept the chamber [sitting](23-hospitality-and-tourism-industry.md#sitting) through the night.

### lobbyist
- **IPA:** /ˈlɒbiɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person paid to influence politicians on behalf of a group or industry.
  - *Example:* Industry **lobbyists** pushed for a longer phase-in period.

### civil servant
- **IPA:** /ˌsɪvl ˈsɜːvənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person employed by the government to carry out its work, not an elected official.
  - *Example:* A senior **civil servant** briefed the new [minister](31-government-and-public-services.md#minister).

### bureaucracy
- **IPA:** /bjʊəˈrɒkrəsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A system of government or administration with many officials and complex rules.
  - *Example:* [Applicants](../toeic-600/04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#2-applicant) complained about the **bureaucracy** involved in getting a permit.

### red tape
- **IPA:** /ˌred ˈteɪp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Official rules and paperwork that seem unnecessary and slow things down.
  - *Example:* The reform aims to cut **red tape** for small businesses.

### public sector
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk ˈsektə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The part of the economy controlled by the government.
  - *Example:* **Public sector** pay was frozen for two years.

### welfare
- **IPA:** /ˈwelfeə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Government support for people in need, such as payments for the unemployed.
  - *Example:* Changes to **welfare** affected millions of households.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The health and wellbeing of a person or group.
  - *Example:* The charity works for the **welfare** of stray animals.

### state benefit
- **IPA:** /ˈsteɪt ˌbenɪfɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A regular payment made by the government to people who qualify for support.
  - *Example:* She claimed a **state benefit** while looking for work.

### pension scheme
- **IPA:** /ˈpenʃn skiːm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement for paying people an income after they retire.
  - *Example:* The state **pension scheme** is funded by current workers' taxes.

### social security
- **IPA:** /ˌsəʊʃl sɪˈkjʊərəti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A government system of payments to support people who are old, ill, or out of work.
  - *Example:* **Social security** [contributions](../toeic-600/18-employee-benefits-and-insurance.md#27-contribution) are deducted from every wage.

### public spending
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk ˈspendɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money spent by the government on services and infrastructure.
  - *Example:* **Public spending** on health rose faster than the [economy](11-economics-and-markets.md#economy) grew.

### national debt
- **IPA:** /ˌnæʃnəl ˈdet/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The total amount of money a government owes.
  - *Example:* Years of [deficits](../toeic-600/03-accounting-and-budgeting.md#14-deficit) pushed the **national debt** to a record level.

### budget allocation
- **IPA:** /ˈbʌdʒɪt ˌæləˈkeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of money assigned to a department or purpose.
  - *Example:* The schools' **budget allocation** was increased by 4%.

### appropriation
- **IPA:** /əˌprəʊpriˈeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money formally set aside by a legislature for a particular use.
  - *Example:* The **appropriation** for flood defences passed without [objection](09-negotiation-and-persuasion.md#objection).

### infrastructure
- **IPA:** /ˈɪnfrəstrʌktʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The basic physical systems of a country, such as roads, power, and water.
  - *Example:* Decades of underinvestment left the **infrastructure** creaking.

### sanitation
- **IPA:** /ˌsænɪˈteɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Systems for keeping places clean, especially the removal of sewage and waste.
  - *Example:* Better **sanitation** cut the rate of disease in the district.

### public transport
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk ˈtrænspɔːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Buses, trains, and other shared transport available to everyone for a fare.
  - *Example:* Cheap **public transport** kept cars out of the city centre.

### law enforcement
- **IPA:** /ˈlɔː ɪnˌfɔːsmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The system and agencies that make sure laws are obeyed.
  - *Example:* **Law enforcement** was given new powers to seize illegal goods.

### judiciary
- **IPA:** /dʒuˈdɪʃəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The judges and courts of a country, taken as a whole.
  - *Example:* An independent **judiciary** checks the power of the [government](31-government-and-public-services.md#government).

### regulator
- **IPA:** /ˈreɡjəleɪtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official body that supervises an industry and enforces its rules.
  - *Example:* The energy **regulator** capped the [standard](../toeic-600/12-quality-control-and-standards.md#3-standard) tariff.

### watchdog
- **IPA:** /ˈwɒtʃdɒɡ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A group that checks that companies or officials behave properly.
  - *Example:* A [consumer](../toeic-600/08-marketing-and-advertising.md#6-consumer) **watchdog** reported the airline for hidden fees.

### ombudsman
- **IPA:** /ˈɒmbʊdzmən/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official who investigates complaints against organisations or the government.
  - *Example:* The **ombudsman** ruled in the customer's favour.

### transparency
- **IPA:** /trænsˈpærənsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Openness about decisions and spending, so that people can see what is done and why.
  - *Example:* Publishing all contracts improved **transparency**.

### corruption
- **IPA:** /kəˈrʌpʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Dishonest or illegal behaviour by people in power, especially taking bribes.
  - *Example:* An [inquiry](../toeic-600/07-correspondence-and-email.md#5-inquiry) uncovered **corruption** in the awarding of [licences](01-career-and-professional-development.md#licence).

### governance
- **IPA:** /ˈɡʌvənəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The way an organisation or country is run and held to account.
  - *Example:* Weak **governance** allowed the funds to be misused.

### diplomacy
- **IPA:** /dɪˈpləʊməsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The management of relations between countries.
  - *Example:* The [dispute](../toeic-600/20-customer-service-and-complaints.md#28-dispute) was settled through quiet **diplomacy**.

### public consultation
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk ˌkɒnslˈteɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A process of asking the public for views before a decision is made.
  - *Example:* The road plan went out for **public consultation** for six weeks.

### town hall meeting
- **IPA:** /ˌtaʊn hɔːl ˈmiːtɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A public meeting where officials answer questions from local people.
  - *Example:* The mayor faced angry residents at the **town hall meeting**.

### freedom of information
- **IPA:** /ˌfriːdəm əv ˌɪnfəˈmeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The legal right of the public to see records held by government bodies.
  - *Example:* A **freedom of information** request revealed the true cost.

### minister
- **IPA:** /ˈmɪnɪstə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A senior member of government in charge of a department.
  - *Example:* The **minister** for housing announced the new building targets.

### election
- **IPA:** /ɪˈlekʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A process in which people vote to choose a person for a position.
  - *Example:* A general **election** must be held within five years.

### vote
- **IPA:** /vəʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A formal expression of choice between candidates or options.
  - *Example:* The measure passed by three **votes**.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To express a choice in an election or decision.
  - *Example:* Members **voted** to accept the pay offer.

### voter
- **IPA:** /ˈvəʊtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who has the right to vote, or who votes in an election.
  - *Example:* **Voters** in rural areas backed the transport plan.

### public service
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk ˈsɜːvɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A service such as health, education, or transport provided by the government for everyone.
  - *Example:* Funding cuts put pressure on every **public service**.

### public official
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk əˈfɪʃl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who holds a government position or does government work.
  - *Example:* A **public official** must [declare](40-travel-commuting-and-getting-around.md#declare) any gifts received.

### government policy
- **IPA:** /ˈɡʌvənmənt ˌpɒləsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The set of plans and aims that a government decides to follow.
  - *Example:* **Government policy** now favours home [insulation](18-construction-and-building-trades.md#insulation) grants.

### public funds
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk ˈfʌndz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money raised through taxes and used by the government.
  - *Example:* The stadium was built partly with **public funds**.

### government spending
- **IPA:** /ˈɡʌvənmənt ˌspendɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The total amount the government spends on services, benefits, and investment.
  - *Example:* **Government spending** rose sharply during the crisis.

### elected official
- **IPA:** /ɪˌlektɪd əˈfɪʃl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person chosen by voters to hold a public position.
  - *Example:* Every **elected official** must publish their [expenses](../toeic-600/02-finance-and-banking.md#20-expense).

### government body
- **IPA:** /ˈɡʌvənmənt ˌbɒdi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official organisation that is part of, or set up by, the government.
  - *Example:* A **government body** now sets safety [standards](../toeic-600/12-quality-control-and-standards.md#3-standard) for e-bikes.

### public administration
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk ədˌmɪnɪˈstreɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The organising and running of government services and departments.
  - *Example:* She studied **public administration** before joining the [civil service](31-government-and-public-services.md#civil-service).

### state
- **IPA:** /steɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A country or its government, seen as an organised political body.
  - *Example:* The railway was owned by the **state** for most of the last century.

### mayor
- **IPA:** /meə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The elected head of a town or city government.
  - *Example:* The **mayor** promised 5,000 new homes in her first term.

### governor
- **IPA:** /ˈɡʌvənə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The elected or appointed head of a state, region, or institution.
  - *Example:* The **governor** [declared](40-travel-commuting-and-getting-around.md#declare) a state of emergency after the flood.

### cabinet minister
- **IPA:** /ˌkæbɪnət ˈmɪnɪstə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A senior minister who sits in the cabinet and helps set government policy.
  - *Example:* A **cabinet minister** resigned over the [spending](35-shopping-and-consumer-life.md#spending) decision.

### public office
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk ˈɒfɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A position of authority in government, held by election or appointment.
  - *Example:* He left business to run for **public office**.

### government scheme
- **IPA:** /ˈɡʌvənmənt skiːm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An organised plan run by the government to achieve a particular aim.
  - *Example:* A **government scheme** helps first-time buyers with a [deposit](../toeic-600/02-finance-and-banking.md#2-deposit).

### policymaker
- **IPA:** /ˈpɒləsimeɪkə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person responsible for creating or shaping government policy.
  - *Example:* The report was written to guide **policymakers** on housing.

### civil service
- **IPA:** /ˌsɪvl ˈsɜːvɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The body of people employed by the government to run its departments.
  - *Example:* The **civil service** provides continuity when [governments](31-government-and-public-services.md#government) change.

### by-election
- **IPA:** /ˈbaɪ ɪˌlekʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An election held between general elections to fill a single vacant seat.
  - *Example:* The party lost the seat in a **by-election**.

### public sector worker
- **IPA:** /ˌpʌblɪk sektə ˈwɜːkə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person employed by the government, such as a teacher, nurse, or clerk.
  - *Example:* **Public sector workers** were offered a below-inflation pay rise.
