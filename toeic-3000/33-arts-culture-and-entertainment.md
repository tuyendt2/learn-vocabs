# 33 — Arts, Culture & Entertainment

### exhibition
- **IPA:** /ˌeksɪˈbɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A public display of art or other objects.
  - *Example:* The photography **exhibition** runs until the end of March.

### gallery
- **IPA:** /ˈɡæləri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A room or building where works of art are shown, often for sale.
  - *Example:* A small **gallery** near the [harbour](21-transportation-and-shipping.md#harbour) shows local painters.

### curator
- **IPA:** /kjʊəˈreɪtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who selects and looks after the works in a museum or exhibition.
  - *Example:* The **curator** arranged the paintings by decade.

### collection
- **IPA:** /kəˈlekʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A group of works of art or objects kept together.
  - *Example:* The museum's **collection** includes 2,000 prints.

### artefact
- **IPA:** /ˈɑːtɪfækt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An object made by a person, especially one of historical interest.
  - *Example:* Each **artefact** is stored in a climate-controlled case.

### sculpture
- **IPA:** /ˈskʌlptʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A work of art made by shaping material such as stone, metal, or wood.
  - *Example:* A bronze **sculpture** stands in the courtyard.

### portrait
- **IPA:** /ˈpɔːtrət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A painting, drawing, or photograph of a person.
  - *Example:* The **portrait** of the [founder](02-company-organization-and-departments.md#founder) hangs in the boardroom.

### canvas
- **IPA:** /ˈkænvəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A strong cloth used as a surface for oil painting; a finished painting on it.
  - *Example:* She works on a large **canvas** stretched over a wooden frame.

### easel
- **IPA:** /ˈiːzl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A stand that holds a canvas while an artist paints.
  - *Example:* The painting was still on the **easel** when he died.

### palette
- **IPA:** /ˈpælət/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A board on which an artist mixes colours; the range of colours used.
  - *Example:* His late work uses a much darker **palette**.

### brushwork
- **IPA:** /ˈbrʌʃwɜːk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The way an artist applies paint with a brush.
  - *Example:* The loose **brushwork** gives the scene a sense of movement.

### installation
- **IPA:** /ˌɪnstəˈleɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large art work built in a space, often using several materials or media.
  - *Example:* The **installation** fills an entire room with hanging lights.

### mural
- **IPA:** /ˈmjʊərəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large painting done directly on a wall.
  - *Example:* Local artists painted a **mural** on the side of the [library](30-education-and-training.md#library).

### retrospective show
- **IPA:** /ˌretrəˈspektɪv ʃəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An exhibition covering the whole career of an artist.
  - *Example:* The **retrospective show** brought together fifty years of her work.

### gallery opening
- **IPA:** /ˈɡæləri ˌəʊpənɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An event marking the start of a new exhibition, usually with invited guests.
  - *Example:* Wine and small talk filled the **gallery opening**.

### private view
- **IPA:** /ˌpraɪvət ˈvjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A showing of an exhibition to invited guests before it opens to the public.
  - *Example:* Collectors bought three pieces at the **private view**.

### auction house
- **IPA:** /ˈɔːkʃn haʊs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A company that sells art and valuables to the highest bidder.
  - *Example:* The painting went to a London **auction house**.

### bidder
- **IPA:** /ˈbɪdə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who offers a price at an auction.
  - *Example:* Two **bidders** on the phone drove the price up.

### lot
- **IPA:** /lɒt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An item or set of items sold together at an auction.
  - *Example:* **Lot** 34 was a pair of antique chairs.

### provenance
- **IPA:** /ˈprɒvənəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The record of where a work of art has been and who has owned it.
  - *Example:* Clear **provenance** raised the value of the [sculpture](33-arts-culture-and-entertainment.md#sculpture).

### restoration
- **IPA:** /ˌrestəˈreɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The careful repair of a damaged work of art or building.
  - *Example:* The **restoration** of the ceiling took three years.

### art conservation
- **IPA:** /ˈɑːt ˌkɒnsəveɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The work of protecting and preserving works of art from damage and decay.
  - *Example:* An **art conservation** lab treats the museum's oldest pieces.

### repertoire
- **IPA:** /ˈrepətwɑː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The set of pieces a performer or company is ready to present.
  - *Example:* The [orchestra](33-arts-culture-and-entertainment.md#orchestra) added three modern works to its **repertoire**.

### ensemble
- **IPA:** /ɒnˈsɒmbl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A group of musicians, actors, or dancers who perform together.
  - *Example:* A small **ensemble** played in the hotel lobby.

### orchestra
- **IPA:** /ˈɔːkɪstrə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large group of musicians playing many kinds of instrument together.
  - *Example:* The city **orchestra** gave a free concert in the park.

### conductor
- **IPA:** /kənˈdʌktə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The person who directs an orchestra or choir during a performance.
  - *Example:* A guest **conductor** led the evening's programme.

### score
- **IPA:** /skɔː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The written form of a piece of music showing all the parts.
  - *Example:* The composer's original **score** is kept in the archive.

### libretto
- **IPA:** /lɪˈbretəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The words of an opera or long musical work.
  - *Example:* The **libretto** was translated for the English [audience](33-arts-culture-and-entertainment.md#audience).

### rehearsal
- **IPA:** /rɪˈhɜːsl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A practice session before a public performance.
  - *Example:* The dress **rehearsal** is the night before opening.

### matinee
- **IPA:** /ˈmætɪneɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An afternoon performance of a play, film, or concert.
  - *Example:* The Saturday **matinee** is popular with families.

### box office
- **IPA:** /ˈbɒks ˌɒfɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The place where tickets are sold; also, ticket sales as a measure of success.
  - *Example:* The show broke records at the **box office**.

### premiere
- **IPA:** /ˈpremieə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The first public performance or showing of a work.
  - *Example:* Stars walked the red carpet at the film's **premiere**.

### encore
- **IPA:** /ˈɒŋkɔː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An extra piece performed after the planned programme, in response to applause.
  - *Example:* The band played two songs as an **encore**.

### standing ovation
- **IPA:** /ˌstændɪŋ əʊˈveɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Applause given while the audience stands, to show great appreciation.
  - *Example:* The [performance](33-arts-culture-and-entertainment.md#performance) ended in a long **standing ovation**.

### playwright
- **IPA:** /ˈpleɪraɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who writes plays.
  - *Example:* The **playwright** attended the first three nights.

### screenplay
- **IPA:** /ˈskriːnpleɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The written script of a film, including dialogue and directions.
  - *Example:* The **screenplay** was based on a true story.

### script
- **IPA:** /skrɪpt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The written text of a play, film, or broadcast.
  - *Example:* The actors received the final **script** a week before filming.

### cast
- **IPA:** /kɑːst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** All the actors in a play or film.
  - *Example:* The **cast** took a bow together at the end.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To choose actors for parts.
  - *Example:* She was **cast** as the lead after one audition.

### understudy
- **IPA:** /ˈʌndəstʌdi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An actor who learns a role in order to replace the main performer if needed.
  - *Example:* The **understudy** went on when the star fell ill.

### stagehand
- **IPA:** /ˈsteɪdʒhænd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A worker who moves scenery and props during a performance.
  - *Example:* **Stagehands** changed the whole set in ninety seconds.

### backstage
- **IPA:** /ˌbækˈsteɪdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (adv/adj):** In the area behind the stage, out of the audience's sight.
  - *Example:* The director gave notes **backstage** before the show.

### props
- **IPA:** /prɒps/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Objects used by actors during a performance.
  - *Example:* A missing **props** table delayed the second act.

### set design
- **IPA:** /ˈset dɪˌzaɪn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The planning and building of the scenery for a stage or film production.
  - *Example:* The **set design** turned the small stage into a busy street.

### lighting rig
- **IPA:** /ˈlaɪtɪŋ rɪɡ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The frame and set of lamps used to light a stage or set.
  - *Example:* The **lighting rig** took a day to hang and focus.

### sound check
- **IPA:** /ˈsaʊnd tʃek/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A test of the audio equipment before a performance.
  - *Example:* The band arrived early for the **sound check**.

### choreography
- **IPA:** /ˌkɒriˈɒɡrəfi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The arrangement of steps and movements for a dance.
  - *Example:* The **choreography** mixes ballet with street dance.

### troupe
- **IPA:** /truːp/

  - *Example:* A circus **troupe** performed in the town square.

### venue hire
- **IPA:** /ˈvenjuː ˌhaɪə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The cost or arrangement of renting a place for an event.
  - *Example:* **Venue hire** was the biggest single item in the [festival](33-arts-culture-and-entertainment.md#festival) budget.

### ticketing
- **IPA:** /ˈtɪkɪtɪŋ/

### season pass
- **IPA:** /ˈsiːzn pɑːs/

### subscription series
- **IPA:** /səbˈskrɪpʃn ˌsɪəriːz/

### festival
- **IPA:** /ˈfestɪvl/

### headliner

### line-up

### gig

### residency

### touring
- **IPA:** /ˈtʊərɪŋ/

### merchandise stall
- **IPA:** /ˈmɜːtʃəndaɪs stɔːl/

### streaming rights
