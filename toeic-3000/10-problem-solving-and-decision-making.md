### issue
- **IPA:** /ˈɪʃuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A problem or matter that needs to be dealt with or discussed.
  - *Example:* The main **issue** is that deliveries keep arriving late.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To officially supply or send out something.
  - *Example:* The office will **issue** new passes on Monday.

### problem
- **IPA:** /ˈprɒbləm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A situation that is difficult and needs to be solved.
  - *Example:* We traced the **problem** to a single faulty sensor.

### challenge
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃælɪndʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A difficult task that tests someone's ability.
  - *Example:* Hiring skilled staff quickly is the biggest **challenge**.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To question whether something is true or right.
  - *Example:* She **challenged** the assumption behind the forecast.

### hitch
