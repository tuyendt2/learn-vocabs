### tourism
- **IPA:** /ˈtʊərɪzəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The business of providing services for people who travel for pleasure.
  - *Example:* **Tourism** brings in a quarter of the island's income.

### inbound
- **IPA:** /ˈɪnbaʊnd/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Relating to travellers coming into a country or area.
  - *Example:* **Inbound** tourism recovered strongly after the border reopened.

### outbound
- **IPA:** /ˈaʊtbaʊnd/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Relating to travellers leaving a country or area to visit somewhere else.
  - *Example:* A weaker currency cut **outbound** travel by locals.

### resort
- **IPA:** /rɪˈzɔːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A place where many people go on holiday, with hotels and things to do.
  - *Example:* The ski **resort** employs 2,000 staff in winter.

### lodge
- **IPA:** /lɒdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small country house or hotel, often used by people doing outdoor activities.
  - *Example:* The safari **lodge** has just twelve rooms.

### hostel
- **IPA:** /ˈhɒstl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A cheap place to stay, often with shared rooms, popular with young travellers.
  - *Example:* The **hostel** offers a bed in a shared room for €20 a night.

### guesthouse
- **IPA:** /ˈɡesthaʊs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A private house that offers rooms to paying visitors, smaller than a hotel.
  - *Example:* They ran a seaside **guesthouse** for thirty years.

### bed and breakfast
- **IPA:** /ˌbed ən ˈbrekfəst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small lodging that provides a room for the night and a morning meal.
  - *Example:* We stayed at a **bed and breakfast** on the coast road.

### timeshare
- **IPA:** /ˈtaɪmʃeə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement where several people share the right to use a holiday property at different times.
  - *Example:* They own a two-week **timeshare** in the mountains each summer.

### all-inclusive
- **IPA:** /ˌɔːl ɪnˈkluːsɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Covering meals, drinks, and activities in a single holiday price.
  - *Example:* The **all-inclusive** package meant no extra bills at the end.

### package tour
- **IPA:** /ˈpækɪdʒ tʊə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A holiday in which travel, accommodation, and some meals are sold together for one price.
  - *Example:* The **package tour** included flights, a hotel, and two day trips.

### sightseeing
- **IPA:** /ˈsaɪtsiːɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The activity of visiting interesting places as a tourist.
  - *Example:* We spent the morning **sightseeing** in the old town.

### landmark
- **IPA:** /ˈlændmɑːk/

### attraction

### tour operator

### travel agency
