# 39 — Leisure, Hobbies & Sports

### pastime
- **IPA:** /ˈpɑːstaɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An activity done regularly for enjoyment in free time.
  - *Example:* Fishing has been his favourite **pastime** for years.

### hobby
- **IPA:** /ˈhɒbi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An activity done for pleasure when not working.
  - *Example:* She turned her **hobby** of baking into a small business.

### recreation
- **IPA:** /ˌrekriˈeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Activity done for enjoyment and relaxation.
  - *Example:* The park is used for sport and general **recreation**.

### leisure time
- **IPA:** /ˈleʒə taɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Time when you are not working and can do what you enjoy.
  - *Example:* Most of his **leisure time** goes on cycling.

### unwind
- **IPA:** /ˌʌnˈwaɪnd/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To relax after a period of work or stress.
  - *Example:* She **unwinds** by walking the dog after work.

### pursuit
- **IPA:** /pəˈsjuːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An activity that someone spends time on, especially for interest or pleasure.
  - *Example:* Painting is a quiet **pursuit** he took up in retirement.

### enthusiast
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈθjuːziæst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who is very interested in and keen on an activity.
  - *Example:* The forum is full of vintage-car **enthusiasts**.

### amateur
- **IPA:** /ˈæmətə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who does an activity for pleasure, not as a paid job.
  - *Example:* The competition is open to **amateurs** only.

### hobbyist
- **IPA:** /ˈhɒbiɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who does a particular hobby.
  - *Example:* The [magazine](32-media-news-and-publishing.md#magazine) is aimed at model-railway **hobbyists**.

### membership
- **IPA:** /ˈmembəʃɪp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The state of belonging to a club or organisation.
  - *Example:* **Membership** of the tennis club includes coaching.

### season ticket
- **IPA:** /ˈsiːzn ˌtɪkɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A ticket that allows entry to all of a team's or venue's events for a season.
  - *Example:* He renews his **season ticket** every summer.

### tournament
- **IPA:** /ˈtʊənəmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A competition in which players or teams compete until one winner is left.
  - *Example:* The chess **tournament** ran over a weekend.

### league
- **IPA:** /liːɡ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A group of teams that play each other over a season for a title.
  - *Example:* The club was [promoted](../toeic-600/08-marketing-and-advertising.md#7-promote) to the top **league**.

### knockout
- **IPA:** /ˈnɒkaʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A competition in which the loser of each round is out.
  - *Example:* After the group stage comes a straight **knockout**.

### heat
- **IPA:** /hiːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An early round of a race to decide who goes through to the next stage.
  - *Example:* She won her **heat** and qualified for the final.

### qualifier
- **IPA:** /ˈkwɒlɪfaɪə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A match or round that decides who enters the main competition.
  - *Example:* The team lost in the **qualifier** and missed the [tournament](39-leisure-hobbies-and-sports.md#tournament).

### semifinal
- **IPA:** /ˌsemiˈfaɪnl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of the two matches whose winners meet in the final.
  - *Example:* They reached the **semifinal** for the first time.

### final
- **IPA:** /ˈfaɪnl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The last match of a competition, deciding the winner.
  - *Example:* Tickets for the **final** sold out in minutes.

### medal
- **IPA:** /ˈmedl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A piece of metal given as a prize for winning or taking part.
  - *Example:* She won a silver **medal** in the relay.

### podium
- **IPA:** /ˈpəʊdiəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A raised platform on which the top three finishers stand.
  - *Example:* A strong last lap put him on the **podium**.

### personal best
- **IPA:** /ˌpɜːsənl ˈbest/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The best result a person has ever achieved in an event.
  - *Example:* He beat his **personal best** by two seconds.

### warm-up
- **IPA:** /ˈwɔːm ʌp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Gentle exercise done before an activity to prepare the body.
  - *Example:* A proper **warm-up** reduces the risk of injury.

### cool-down
- **IPA:** /ˈkuːl daʊn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Gentle exercise done after activity to let the body recover.
  - *Example:* The class ends with a five-minute **cool-down**.

### stretching
- **IPA:** /ˈstretʃɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Exercises that lengthen the muscles to improve flexibility.
  - *Example:* Daily **stretching** eased his stiff back.

### stamina
- **IPA:** /ˈstæmɪnə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The physical or mental strength to keep going for a long time.
  - *Example:* Long runs built up her **stamina** for the marathon.

### endurance
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈdjʊərəns/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The ability to keep doing something difficult over a long period.
  - *Example:* The race is a test of **endurance** more than speed.

### agility
- **IPA:** /əˈdʒɪləti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The ability to move quickly and easily.
  - *Example:* The drills are designed to improve **agility** and balance.

### coordination
- **IPA:** /kəʊˌɔːdɪˈneɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The ability to use different parts of the body together smoothly.
  - *Example:* Juggling is good for hand-eye **coordination**.

### referee
- **IPA:** /ˌrefəˈriː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official who controls a match and enforces the rules.
  - *Example:* The **referee** stopped play to check the injury.

### umpire
- **IPA:** /ˈʌmpaɪə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official who makes decisions in sports such as tennis and cricket.
  - *Example:* The **umpire** called the ball out.

### linesman
- **IPA:** /ˈlaɪnzmən/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official who helps the referee by watching the lines of the field.
  - *Example:* The **linesman** raised his flag for offside.

### spectator
- **IPA:** /spekˈteɪtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who watches a sport or event.
  - *Example:* Thousands of **spectators** filled the stands.

### grandstand
- **IPA:** /ˈɡrænstænd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The main seating area at a sports ground, usually covered.
  - *Example:* Rain sent everyone in the open seats toward the **grandstand**.

### terrace
- **IPA:** /ˈterəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A set of wide steps where standing spectators watch a match.
  - *Example:* The old **terrace** was replaced with seats for safety.

### kick-off
- **IPA:** /ˈkɪk ɒf/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The start of a football match, or its scheduled start time.
  - *Example:* **Kick-off** is at three o'clock.

### half-time
- **IPA:** /ˌhɑːf ˈtaɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The short break in the middle of a match.
  - *Example:* The score was level at **half-time**.

### full-time
- **IPA:** /ˌfʊl ˈtaɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The end of a match when the referee stops play.
  - *Example:* They scored again just before **full-time**.

### extra time
- **IPA:** /ˌekstrə ˈtaɪm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An additional period played when a knockout match is level.
  - *Example:* The tie went to **extra time** and then penalties.

### penalty shootout
- **IPA:** /ˈpenəlti ˌʃuːtaʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A way of deciding a drawn match by each team taking penalty kicks.
  - *Example:* They lost the **penalty shootout** five to four.

### draw
- **IPA:** /drɔː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A result in which both sides have the same score.
  - *Example:* The match ended in a goalless **draw**.

### fixture list
- **IPA:** /ˈfɪkstʃə lɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The schedule of matches a team will play in a season.
  - *Example:* The new **fixture list** is published in June.

### transfer window
- **IPA:** /ˈtrænsfɜː ˌwɪndəʊ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The period during which clubs are allowed to buy and sell players.
  - *Example:* The club signed two strikers before the **transfer window** closed.

### coaching staff
- **IPA:** /ˈkəʊtʃɪŋ stɑːf/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The trainers and assistants who prepare a team.
  - *Example:* The new manager brought his own **coaching staff**.

### training ground
- **IPA:** /ˈtreɪnɪŋ ɡraʊnd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The place where a sports team practises.
  - *Example:* The players reported to the **training ground** at nine.

### gym membership
- **IPA:** /ˈdʒɪm ˌmembəʃɪp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A paid arrangement allowing regular use of a gym.
  - *Example:* Her **gym membership** includes classes and the pool.

### personal trainer
- **IPA:** /ˌpɜːsənl ˈtreɪnə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fitness expert who plans and supervises a person's exercise.
  - *Example:* A **personal trainer** helped him [recover](../toeic-600/17-health-and-medical.md#12-recover) strength after the injury.

### workout
- **IPA:** /ˈwɜːkaʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A session of physical exercise.
  - *Example:* A short morning **workout** set him up for the day.

### circuit
- **IPA:** /ˈsɜːkɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A series of exercises done one after another with little rest.
  - *Example:* The class does a **circuit** of eight stations.

### treadmill
- **IPA:** /ˈtredmɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A machine with a moving belt for walking or running indoors.
  - *Example:* She runs on the **treadmill** when it rains.

### rowing machine
- **IPA:** /ˈrəʊɪŋ məˌʃiːn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An exercise machine that copies the action of rowing a boat.
  - *Example:* Ten minutes on the **rowing machine** works the whole body.

### yoga mat
- **IPA:** /ˈjəʊɡə mæt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A thin cushioned mat used for floor exercises and yoga.
  - *Example:* She keeps a **yoga mat** rolled up behind the door.

### hiking trail
- **IPA:** /ˈhaɪkɪŋ treɪl/

  - *Example:* The **hiking trail** climbs to a viewpoint over the lake.

### campsite
- **IPA:** /ˈkæmpsaɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A place where people can pitch tents or park a caravan.
  - *Example:* The **campsite** by the river has showers and a small shop.

### pitch a tent
- **IPA:** /ˌpɪtʃ ə ˈtent/
- **Meaning 1 (phr):** To put up a tent.
  - *Example:* They **pitched a tent** on a flat spot near the trees.

### kayak
- **IPA:** /ˈkaɪæk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A narrow covered boat for one person, moved with a double-bladed paddle.
  - *Example:* He took the **kayak** out on the calm morning water.

### paddle
- **IPA:** /ˈpædl/

  - *Example:* One **paddle** stroke each side keeps the kayak straight.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To move a small boat through water with a paddle.
  - *Example:* They **paddled** across to the island and back.

### angling
- **IPA:** /ˈæŋɡlɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The sport of catching fish with a rod, line, and hook.
  - *Example:* **Angling** on the canal needs a day permit.

### allotment
- **IPA:** /əˈlɒtmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small piece of public land rented to grow vegetables and flowers.
  - *Example:* He grows all his own beans on the **allotment**.

### potting shed
- **IPA:** /ˈpɒtɪŋ ʃed/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small building where gardening is done and tools are kept.
  - *Example:* She sows the seeds in trays in the **potting shed**.

### knitting
- **IPA:** /ˈnɪtɪŋ/

### crochet

### woodwork

  - *Example:* He does **woodwork** in the garage at weekends.

### model-making

### photography club

### book club
- **IPA:** /ˈbʊk klʌb/

  - *Example:* Her **book club** picks a new title every month.

### board game night

### jigsaw

### sport

### fitness

### leisure centre
- **IPA:** /ˈleʒə ˌsentə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A public building with a pool, gym, and sports halls.
  - *Example:* The **leisure centre** runs cheap classes for pensioners.

### sports club
- **IPA:** /ˈspɔːts klʌb/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An organisation whose members meet to play a particular sport.
  - *Example:* He joined the local **sports club** to play five-a-side.
