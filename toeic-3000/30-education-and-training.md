### curriculum
- **IPA:** /kəˈrɪkjələm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The complete set of courses offered by a school or covered in a subject.
  - *Example:* Coding was added to the primary school **curriculum**.

### syllabus
- **IPA:** /ˈsɪləbəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An outline of the topics and requirements of one course.
  - *Example:* The **syllabus** lists the reading for every week.

### module
- **IPA:** /ˈmɒdjuːl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A separate unit of study that forms part of a course.
  - *Example:* [Students](30-education-and-training.md#student) choose two optional **modules** in the second year.

### unit
- **IPA:** /ˈjuːnɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A section of a course covering one topic.
  - *Example:* The next **unit** covers workplace safety law.

### lesson plan
- **IPA:** /ˈlesn plæn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A teacher's outline of what will be taught in one class and how.
  - *Example:* Her **lesson plan** built in ten minutes for questions.

### learning outcome
- **IPA:** /ˈlɜːnɪŋ ˌaʊtkʌm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A statement of what a learner should be able to do after a course.
  - *Example:* Each **learning outcome** is tested by a specific task.

### assessment
- **IPA:** /əˈsesmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of judging a learner's knowledge or ability.
  - *Example:* The course uses continuous **assessment** rather than one big exam.

### examination
- **IPA:** /ɪɡˌzæmɪˈneɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A formal test of knowledge or ability.
  - *Example:* The final **examination** counts for half the grade.

### student assignment
- **IPA:** /ˈstjuːdnt əˌsaɪnmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A piece of work given to students to complete as part of a course.
  - *Example:* The **student assignment** is a 2,000-word report due in three weeks.

### dissertation
- **IPA:** /ˌdɪsəˈteɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A long piece of writing on a subject, done for a degree.
  - *Example:* Her **dissertation** examined remote-work [productivity](11-economics-and-markets.md#productivity).

### thesis
- **IPA:** /ˈθiːsɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A long research document submitted for a higher degree.
  - *Example:* He defended his **thesis** before a panel of three examiners.

### grade
- **IPA:** /ɡreɪd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A mark showing the standard of a piece of work or overall performance.
  - *Example:* She got the top **grade** in the [practical](30-education-and-training.md#practical) [assessment](30-education-and-training.md#assessment).

### semester
- **IPA:** /sɪˈmestə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of the two main periods into which an academic year is divided.
  - *Example:* Exams fall at the end of each **semester**.

### term
- **IPA:** /tɜːm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** One of the periods, usually three, into which a school year is divided.
  - *Example:* The spring **term** runs from January to April.

### faculty
- **IPA:** /ˈfæklti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A group of related departments in a university.
  - *Example:* She teaches in the **faculty** of business.
- **Meaning 2 (n):** The teaching staff of a college or university (mainly US).
  - *Example:* Several **faculty** members joined the review panel.

### lecturer
- **IPA:** /ˈlektʃərə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who teaches at a university, below the rank of professor.
  - *Example:* A visiting **lecturer** gave a talk on data ethics.

### tutor
- **IPA:** /ˈtjuːtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A teacher who works with one student or a small group.
  - *Example:* A private **tutor** helped him prepare for the exam.
- **Meaning 2 (v):** To teach a person or small group.
  - *Example:* She **tutors** two [students](30-education-and-training.md#student) in accounting after work.

### teaching assistant
- **IPA:** /ˈtiːtʃɪŋ əˌsɪstənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who helps a teacher, often a graduate student or classroom aide.
  - *Example:* A **teaching assistant** marks the weekly quizzes.

### professor
- **IPA:** /prəˈfesə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A senior university teacher, usually the head of a subject area.
  - *Example:* The **professor** [supervises](03-management-and-leadership.md#supervise) six [research](27-science-and-research.md#research) [students](30-education-and-training.md#student).

### dean
- **IPA:** /diːn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A senior official in charge of a faculty or division of a university.
  - *Example:* The **dean** [approved](03-management-and-leadership.md#approve) the new degree programme.

### enrolment
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈrəʊlmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The act of officially joining a course or institution; the number of people enrolled.
  - *Example:* **Enrolment** on the evening course doubled this year.

### admission
- **IPA:** /ədˈmɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of being accepted into a school, college, or course.
  - *Example:* **Admission** to the programme requires two years of work [experience](../toeic-600/04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#8-experience).

### prerequisite
- **IPA:** /ˌpriːˈrekwəzɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something required before you can do or have something else.
  - *Example:* Basic [statistics](07-data-reports-and-analysis.md#statistic) is a **prerequisite** for the data module.

### elective
- **IPA:** /ɪˈlektɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A course that a student may choose rather than one that is required.
  - *Example:* He took a [marketing](../toeic-600/08-marketing-and-advertising.md#1-marketing) **elective** alongside his engineering degree.

### core subject
- **IPA:** /ˈkɔː ˌsʌbdʒɪkt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A subject that every student on a course must study.
  - *Example:* Maths and language are **core subjects** in every year.

### remedial
- **IPA:** /rɪˈmiːdiəl/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Intended to help students who are behind reach the required level.
  - *Example:* A short **remedial** class covered the basics again.

### tutorial
- **IPA:** /tjuːˈtɔːriəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A small class or session where students discuss work with a tutor.
  - *Example:* The weekly **tutorial** has just six [students](30-education-and-training.md#student).

### lecture
- **IPA:** /ˈlektʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A talk given to a large group to teach them about a subject.
  - *Example:* The **lecture** was recorded for [students](30-education-and-training.md#student) who could not attend.

### practical
- **IPA:** /ˈpræktɪkl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A class in which students do experiments or hands-on work.
  - *Example:* The [chemistry](04-teamwork-and-workplace-relationships.md#chemistry) **practical** takes place in the lab on Fridays.

### laboratory session
- **IPA:** /ləˈbɒrətri ˌseʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A timetabled period for practical work in a laboratory.
  - *Example:* Each **laboratory session** ends with a short written report.

### field trip
- **IPA:** /ˈfiːld trɪp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A visit made by students to a place away from the classroom to study something.
  - *Example:* A **field trip** to the [recycling](28-environment-and-sustainability.md#recycling) plant [supported](04-teamwork-and-workplace-relationships.md#support) the waste unit.

### distance learning
- **IPA:** /ˈdɪstəns ˌlɜːnɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Study done from home, with materials and support provided remotely.
  - *Example:* **Distance learning** let her finish the [diploma](01-career-and-professional-development.md#diploma) while working [full-time](39-leisure-hobbies-and-sports.md#full-time).

### e-learning
- **IPA:** /ˈiː ˌlɜːnɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Learning carried out through digital courses and online materials.
  - *Example:* The company moved its safety training to **e-learning**.

### blended learning
- **IPA:** /ˌblendɪd ˈlɜːnɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A mix of classroom teaching and online study.
  - *Example:* The **blended learning** course meets once a month, with the rest online.

### self-paced
- **IPA:** /ˌself ˈpeɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Allowing learners to work through material at their own speed.
  - *Example:* The **self-paced** modules suit staff on shifting rotas.

### certification body
- **IPA:** /ˌsɜːtɪfɪˈkeɪʃn ˌbɒdi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An organisation that assesses people or courses and awards recognised qualifications.
  - *Example:* The course is [approved](03-management-and-leadership.md#approve) by a national **certification body**.

### qualification framework
- **IPA:** /ˌkwɒlɪfɪˈkeɪʃn ˌfreɪmwɜːk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A system that ranks qualifications by level so their value can be compared.
  - *Example:* The [diploma](01-career-and-professional-development.md#diploma) sits at level 5 of the **qualification framework**.

### vocational
- **IPA:** /vəʊˈkeɪʃənl/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Relating to training for a specific trade or occupation.
  - *Example:* **Vocational** courses in [plumbing](18-construction-and-building-trades.md#plumbing) and wiring are always full.

### work placement
- **IPA:** /ˈwɜːk ˌpleɪsmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A period spent working in a company as part of a course.
  - *Example:* The degree includes a year-long **work placement** in industry.

### internship programme
- **IPA:** /ˈɪntɜːnʃɪp ˌprəʊɡræm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An organised scheme offering students temporary work experience.
  - *Example:* Half the [graduate](01-career-and-professional-development.md#graduate) hires came through the summer **internship programme**.

### scholarship
- **IPA:** /ˈskɒləʃɪp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money awarded to a student to help pay for study, usually based on merit.
  - *Example:* A **scholarship** covered her full tuition for three years.

### bursary
- **IPA:** /ˈbɜːsəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A grant to help a student with living or study costs, usually based on need.
  - *Example:* The college **bursary** helped with books and travel.

### tuition fee
- **IPA:** /tjuˈɪʃn fiː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount charged for teaching on a course.
  - *Example:* **Tuition fees** rose again for the coming year.

### student loan
- **IPA:** /ˈstjuːdnt ləʊn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money borrowed to pay for study, repaid after graduation.
  - *Example:* He is still repaying his **student loan** ten years on.

### campus
- **IPA:** /ˈkæmpəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The grounds and buildings of a university or college.
  - *Example:* The new **campus** has a [library](30-education-and-training.md#library), labs, and [student](30-education-and-training.md#student) housing.

### lecture hall
- **IPA:** /ˈlektʃə hɔːl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large room with rows of seats where lectures are given.
  - *Example:* The **lecture hall** seats 300 [students](30-education-and-training.md#student).

### library
- **IPA:** /ˈlaɪbrəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A building or room with a collection of books and resources for study.
  - *Example:* The **library** stays open until midnight during exams.

### invigilator
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈvɪdʒɪleɪtə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who watches students during an exam to prevent cheating.
  - *Example:* An **invigilator** collected the papers at exactly 11 o'clock.

### plagiarism
- **IPA:** /ˈpleɪdʒərɪzəm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Presenting someone else's work or ideas as your own.
  - *Example:* The essay was flagged for **plagiarism** by the checking [software](../toeic-600/13-technology-and-computers.md#1-software).

### citation style
- **IPA:** /saɪˈteɪʃn staɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A set of rules for how to write references to sources in a document.
  - *Example:* The [department](31-government-and-public-services.md#department) requires a single **citation style** in all essays.

### referencing
- **IPA:** /ˈrefrənsɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The practice of listing the sources used in a piece of academic work.
  - *Example:* Marks were lost for careless **referencing**.

### continuing education
- **IPA:** /kənˌtɪnjuːɪŋ ˌedjuˈkeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Courses taken by adults after leaving formal education.
  - *Example:* The college's **continuing education** wing runs evening classes.

### professional development
- **IPA:** /prəˌfeʃənl dɪˈveləpmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Training and learning done to improve skills during a career.
  - *Example:* Staff get five days a year for **professional development**.

### in-service training
- **IPA:** /ˌɪn ˈsɜːvɪs ˌtreɪnɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Training given to employees while they continue to do their job.
  - *Example:* [Teachers](30-education-and-training.md#teacher) have an **in-service training** day before term starts.

### induction
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈdʌkʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The process of introducing a new employee or student to an organisation.
  - *Example:* The **induction** covers safety, systems, and who to ask for help.

### onboarding programme
- **IPA:** /ˈɒnbɔːdɪŋ ˌprəʊɡræm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A structured plan for settling new employees into their role over weeks or months.
  - *Example:* The **onboarding programme** pairs each new hire with a buddy.

### refresher course
- **IPA:** /rɪˈfreʃə kɔːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A short course to remind people of skills or update them on changes.
  - *Example:* Drivers take a **refresher course** every three years.

### competency test
- **IPA:** /ˈkɒmpɪtənsi test/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A test that checks whether a person can perform a specific skill to standard.
  - *Example:* New [operators](26-telecommunications-and-networks.md#operator) must pass a **competency test** before working alone.

### learner
- **IPA:** /ˈlɜːnə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who is learning something.
  - *Example:* The course suits **learners** with no prior [experience](../toeic-600/04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#8-experience).

### cohort
- **IPA:** /ˈkəʊhɔːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A group of students who start a course together.
  - *Example:* This year's **cohort** has 60 [students](30-education-and-training.md#student) from twelve countries.

### alumnus
- **IPA:** /əˈlʌmnəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A former student of a school, college, or university.
  - *Example:* A wealthy **alumnus** funded the new science block.

### pedagogy
- **IPA:** /ˈpedəɡɒdʒi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The method and practice of teaching.
  - *Example:* The training focused on **pedagogy** as much as subject knowledge.

### rubric
- **IPA:** /ˈruːbrɪk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A guide setting out how work will be marked, with levels and criteria.
  - *Example:* [Students](30-education-and-training.md#student) are given the **rubric** before they start the project.

### formative assessment
- **IPA:** /ˌfɔːmətɪv əˈsesmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Assessment during learning that gives feedback rather than a final grade.
  - *Example:* Weekly quizzes act as **formative assessment**.

### summative assessment
- **IPA:** /ˌsʌmətɪv əˈsesmənt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Assessment at the end of a course that measures what has been learned.
  - *Example:* The **summative assessment** is a three-hour written exam.

### student
- **IPA:** /ˈstjuːdnt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person studying at a school, college, or university.
  - *Example:* The class has 25 **students**, most of them working adults.

### teacher
- **IPA:** /ˈtiːtʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person whose job is to help others learn.
  - *Example:* An [experienced](../toeic-600/04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#8-experience) **teacher** ran the training for new managers.

### classroom
- **IPA:** /ˈklɑːsruːm/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A room where a class is taught.
  - *Example:* The **classroom** was set up with tables in small groups.

### textbook
- **IPA:** /ˈtekstbʊk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A book used for studying a particular subject.
  - *Example:* A new [edition](32-media-news-and-publishing.md#edition) of the **textbook** comes out every two years.

### homework
- **IPA:** /ˈhəʊmwɜːk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Work that students are given to do outside class.
  - *Example:* The **homework** was to [interview](../toeic-600/04-human-resources-and-recruitment.md#6-interview) someone about their job.

### degree course
- **IPA:** /dɪˈɡriː kɔːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A programme of study at a university leading to a degree.
  - *Example:* The three-year **degree course** ends with a [research](27-science-and-research.md#research) project.

### study skills
- **IPA:** /ˈstʌdi skɪlz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Techniques for learning effectively, such as note-taking and time management.
  - *Example:* A short **study skills** [session](25-information-technology.md#session) helped the new [students](30-education-and-training.md#student) settle in.

### revision
- **IPA:** /rɪˈvɪʒn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The reviewing of work already covered, especially before an exam.
  - *Example:* She drew up a **revision** [timetable](40-travel-commuting-and-getting-around.md#timetable) for the final month.

### teaching material
- **IPA:** /ˈtiːtʃɪŋ məˌtɪəriəl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The resources a teacher uses, such as slides, handouts, and exercises.
  - *Example:* All the **teaching material** is shared online after each [session](25-information-technology.md#session).

### learning objective
- **IPA:** /ˈlɜːnɪŋ əbˌdʒektɪv/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A clear statement of what learners are meant to know or do by the end of a lesson.
  - *Example:* Each [session](25-information-technology.md#session) opens by putting the **learning objective** on the board.
