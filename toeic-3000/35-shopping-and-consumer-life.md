# 35 — Shopping & Consumer Life

> Cross-links jump to another entry in the 3000-word set. **Ctrl/Cmd + click** to follow.
> Words from `../toeic-600/` are left as plain text.

### shopper
- **IPA:** /ˈʃɒpə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who is buying goods in a shop.
  - *Example:* Weekend **shoppers** filled the mall by mid-morning.

### purchaser
- **IPA:** /ˈpɜːtʃəsə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person or organisation that buys something.
  - *Example:* The **purchaser** must keep the [receipt](../toeic-600/03-accounting-and-budgeting.md#5-receipt) for any claim.

### browse
- **IPA:** /braʊz/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To look at goods in a relaxed way, without planning to buy anything in particular.
  - *Example:* We **browsed** the bookshop for an hour.

### window-shopping
- **IPA:** /ˈwɪndəʊ ˌʃɒpɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Looking at goods in shop windows without buying.
  - *Example:* They spent the evening **window-shopping** on the main street.

### trolley
- **IPA:** /ˈtrɒli/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A large metal basket on wheels used to carry goods around a shop.
  - *Example:* Her **trolley** was full before she reached the tills.

### basket
- **IPA:** /ˈbɑːskɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A container carried by hand for a few items in a shop, or the list of items in an online order.
  - *Example:* He left two items in the online **basket** and never bought them.

### checkout queue
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃekaʊt kjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The line of customers waiting to pay.
  - *Example:* A long **checkout queue** on Saturday put some [shoppers](35-shopping-and-consumer-life.md#shopper) off.

### till receipt
- **IPA:** /ˈtɪl rɪˌsiːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The printed slip from a shop showing what was bought and the price.
  - *Example:* Keep the **till receipt** in case you need to return anything.

### proof of purchase
- **IPA:** /ˌpruːf əv ˈpɜːtʃəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Something, usually a receipt, that shows you bought an item.
  - *Example:* Refunds require **proof of purchase**.

### price tag
- **IPA:** /ˈpraɪs tæɡ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A label showing the price of an item.
  - *Example:* The **price tag** was still on the jacket.

### unit price
- **IPA:** /ˈjuːnɪt praɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The price per standard measure, such as per litre or per 100 grams.
  - *Example:* Comparing the **unit price** showed the big pack was not cheaper.

### bulk buy
- **IPA:** /ˈbʌlk baɪ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A purchase of a large quantity, usually to get a lower price.
  - *Example:* A **bulk buy** of paper saved the office 20%.

### multipack
- **IPA:** /ˈmʌltipæk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A package containing several of the same item, sold together.
  - *Example:* The **multipack** of six works out cheaper per can.

### name brand
- **IPA:** /ˈneɪm brænd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A product made by a well-known manufacturer, usually more expensive than a shop's own.
  - *Example:* She buys the **name brand** for coffee but not for cleaning products.

### generic
- **IPA:** /dʒəˈnerɪk/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Sold without a brand name, usually cheaper.
  - *Example:* The **generic** version has the same [ingredients](38-food-cooking-and-dining-out.md#ingredient) as the branded one.

### knock-off
- **IPA:** /ˈnɒk ɒf/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A cheap copy of a well-known product.
  - *Example:* The market was full of **knock-off** watches.

### counterfeit
- **IPA:** /ˈkaʊntəfɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Made to look exactly like something real in order to deceive.
  - *Example:* [Customs](../toeic-600/11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#13-customs) seized a [shipment](../toeic-600/11-logistics-shipping-and-warehousing.md#1-shipment) of **counterfeit** goods.

### discount voucher
- **IPA:** /ˈdɪskaʊnt ˌvaʊtʃə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A printed or digital token giving money off a purchase.
  - *Example:* A **discount voucher** knocked £5 off the bill.

### cashback
- **IPA:** /ˈkæʃbæk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money returned to a buyer after a purchase, as a reward or incentive.
  - *Example:* The card offers 1% **cashback** on all [spending](35-shopping-and-consumer-life.md#spending).

### loyalty points
- **IPA:** /ˈlɔɪəlti pɔɪnts/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Rewards earned by shopping regularly with the same retailer.
  - *Example:* She had enough **loyalty points** for a free coffee.

### store credit
- **IPA:** /ˈstɔː ˌkredɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Money held by a shop for a customer to spend there, often given instead of a cash refund.
  - *Example:* Without a [receipt](../toeic-600/03-accounting-and-budgeting.md#5-receipt), he was offered **store credit** rather than his money back.

### gift receipt
- **IPA:** /ˈɡɪft rɪˌsiːt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A receipt that shows the item but not the price, given so a gift can be returned.
  - *Example:* Ask for a **gift receipt** so they can [exchange](../toeic-600/09-sales-and-purchasing.md#23-exchange) it if it doesn't fit.

### return policy
- **IPA:** /rɪˈtɜːn ˌpɒləsi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A shop's rules on taking goods back and giving refunds or exchanges.
  - *Example:* Their **return policy** allows 60 days with a [receipt](../toeic-600/03-accounting-and-budgeting.md#5-receipt).

### extended warranty
- **IPA:** /ɪkˌstendɪd ˈwɒrənti/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Extra cover, bought separately, that repairs a product after the standard guarantee ends.
  - *Example:* The **extended warranty** cost almost as much as a repair would.

### faulty goods
- **IPA:** /ˌfɔːlti ˈɡʊdz/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Products that do not work properly or are damaged.
  - *Example:* You have a right to a refund on **faulty goods**.

### recall notice
- **IPA:** /rɪˈkɔːl ˌnəʊtɪs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An official announcement asking customers to return a product because of a safety problem.
  - *Example:* A **recall notice** was posted for one model of the heater.

### best-before date
- **IPA:** /ˈbest bɪfɔː deɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The date up to which a food is at its best quality, though still safe after.
  - *Example:* The biscuits are past their **best-before date** but fine to eat.

### use-by date
- **IPA:** /ˈjuːz baɪ deɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The date after which a food should not be eaten for safety reasons.
  - *Example:* Throw out the chicken once the **use-by date** has passed.

### expiry
- **IPA:** /ɪkˈspaɪəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The end of the period for which something is valid or usable.
  - *Example:* Check the **expiry** on the [voucher](../toeic-600/14-travel-and-transportation.md#19-voucher) before you go.

### shelf edge
- **IPA:** /ˈʃelf edʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The front strip of a shop shelf where the price and product name are displayed.
  - *Example:* The **shelf edge** label still showed the old, higher price.

### promotional offer
- **IPA:** /prəˌməʊʃənl ˈɒfə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A special deal for a limited time to encourage buying.
  - *Example:* A **promotional offer** gave a free case with every phone.

### buy-one-get-one-free
- **IPA:** /ˌbaɪ wʌn ɡet wʌn ˈfriː/
- **Meaning 1 (adj):** Describing a deal where you get a second item at no cost.
  - *Example:* The **buy-one-get-one-free** deal doubled sales of the shampoo.

### clearance rack
- **IPA:** /ˈklɪərəns ræk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A rail or shelf of goods being sold off cheaply to clear space.
  - *Example:* She found the coat on the **clearance rack** at half price.

### flash sale
- **IPA:** /ˈflæʃ seɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A sale with big discounts lasting only a few hours.
  - *Example:* A midnight **flash sale** sold out the last of the stock.

### instalment plan
- **IPA:** /ɪnˈstɔːlmənt plæn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An arrangement to pay for something in regular parts over time.
  - *Example:* The sofa was bought on a 12-month **instalment plan**.

### hire purchase
- **IPA:** /ˌhaɪə ˈpɜːtʃəs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A way of buying goods by paying in instalments, with ownership passing only after the last payment.
  - *Example:* The car was on **hire purchase**, so the finance company still owned it.

### delivery slot
- **IPA:** /dɪˈlɪvəri slɒt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A booked period during which an order will be delivered.
  - *Example:* The only free **delivery slot** was Thursday morning.

### curbside pickup
- **IPA:** /ˈkɜːbsaɪd ˌpɪkʌp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A service where staff bring an online order out to the customer's car.
  - *Example:* **Curbside pickup** meant she never had to leave the vehicle.

### order confirmation
- **IPA:** /ˈɔːdə ˌkɒnfəˈmeɪʃn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A message confirming that an order has been received and accepted.
  - *Example:* The **order confirmation** listed everything except the free gift.

### dispatch note
- **IPA:** /dɪˈspætʃ nəʊt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A document sent with or before goods, listing what has been sent.
  - *Example:* The **dispatch note** showed one item was on [back order](35-shopping-and-consumer-life.md#back-order).

### tracking link
- **IPA:** /ˈtrækɪŋ lɪŋk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A web address that shows the current location and status of a parcel.
  - *Example:* The **tracking link** said the parcel was "out for delivery".

### doorstep delivery
- **IPA:** /ˈdɔːstep dɪˌlɪvəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Delivery of goods right to the customer's front door.
  - *Example:* Free **doorstep delivery** is included on orders over £30.

### signature on delivery
- **IPA:** /ˈsɪɡnətʃər ɒn dɪˌlɪvəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A requirement for someone to sign for a parcel when it arrives.
  - *Example:* High-value items need a **signature on delivery**.

### chargeback
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃɑːdʒbæk/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A refund forced by the card company when a customer disputes a charge.
  - *Example:* She filed a **chargeback** when the seller stopped replying.

### buyer's remorse
- **IPA:** /ˌbaɪəz rɪˈmɔːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A feeling of regret after making a purchase, especially an expensive one.
  - *Example:* **Buyer's remorse** set in the moment the box arrived.

### comparison shopping
- **IPA:** /kəmˈpærɪsn ˌʃɒpɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Checking prices and features across shops before buying.
  - *Example:* A few minutes of **comparison shopping** saved him a third of the price.

### product review
- **IPA:** /ˈprɒdʌkt rɪˌvjuː/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written opinion of a product by someone who has used it.
  - *Example:* A run of bad **product reviews** hurt sales badly.

### star rating
- **IPA:** /ˈstɑː ˌreɪtɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A score out of five stars given by customers to a product or service.
  - *Example:* The [blender](38-food-cooking-and-dining-out.md#blender) has a 4.6 **star rating** from 2,000 reviews.

### wish list
- **IPA:** /ˈwɪʃ lɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A saved list of items a shopper would like to buy later.
  - *Example:* He moved the coat from his **wish list** to his basket in the sale.

### out of stock
- **IPA:** /ˌaʊt əv ˈstɒk/
- **Meaning 1 (phr):** Not currently available to buy because supplies have run out.
  - *Example:* The size she wanted was **out of stock** everywhere.

### back order
- **IPA:** /ˈbæk ˌɔːdə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** An order for goods that are not in stock and will be sent when they arrive.
  - *Example:* The part is on **back order** with a two-week wait.

### rain check
- **IPA:** /ˈreɪn tʃek/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A promise that a customer can buy a sold-out sale item later at the sale price.
  - *Example:* The store gave her a **rain check** for the [discounted](../toeic-600/08-marketing-and-advertising.md#16-discount) printer.

### subscription box
- **IPA:** /səbˈskrɪpʃn bɒks/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A package of products delivered regularly for a recurring fee.
  - *Example:* A monthly coffee **subscription box** arrives on the first Friday.

### shopping list
- **IPA:** /ˈʃɒpɪŋ lɪst/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A written list of things to buy.
  - *Example:* Sticking to the **shopping list** kept the bill down.

### sale
- **IPA:** /seɪl/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A period when a shop sells goods at reduced prices.
  - *Example:* The winter **sale** starts the day after the holidays.

### online shopping
- **IPA:** /ˌɒnlaɪn ˈʃɒpɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Buying goods over the internet.
  - *Example:* **Online shopping** now accounts for a third of the retailer's sales.

### home delivery
- **IPA:** /ˌhəʊm dɪˈlɪvəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The bringing of goods to a customer's home.
  - *Example:* The supermarket offers **home delivery** seven days a week.

### delivery charge
- **IPA:** /dɪˈlɪvəri tʃɑːdʒ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A fee added for bringing goods to the customer.
  - *Example:* The **delivery charge** is waived on larger orders.

### purchase history
- **IPA:** /ˈpɜːtʃəs ˌhɪstri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A record of everything a customer has bought from a shop or website.
  - *Example:* The app suggests items based on your **purchase history**.

### retail therapy
- **IPA:** /ˌriːteɪl ˈθerəpi/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Shopping done to make yourself feel better.
  - *Example:* A bad week ended with some **retail therapy** at the outlet mall.

### consumer rights
- **IPA:** /kənˌsjuːmə ˈraɪts/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The legal protections a buyer has, such as the right to a refund on faulty goods.
  - *Example:* A [leaflet](08-business-communication.md#leaflet) on **consumer rights** is [displayed](16-retail-and-store-operations.md#display) near the [returns desk](16-retail-and-store-operations.md#returns-desk).

### cooling-off period
- **IPA:** /ˈkuːlɪŋ ɒf ˌpɪəriəd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A short time after buying, especially online, when a customer can cancel without a reason.
  - *Example:* The 14-day **cooling-off period** let her return the unopened kit.

### price comparison
- **IPA:** /ˈpraɪs kəmˌpærɪsn/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The checking of what different sellers charge for the same item.
  - *Example:* A **price comparison** site found the cheapest [supplier](../toeic-600/09-sales-and-purchasing.md#2-supplier) in seconds.

### in stock
- **IPA:** /ˌɪn ˈstɒk/
- **Meaning 1 (phr):** Available to buy immediately.
  - *Example:* Call ahead to check the model is **in stock**.

### pre-order
- **IPA:** /ˌpriː ˈɔːdə/
- **Meaning 1 (v):** To order an item before it is released or available.
  - *Example:* Fans could **pre-order** the console three months early.

### gift wrap
- **IPA:** /ˈɡɪft ræp/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Decorative paper used to wrap a present; the service of wrapping it.
  - *Example:* The shop offers free **gift wrap** in December.

### loyalty card
- **IPA:** /ˈlɔɪəlti kɑːd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A card that records a customer's spending so they can earn rewards.
  - *Example:* Scan your **loyalty card** before the [cashier](16-retail-and-store-operations.md#cashier) rings up the sale.

### e-commerce
- **IPA:** /ˈiː ˌkɒmɜːs/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The buying and selling of goods and services over the internet.
  - *Example:* The company built an **e-commerce** site to reach customers abroad.

### checkout process
- **IPA:** /ˈtʃekaʊt ˌprəʊses/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The steps a customer goes through to pay for an online order.
  - *Example:* A shorter **checkout process** cut the number of abandoned baskets.

### spending
- **IPA:** /ˈspendɪŋ/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** The amount of money used to buy things.
  - *Example:* Household **spending** on clothes fell during the [downturn](11-economics-and-markets.md#downturn).

### shopping habit
- **IPA:** /ˈʃɒpɪŋ ˌhæbɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A regular pattern in how and what a person buys.
  - *Example:* The pandemic changed many people's **shopping habits** for good.

### bargain hunter
- **IPA:** /ˈbɑːɡɪn ˌhʌntə/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A person who looks hard for goods at low prices.
  - *Example:* **Bargain hunters** queued outside before the sale opened.

### free delivery
- **IPA:** /ˌfriː dɪˈlɪvəri/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** Delivery of goods at no extra charge to the customer.
  - *Example:* **Free delivery** on orders over £40 pushed up the [average](07-data-reports-and-analysis.md#average) basket.

### add to basket
- **IPA:** /ˌæd tə ˈbɑːskɪt/
- **Meaning 1 (phr):** To place an item in an online shopping basket ready to buy.
  - *Example:* [Shoppers](35-shopping-and-consumer-life.md#shopper) who **add to basket** but don't buy get a [reminder](08-business-communication.md#reminder) email.

### discount code
- **IPA:** /ˈdɪskaʊnt kəʊd/
- **Meaning 1 (n):** A word or set of characters entered online to get a price reduction.
  - *Example:* A **discount code** from the [newsletter](08-business-communication.md#newsletter) gave 15% off the first order.
